## 4. Military Spouse Network Strategy

Killeen OTs working with military families have unique opportunities:

- **EFMP (Exceptional Family Member Program)** at Fort Hood routes pediatric OT cases to nearby private clinics — these contracts are stable and PSLF-adjacent through hospital partnerships.
- **Texas State Loan Repayment Program** offers up to $160,000 to OTs working in HPSA-designated areas (parts of Bell County qualify).
- Tap **Fort Hood Family Readiness Groups (FRGs)** for word-of-mouth referrals into school district contract roles.
- Use **MyCAA** for OT continuing education hours required for Texas license renewal every 2 years.
- VA Central Texas (Temple) and CRDAMC both prefer hiring OTs with military-spouse background due to context fluency.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: Do school-based OTs at KISD qualify for PSLF?**
A: Yes — KISD is a government employer, so direct W-2 employment qualifies. Contract OTs via third-party agencies typically do NOT.

**Q: Is there an OT signing bonus market in Killeen?**
A: Hospital-based OT roles at CRDAMC and Baylor Scott & White often offer $5-15k signing bonuses, especially for specialty certifications (CHT, NDT).

**Q: Should I rent or buy in Killeen as an OT?**
A: Buy if you'll stay 5+ years — median home is $230k, mortgages often beat rent. Rent if your spouse is active-duty with PCS uncertainty.

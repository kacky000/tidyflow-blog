## 5 Financial Tips for Greenville Dental Hygienists

1. **Live in Simpsonville or Mauldin.** These suburbs offer 1BR apartments for $900–$1,050 with easy access to major dental office corridors on Woodruff Road and Haywood Road. You save $150–$250/month on rent vs. downtown.

2. **Maxie out your SC retirement contributions.** South Carolina doesn't tax Social Security benefits and has favorable treatment for retirement income. If your employer offers a 403(b) or 401(k), prioritize contributions early.

3. **Take advantage of Bon Secours and Prisma Health system opportunities.** Both are large nonprofit health systems in Greenville. If you work in their dental departments or affiliated FQHCs, you may qualify for [PSLF](https://gettidyflow.com/blog/pslf-for-nurses-complete-guide).

4. **Negotiate per-diem shifts during the growth surge.** Greenville has a shortage of experienced dental hygienists. Per-diem work at $35–$45/hour on top of your regular position can add $500–$1,000/month.

5. **Track variable expenses.** Greenville's dining and entertainment scene has exploded. Use a [monthly budget template](https://gettidyflow.com/blog/monthly-budget-checklist) to keep lifestyle inflation in check as the city grows.

## Student Loan Strategy

## Banking Fees: Genuinely Close to Zero

SoFi's biggest strength is its **checking and savings account**, and here the "no fees" claim is largely accurate:

- **No monthly maintenance fee**
- **No minimum balance requirement**
- **No overdraft fee** when you opt into overdraft coverage (up to a set limit, with qualifying direct deposit)
- **Surcharge-free ATM network** for in-network withdrawals

To unlock the **highest savings APY** and perks like early direct deposit, SoFi generally asks you to set up **qualifying direct deposit** (or meet a monthly deposit threshold). You are not charged a fee if you don't — you simply earn the lower APY tier. That is a fair trade, but it is the one "string attached" worth knowing.

The main cost most people might still hit: **out-of-network ATM fees** (charged by the machine's owner, not SoFi) and **outbound wire transfers**, which can carry a fee. For everyday debit and direct-deposit banking, though, you will rarely pay SoFi anything. ## Investing Fees: $0 Commissions, Watch the Edges

**SoFi Invest** offers **$0-commission trading** on stocks and ETFs through its self-directed brokerage, with **fractional shares** so you can start with a few dollars. For a casual investor, the headline cost of buying and selling is zero — in line with the rest of the industry.

Where to look closer:

- **SoFi's own ETFs** carry an **expense ratio** (the fund's annual fee), like any fund. These are separate from trading commissions and are charged inside the fund.
- **Crypto** pricing works through a **spread** rather than a flat commission, and SoFi's crypto availability has shifted over time — confirm what is currently offered and at what cost before relying on it.
- **Automated investing** (robo-advisor) tiers may have their own terms; check the current management fee, if any.

For a true low-cost, long-term portfolio, you would still compare SoFi's funds against the giants — see [Fidelity vs Schwab](/blog/fidelity-vs-schwab/) and our [best investing app for beginners 2026](/blog/best-investing-app-for-beginners-2026/) guide — but SoFi's $0 commissions make it a reasonable all-in-one for people who want banking and investing under one login.

## Loan Fees: No Origination, But Mind the Interest

SoFi built its name on **lending**, and its fee structure there is borrower-friendly on the *fee* side:

- **No origination fees** on personal loans and student loan refinancing
- **No prepayment penalties** — you can pay off early without a charge
- **No application fees**

The real "cost" of any loan, of course, is the **interest rate (APR)**, which depends on your credit, loan term, and amount — not a fee, but the number that actually determines what you pay. SoFi's no-fee lending is a genuine plus, but always compare the **total APR** across lenders rather than focusing only on the absence of fees. A loan with no origination fee but a higher rate can still cost more overall. ## The Hidden or Easy-to-Miss Costs

Even with a "no-fee" provider, a few charges can surprise you:

1. **Out-of-network ATM operator fees** — SoFi doesn't charge, but the ATM owner might.
2. **Outbound wire transfers** — domestic and international wires can carry a fee.
3. **Lower APY tier** — not a fee, but skipping direct deposit means a smaller return on savings.
4. **Fund expense ratios** — baked into SoFi ETFs and any automated-investing portfolios.
5. **Crypto spreads** — the cost is in the buy/sell spread, not a visible commission.

None of these are unusual or predatory — they are standard across the industry — but knowing them keeps "no fees" from becoming "no surprises... until one shows up."

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does SoFi really have no fees?

For checking and savings, mostly yes — no monthly fee, no minimum balance, and no overdraft fee with coverage. You can still hit out-of-network ATM fees (charged by the machine owner) and wire fees, and you need direct deposit to earn the highest savings APY.

### Are there fees to invest with SoFi?

SoFi Invest charges $0 commissions on self-directed stock and ETF trades. The costs to watch are fund expense ratios on SoFi's own ETFs, any robo-advisor management terms, and crypto spreads rather than flat commissions.

### Does SoFi charge loan origination fees?

No. SoFi personal loans and student loan refinancing have no origination fees, application fees, or prepayment penalties. The real cost is the interest rate (APR), so compare total APR across lenders before borrowing.

### What's the catch with SoFi's high savings APY?

It is tiered. To earn the top APY, you generally need a qualifying direct deposit or to meet a monthly deposit threshold. Without it, you still pay no fee — you just earn a lower rate.

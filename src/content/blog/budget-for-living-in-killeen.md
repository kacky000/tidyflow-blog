# How to Budget for Living in Killeen, TX

**Budgeting for living in Killeen, TX** is inseparable from understanding Fort Cavazos (renamed from Fort Hood in 2023) — the largest US Army installation in the world by active-duty population, with approximately 45,000 soldiers. Killeen's economy, housing market, and financial dynamics are built around the military. Whether you're active duty receiving BAH, a military spouse navigating household budgeting on one income, or a civilian contractor supporting Army operations, your Killeen budget has structural differences from most American cities.

Texas has no state income tax, and Killeen's rents are among the lowest in Texas — a combination that creates genuine affordability for military and civilian households alike.

## BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) in Killeen

For active-duty soldiers, BAH is the critical number. Fort Cavazos BAH rates for 2026:

| Grade | Without Dependents | With Dependents |
|-------|-------------------|-----------------|
| E-4 | $1,080 | $1,320 |
| E-5 | $1,110 | $1,380 |
| E-6 | $1,170 | $1,440 |
| O-1 | $1,260 | $1,530 |
| O-3 | $1,440 | $1,740 |

BAH is tax-free income. A soldier at E-5 with dependents receiving $1,380/month BAH can cover a 2BR apartment at $1,000–$1,100 and pocket the difference — or upgrade housing. BAH is designed to cover 100% of median rental costs in the local area at the appropriate grade level.

## Budget Breakdown by Income Level

### Active Duty E-4/E-5 (With BAH and BAS)
Military compensation includes base pay, BAH (housing), and BAS (Basic Allowance for Subsistence, approximately $460/month for enlisted). An E-5 with 6 years of service earns approximately $2,800/month base pay plus $1,380 BAH plus $460 BAS — roughly $4,640/month total, all before accounting for tax advantages on the allowances. This is a stronger financial position than the headline base pay number suggests.

### $3,200/month (Civilian GS worker or contractor)
Fort Cavazos employs substantial civilian personnel in GS-7 through GS-11 roles. At $3,200/month take-home, a 1BR at $800, utilities at $140, groceries at $300, and transportation at $200 leaves approximately $900 for savings and discretionary spending.

### $4,500/month (Contractor, senior civilian, or military officer)
Defense contractors supporting Fort Cavazos operations, and military officers at O-3 to O-5 grades (adding BAH), operate comfortably in Killeen's cost structure. Savings rates of $1,000–$1,500/month are achievable with discipline.

## Killeen's Unique Cost Factors

### No Texas State Income Tax
Military BAH and BAS are already tax-free at the federal level. Adding Texas's zero state income tax means Killeen military families face a significantly lower tax burden than equivalent-income households in states with income tax.

### TRICARE Healthcare — Near Zero Cost for Military
Active-duty soldiers and their dependents receive healthcare through TRICARE at no cost for active-duty members. This eliminates $150–$400/month in health insurance premiums that civilian budgets must include. For military families, this is one of the most significant financial benefits of service.

### Vehicle Costs — Texas Car Registration
Texas requires annual vehicle inspection and registration. Registration costs $50–$100/year. Car insurance in Killeen averages $110–$170/month — lower than Austin or Houston but higher than rural areas.

### Heat and Electricity
Central Texas summers are hot and dry — 95–105°F from June through August. Electricity bills run $120–$200/month during summer. Oncor provides electricity in the area; Texas's deregulated electricity market allows rate shopping, which can meaningfully reduce costs.

### Military Housing vs. Off-Post
Fort Cavazos has on-post housing managed by Lendlease. On-post housing converts BAH directly to rent — you pay nothing additional but give up the ability to pocket any BAH surplus. Off-post living allows you to keep any BAH above actual rent costs, which is a real financial advantage in Killeen's affordable market. Many financially savvy soldiers live off-post specifically to save the BAH surplus.

### Frequent PCS — Plan Your Housing Duration
If you're active duty, a Permanent Change of Station (PCS) move is likely every 2–3 years. Buying a home in Killeen requires careful evaluation — the resale market is adequate but not exceptional, and buying and selling within 2 years often results in a loss after closing costs. For PCS soldiers with 2-3 year assignments, renting is usually the safer financial choice.

## What You Save in Killeen

Median home prices in Killeen run $180,000–$230,000. Texas property taxes are higher than most states — approximately 1.8–2.3% of assessed value. On a $200,000 home, expect $3,600–$4,600/year in property taxes. VA loans eliminate the down payment requirement for eligible veterans and active-duty soldiers, making homeownership accessible without years of saving.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is it better to live on-post or off-post in Killeen?**
Financially, off-post often wins for soldiers who can pocket BAH surplus. Killeen rents are well below BAH rates for most grades, creating a meaningful savings opportunity. On-post housing offers convenience and community but eliminates the financial surplus. Evaluate based on your grade, dependent status, and personal priorities.

**How do I budget with deployment coming up?**
A pre-deployment budget should redirect housing costs (your spouse or family stays in the unit) and increase savings rate during deployment — you won't be spending on restaurants, entertainment, or transportation at the same rate. Consider opening a Savings Deposit Program (SDP) account — 10% annual return guaranteed on deployments to combat zones.

**Is Killeen a good city for veterans?**
Yes — access to VA services, military-community infrastructure (commissary, VFW, American Legion), and affordable housing make Killeen practical for veterans. However, civilian job opportunities in Killeen proper are limited outside defense contracting. Veterans pursuing non-military careers often commute to Austin (1 hour) or Waco (1 hour).

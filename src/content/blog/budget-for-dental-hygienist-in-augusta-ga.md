Augusta is one of the most underrated cities for dental hygienists in the Southeast. The military presence at Fort Eisenhower (formerly Fort Gordon), the Medical College of Georgia (MCG) teaching infrastructure, and Wellstar's recent acquisitions reshape what PSLF eligibility looks like here. This guide breaks down salary, cost of living, and a PSLF map that's frequently misread.

## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Distinction: Wellstar MCG Health vs Doctors Hospital

**Augusta's PSLF map is highly specific.**

- **Wellstar MCG Health** (formerly AU Health / Augusta University Health) — In 2023, Augusta University Health System was acquired by Wellstar. **Wellstar Health System IS a 501(c)(3) nonprofit** → PSLF-eligible. But your W-2 must be from Wellstar MCG Health, not a contracted dental group leasing space.
- **Charlie Norwood VA Medical Center** — Federal government → fully PSLF-eligible.
- **Eisenhower Army Medical Center** (Fort Eisenhower) — Federal government → fully PSLF-eligible. Some dental hygienist roles are GS civilian or contractor — contractor positions do NOT qualify.
- **Doctors Hospital of Augusta** — Owned by HCA Healthcare → **for-profit, NOT PSLF-eligible.**
- **University Hospital (Piedmont Augusta as of 2024)** — Piedmont Healthcare is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible.

Three hospital systems within a 10-minute drive of each other. Two qualify, one doesn't.

## 5 Money Tips for Dental Hygienists in Augusta

1. **Recertify PSLF annually — and confirm employer name on Form PSLF.** The Wellstar acquisition of MCG Health is recent enough that some HR systems still show "Augusta University Medical Associates" on W-2s. Verify the legal employer name before submitting Form PSLF.

2. **Avoid the "DSO trap" near Fort Eisenhower.** Heartland Dental and Pacific Dental Services have expanded into the Augusta market. They pay competitive hourly rates but are for-profit. A hygienist switching from Wellstar to Heartland for a $3/hour raise can lose tens of thousands in eventual PSLF discharge.

3. **Masters Week rental income is taxable.** Many Augusta residents rent their homes during the Masters Tournament for $5,000–$30,000 for one week. This is **not** tax-free above 14 nights — and most rentals are exactly 7 nights, which IS tax-free under the IRS Augusta Rule. Document everything.

4. **Take advantage of the South Carolina border.** North Augusta SC has slightly lower property taxes and no GA state income tax issue. But your W-2 is still GA-sourced if you work in Augusta — file GA non-resident return.

5. **Watch the Piedmont vs Wellstar competition.** University Hospital (now Piedmont Augusta) and Wellstar MCG are competing for staff. Both qualify for PSLF, but the benefits packages differ significantly. Don't switch for a small raise without checking 401(k) match and HSA contribution.

## Student Loan Considerations

Most Augusta hygienists graduated from Augusta Technical College (in-state tuition ~$3,000/year) — debt loads are lower than national average, typically **$15,000–$35,000**. On an IDR plan at $62,000 income:
- SAVE plan payment: ~$210–$280/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$200–$420/month

PSLF eligibility map for Augusta:
- **Wellstar MCG Health, Piedmont Augusta**: 501(c)(3) — PSLF eligible.
- **Charlie Norwood VA, Eisenhower Army Medical**: Federal — PSLF eligible.
- **Doctors Hospital of Augusta (HCA)**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.
- **Private dental practices, DSOs (Heartland, Aspen, PDS)**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.
- **Richmond County Health Department / FQHCs**: PSLF eligible.

## FAQ

**Is Augusta a good city for new dental hygienists?**
Yes — low cost of living, three large healthcare employers, and military spouse-friendly culture. Better than Macon for career mobility, cheaper than Atlanta.

**Why does Wellstar MCG qualify but Doctors Hospital doesn't?**
Wellstar Health System is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Doctors Hospital of Augusta is owned by HCA Healthcare, which is publicly traded and for-profit. Tax status determines PSLF eligibility, not the word "hospital."

**Should I work at Eisenhower Army Medical Center as a civilian?**
GS-level federal civilian positions qualify for PSLF. Contractor positions (working at the hospital but employed by a private staffing firm) do NOT. Always verify your W-2 employer.

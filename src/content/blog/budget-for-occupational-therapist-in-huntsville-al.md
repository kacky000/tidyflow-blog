## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Information: Huntsville Hospital vs Crestwood vs Defense Contractors

**This is the most important paragraph in this guide.**

OT-relevant employers in Huntsville and their PSLF status:

- **Huntsville Hospital Health System (HH)**: 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible. Includes Huntsville Hospital, Madison Hospital, HH Heart Center, and the inpatient rehab unit.
- **Crestwood Medical Center**: HCA Healthcare (publicly traded for-profit) → **does NOT qualify for PSLF**.
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Huntsville**: Encompass Health is publicly traded for-profit (NYSE: EHC) → does NOT qualify.
- **Madison City Schools / Huntsville City Schools / Madison County Schools**: Public school systems → PSLF-eligible (school-based OTs).
- **United Cerebral Palsy of Huntsville/Tennessee Valley**: 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible.
- **Defense contractor on-site clinics (Lockheed, Boeing, Raytheon, SAIC, Leidos, Northrop Grumman)**: For-profit private sector → does NOT qualify.
- **Redstone Arsenal Federal Civilian Clinic / VA Huntsville**: Direct federal employment → PSLF-eligible.

**The Encompass trap is severe for rehab-track OTs.** Encompass Health's rehabilitation hospital in Huntsville is a flagship inpatient rehab site — heavily recruited for OTs — but it's for-profit. If you're PSLF-pursuing, choose Huntsville Hospital's inpatient rehab unit instead.

**Aerospace contractor trap (similar to Fayetteville NC pattern)**: Defense contractors recruit therapists for on-site occupational health clinics. The work is federal-adjacent, but your W-2 is corporate for-profit. Federal employees as patients don't change your employer's tax status.

## 5 Money Tips for Occupational Therapists in Huntsville

1. **Choose Huntsville Hospital inpatient rehab over Encompass.** Both serve similar caseloads. One qualifies for PSLF; the other doesn't. Difference over 10 years can be $80,000+ in forgiven debt.

2. **Madison Hospital can pair with Madison City Schools.** PRN at Madison Hospital + school-based OT in Madison City Schools = both PSLF-qualifying. Stack hours efficiently.

3. **Aerospace contractor clinics pay well — but ruin PSLF.** Lockheed and Boeing pay ~10–15% above hospital scale for on-site occupational health work. If you have $130k of OT student debt, the PSLF math usually favors the lower-paying nonprofit job.

4. **Tornado/severe weather fund.** North Alabama is in the eastern Tornado Alley. Severe weather can cause damage and brief job disruption. $1,500+ separate weather fund recommended. Renter's insurance is essential.

5. **Don't over-commit to a high-rent area.** Twickenham is beautiful but $1,500+ for a 1BR. Madison offers cheaper rent and shorter commutes for OTs working at Madison Hospital or Madison City Schools.

## FAQ

**Why does the aerospace economy matter for OT salary?**
Defense/aerospace pays high salaries and pulls up wage benchmarks across the region. Hospitals compete for talent against tech employers, so OT pay has risen ~8–10% since 2020 in Huntsville.

**Does the VA Huntsville outpatient clinic qualify for PSLF?**
Yes — direct federal employment qualifies. The VA Tennessee Valley Healthcare System operates a Community Based Outpatient Clinic in Huntsville. OT positions there are PSLF-eligible.

**Is it worth taking a lower-paying Huntsville Hospital job over a higher-paying contractor clinic?**
If you have $100k+ in OT student loans and want PSLF, almost always yes. The forgiveness math typically beats a 10–15% salary premium over 10 years.

## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Distinction in Augusta

OTs in Augusta have **five major employer categories** with different PSLF tax statuses:

- **Wellstar MCG Health** (former AU Health, acquired by Wellstar in 2023) — 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible.
- **Charlie Norwood VA Medical Center** — Federal government → PSLF-eligible. Excellent PT/OT residencies.
- **Eisenhower Army Medical Center** (Fort Eisenhower) — Federal government → PSLF-eligible.
- **University Hospital / Piedmont Augusta** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible.
- **Doctors Hospital of Augusta** (HCA Healthcare) — **For-profit, NOT PSLF-eligible.**
- **Select Specialty Hospital Augusta** — For-profit (Select Medical) → NOT PSLF-eligible.
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Augusta** — For-profit (publicly traded EHC) → NOT PSLF-eligible.

The trap many OTs fall into: rehab hospitals sound like nonprofits but are usually for-profit chains.

## 5 Money Tips for OTs in Augusta

1. **Federal employers offer the most predictable PSLF path.** Charlie Norwood VA and Eisenhower Army Medical both have OT positions. Federal salaries are transparent (GS pay scale), benefits include TSP match (4% automatic), and PSLF certification is straightforward.

2. **Watch the Wellstar acquisition transition.** AU Health Medical Associates was acquired by Wellstar in 2023. Some HR systems still show old employer names on W-2s. **Recertify PSLF immediately after any HR system change** — confirm the W-2 EIN matches a 501(c)(3) on the IRS exempt org search.

3. **Avoid Encompass Health and Select Specialty for PSLF.** Both have rehab hospitals in Augusta. Both look like rehab institutions. Both are for-profit publicly traded chains. They sometimes recruit OTs with $5,000–$15,000 signing bonuses — that bonus loses value against decades of PSLF discharge.

4. **Masters Week side income is taxable.** OTs renting their homes during the Masters Tournament can earn $5,000–$30,000 in a week. Under the IRS "Augusta Rule" (Section 280A), rentals of 14 nights or fewer per year are tax-free. Masters week is exactly 7 nights — keep it under 14 and document carefully.

5. **The North Augusta SC commute saves on income tax.** South Carolina has lower tax rates than Georgia for some income brackets. But if your job is in Augusta GA, you file GA non-resident return on GA-sourced income. Run the numbers — sometimes the savings are real, sometimes negligible.

## Student Loan Considerations

OTs typically graduate with **$80,000–$140,000** in debt (master's or doctoral degree). On IDR at $80,000 income:
- SAVE plan payment: ~$340–$480/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$900–$1,600/month

The 10-year PSLF math in Augusta with median OT salary:
- Average debt: $110,000
- 120 SAVE payments at ~$400/month = $48,000 paid in
- PSLF discharges remaining ~$62,000+ accrued interest
- **Net benefit: $80,000–$120,000 over a career**

## FAQ

**Is Augusta a strong PSLF market for OTs?**
Yes — five qualifying employers (Wellstar MCG, VA, Eisenhower Army, Piedmont, FQHCs) within 15 minutes of each other. Better employer density than Macon or Columbus.

**Why doesn't Encompass Health Rehab qualify for PSLF?**
Encompass Health is a publicly traded for-profit corporation (NYSE: EHC). Rehab hospitals are not automatically nonprofit. Verify each employer's 501(c)(3) status on the IRS exempt organizations database.

**Should I take the Doctors Hospital signing bonus?**
Doctors Hospital of Augusta is HCA Healthcare (for-profit). If you're early career with $100k+ debt, the bonus rarely beats long-term PSLF discharge from a qualifying employer.

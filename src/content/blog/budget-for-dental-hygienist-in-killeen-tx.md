# Dental Hygienist Budget Template for Killeen, TX (2026 Guide)

A practical **dental hygienist budget for Killeen, TX** has to do three things at once: account for the cost of living around **Fort Hood** and the III Armored Corps community of roughly 36,500 active-duty soldiers, take advantage of Texas having **no state income tax**, and align with PSLF-eligible employers like **Carl R. Darnall Army Medical Center (CRDAMC)** and **Killeen Independent School District**. This 2026 guide gives you salary ranges, a sample monthly budget, a military spouse network playbook, and a PSLF employer map so you can plan with confidence as a DH in Bell County.

## 1. Average Dental Hygienist Salary in Killeen

Killeen's DH market is shaped by Fort Hood's massive population, two regional hospital systems, and high turnover driven by PCS (Permanent Change of Station) cycles.

| Experience Level | Annual Salary (Bell County BLS) | Hourly |
|---|---|---|
| Entry (0-2 yrs) | $70,000 - $75,000 | $33 - $36 |
| Mid (3-7 yrs) | $75,000 - $82,000 | $36 - $39 |
| Senior (8+ yrs) | $82,000 - $90,000 | $39 - $43 |
| Lead / Hospital DH | $88,000 - $95,000 | $42 - $46 |

Because **Texas has no state income tax**, take-home pay on $78,000 in Killeen is meaningfully higher than the same nominal salary in California or New York.

## 2. Cost of Living Overview

Killeen remains one of the more affordable mid-size markets in the state, largely thanks to military housing supply.

- **Housing:** 1BR $950-1,200/mo; 2BR $1,250-1,650/mo; modest 3BR house $1,600-2,000/mo
- **Utilities:** $180-240/mo (Texas summers push electric bills up)
- **Groceries:** $380-460/mo single, $700-850 couple
- **Transportation:** Car required; gas $130-180/mo, insurance $130-170/mo
- **Healthcare premiums (DH employer plan):** $120-220/mo

## 4. Military Spouse Network Strategy

About 1 in 4 working-age adults in Killeen is connected to Fort Hood, and the DH community reflects that. Specific moves that pay off:

- **Texas State Loan Repayment Program (SLRP)** participation is concentrated in Bell County — ask employers about it during interviews.
- Join **Fort Hood Spouses' Club** and the **Killeen Chamber Military Affairs Committee** — both are direct pipelines to private practices that prioritize hiring spouses.
- Use **MyCAA** funds (if you're an E-1 to E-5 spouse) for continuing education credits required for Texas DH license renewal.
- Practices that participate in **TRICARE** networks tend to be the most flexible with PCS-driven schedule gaps.

## 5. PSLF Eligible Employers in Killeen

These are confirmed 501(c)(3) or government employers qualifying for **Public Service Loan Forgiveness**:

- **Carl R. Darnall Army Medical Center (CRDAMC)** — federal/Army hospital, dental clinics across Fort Hood
- **Killeen Independent School District (KISD)** — district health services and school-based dental
- **AdventHealth Central Texas** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit hospital in Killeen
- **Baylor Scott & White Medical Center – Temple** — 30 minutes north, large nonprofit system with dental specialty support
- **Bell County Public Health District** — county government, community dental programs
- **Fort Hood Dental Clinics (DENTAC)** — federal civilian dental positions

For the full breakdown, see our [PSLF Eligible Employers in Killeen, TX](/blog/pslf-eligible-employers-in-killeen-tx/) guide.

## 6. Savings Strategy for Killeen DHs

1. **Max the Texas advantage:** Redirect 6-9% of gross to retirement, since you're not losing it to state tax.
2. **Stack PSLF + 401(k):** Lower AGI through pre-tax retirement contributions reduces IDR payments AND grows tax-deferred wealth.
3. **Build a 6-month emergency fund** — Killeen's market is dependent on Fort Hood policy; BRAC-like shifts have hit local employment before.
4. **Roth IRA before HSA** unless your employer plan is HDHP-compatible.
5. Compare your strategy in higher-paying markets like our [Dental Hygienist Budget for Atlanta, GA](/blog/budget-for-dental-hygienist-in-atlanta-ga/) — Atlanta pays more but state tax + housing eats most of it.

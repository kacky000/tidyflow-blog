Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital Boston is widely considered one of the top three inpatient rehabilitation flagships in the United States, alongside Shirley Ryan AbilityLab in Chicago and Shepherd Center in Atlanta. What makes Spaulding strategically important for healthcare workers carrying federal student loans, however, is not just its clinical reputation — it is the fact that Spaulding sits inside the Mass General Brigham (MGB) family of 501(c)(3) nonprofit hospitals, which means **every clinical role at Spaulding qualifies for Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF)**.

This guide is the second installment of our [Inpatient Rehabilitation PSLF Map](/blog/inpatient-rehabilitation-pslf-map-2026/) deep-dive series. If you are an OT, PT, OTA, PTA, SLP, RT, RN, physiatrist, rehab psychologist, or social worker considering Spaulding, this article walks through the exact W-2 entities, pay ranges by role, and the certification steps you need to lock in 120 qualifying payments.

## 30-Second Summary

- Spaulding sits under Mass General Brigham, a **501(c)(3) nonprofit** — PSLF eligible ✅
- **Four locations**: Charlestown (flagship), Cambridge (LTAC + rehab), Cape Cod (East Sandwich), and Spaulding Hospital for Continuing Medical Care
- All clinical roles qualify: **OT, PT, OTA, PTA, SLP, RT, RN, MD, rehab counselor, case manager, LICSW**
- W-2 entity will read **"Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital Corporation"** or another MGB-affiliated 501(c)(3) — both are PSLF-qualified
- Acts as the **nonprofit defensive line** against Encompass Health's expansion into New England

## The Spaulding Rehabilitation Network

Spaulding is not a single building — it is a four-site network that gives clinicians flexibility on geography while keeping PSLF eligibility intact across the entire system.

### 1. Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital Boston (Charlestown)

The flagship 132-bed inpatient rehab hospital, founded in 1971 and relocated to its current Charlestown waterfront campus in 2013. Spaulding Charlestown is a federally designated **Spinal Cord Injury (SCI) Model System** center — one of only 14 in the country — which gives it access to NIDILRR research funding and a continuous flow of complex SCI patients. The hospital also hosts the Spaulding Neuromodulation Center and partners with the Cerebral Palsy Foundation for research.

### 2. Spaulding Hospital Cambridge

A 60-bed long-term acute care (LTAC) hospital plus rehab beds, serving patients who need both medically complex stabilization and rehabilitation. Many therapists who want a slower census and more medically complex case mix prefer Cambridge over Charlestown.

### 3. Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital Cape Cod (East Sandwich)

60-bed inpatient rehab hospital serving the Cape and the Islands. This site stays open year-round but sees a meaningful census bump during summer tourist season (more falls, more cardiac, more orthopedic post-op).

### 4. Spaulding Hospital for Continuing Medical Care (Cambridge)

LTAC-focused facility for long-stay medically complex patients. Strong RT and complex RN demand.

## MGB Entity Structure & PSLF Eligibility

PSLF qualification is determined by **the legal employer on your W-2**, not the brand on the building. The Mass General Brigham family of 501(c)(3) entities includes:

- The General Hospital Corporation (MGH)
- The Brigham and Women's Hospital, Inc.
- **Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital Corporation**
- McLean Hospital Corporation
- Newton-Wellesley Hospital Inc.
- North Shore Medical Center, Inc.

All are 501(c)(3) nonprofits, all are listed in the federal PSLF Employer Search Tool, and all qualify ✅. If your offer letter shows any of these names, your employment will certify cleanly.

## Career Paths at Spaulding: Pay Ranges & Responsibilities

The following ranges reflect Boston-area pay bands for 2026, drawing on MGB-posted listings, Glassdoor self-reported wages, and Massachusetts Department of Labor occupational wage data. Cape Cod base rates are typically 6–10% below Charlestown.

### Occupational Therapist (OT, OTR/L)

- **Pay**: $80,000–$98,000
- High specialty acuity: SCI, TBI, stroke, and burn rehab. Strong fieldwork pipeline from MGH Institute of Health Professions.

### Physical Therapist (PT, DPT)

- **Pay**: $82,000–$100,000
- SCI Model System research participation available. Locomotor training and robotic gait programs are funded at Charlestown.

### Occupational Therapy Assistant (OTA, COTA)

- **Pay**: $62,000–$78,000
- Hired at all four locations. Cape Cod is the easiest entry point for new graduates. See our [OTA budgeting guide for Boston](/blog/budget-for-occupational-therapy-assistant-in-boston) for cost-of-living planning.

### Physical Therapist Assistant (PTA)

- **Pay**: $60,000–$76,000
- Strong outpatient extension network across MGB campuses. PTAs are PSLF-eligible — see our [PTA PSLF guide](/blog/pslf-for-physical-therapist-assistants-complete-guide).

### Speech-Language Pathologist (SLP, CCC-SLP)

- **Pay**: $78,000–$95,000
- TBI cognitive-communication and post-stroke aphasia caseloads; videofluoroscopy access at Charlestown.

### Respiratory Therapist (RT)

- **Pay**: $75,000–$92,000
- Vent-weaning unit at Cambridge LTAC. SCI pulmonary rehab and tracheostomy management at Charlestown.

### Registered Nurse (RN, rehab specialty)

- **Pay**: $85,000–$110,000
- CRRN certification strongly encouraged; MGB pays for the exam and offers a clinical ladder differential.

### Physiatrist (PM&R MD)

- **Pay**: $280,000–$380,000
- Harvard Medical School academic appointment is available; protected research time at the SCI Model System.

### Rehab Psychologist / Counselor

- **Pay**: $80,000–$105,000
- Adjustment-to-disability, SCI/TBI psychological support, and family counseling. Master's-level counselors are PSLF-eligible at Spaulding as long as the W-2 employer is the 501(c)(3) entity (not a contracted staffing agency).

### Case Manager / Social Worker (LICSW)

- **Pay**: $72,000–$92,000
- Discharge planning and complex insurance coordination. LICSW preferred.

## MGB Tuition Benefits + PSLF Stack

This is where Spaulding becomes especially powerful. The **MGH Institute of Health Professions** is MGB's in-house graduate school, offering OTD, DPT, MS-SLP, NP, and MS-Counseling tracks. Spaulding employees receive an MGB tuition benefit that meaningfully reduces graduate tuition.

The practical stack: hire on as an **OTA or PTA at Spaulding Cape Cod** at $62K–$70K, work full-time, accrue PSLF months from day one, enroll in the MGH Institute OTD or DPT program part-time using the tuition benefit, then transition into the OT/PT role while **PSLF months continue uninterrupted** because the W-2 employer never changes. Many MGB therapists hit 120 qualifying payments before their forty-fifth birthday using this exact path.

## Why Spaulding Is the Nonprofit Defensive Line in New England

Encompass Health, the largest for-profit inpatient rehab operator in the country, has steadily expanded into New England. The 2024 opening of **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Western Massachusetts** marked the first major for-profit inpatient rehab campus in the state.

For-profit rehab hospitals do **not qualify for PSLF**. A therapist who accepts an Encompass offer instead of a Spaulding offer is effectively trading roughly $40,000–$60,000 of expected loan forgiveness for a few thousand dollars in starting salary differential. We documented a similar trap with [Emory Rehabilitation Hospital's Select Medical joint venture in Atlanta](/blog/emory-rehabilitation-hospital-atlanta-select-medical-trap) — same playbook, different region.

Spaulding's continued growth (and the SCI Model System designation, which is hard to relocate) is what keeps inpatient rehab PSLF careers viable across Massachusetts.

## PSLF Certification: Step-by-Step at Spaulding

1. On your offer letter, confirm the legal employer name reads **"Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital Corporation"** or another MGB 501(c)(3) entity — not a contracted staffing agency.
2. Use the [PSLF Employer Search Tool](https://studentaid.gov/pslf/employer-search/search-tool) on studentaid.gov to confirm the EIN is flagged as qualifying.
3. Consolidate any FFEL loans into Direct Loans if you have not already.
4. Enroll in an income-driven repayment plan (PAYE, IBR, or SAVE once litigation resolves).
5. Submit the PSLF & Employment Certification Form **once per year** and again any time you change employers.
6. After 120 qualifying monthly payments, submit the PSLF application — the remaining balance is forgiven tax-free under current federal law.

## FAQ

**Q: I worked at Partners HealthCare before it rebranded as Mass General Brigham. Do those months still count?**
Yes. Partners HealthCare and Mass General Brigham are the same legal nonprofit entity — only the brand name changed in 2019. Months worked under "Partners HealthCare System, Inc." count toward PSLF as long as your W-2 employer was one of the 501(c)(3) member hospitals.

**Q: Cape Cod salaries are lower than Charlestown. Is the trade worth it?**
For OTAs and PTAs, the cost-of-living differential and shorter commute often more than offset the 6–10% lower base. PSLF eligibility is **identical** at all four Spaulding sites because the W-2 employer is the same nonprofit corporation, so do not let pay alone drive the decision.

**Q: I want to start at Spaulding as a PT, then move to MGH outpatient PT later. Do PSLF months stay continuous?**
Yes, with one caveat: you must verify the W-2 employer at the new MGH outpatient clinic. Most MGH outpatient sites employ therapists through "The General Hospital Corporation," which is also 501(c)(3) and PSLF-qualified. Submit a fresh PSLF certification form within 30 days of the transfer to lock in the continuity.

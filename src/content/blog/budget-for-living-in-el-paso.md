# How to Budget for Living in El Paso

**Budgeting for life in El Paso** is one of the best financial decisions you can make if you're looking for an affordable Texas city. El Paso sits on the US-Mexico border with some of the lowest costs of living in the entire state — making it a hidden gem for budget-conscious residents and military families stationed at Fort Bliss.

El Paso's cost of living runs about 10-15% below the national average. Housing is the biggest win: you can rent a solid one-bedroom apartment for under $1,000 in many neighborhoods.

## Money-Saving Tips Specific to El Paso

**1. Shop at local border markets.** El Paso's proximity to Juárez means access to incredibly affordable fresh produce, meats, and goods. Ciudad Juárez markets offer savings of 30-50% on everyday groceries.

**2. Take advantage of military discounts.** Fort Bliss is one of the largest US Army installations. Even if you're not military, businesses throughout El Paso offer competitive pricing to stay attractive to military families.

**3. Use Sun Metro bus system.** El Paso's public transit covers most of the city for $1.50 per ride. A monthly pass costs around $30, making car ownership optional for many residents.

**4. Eat at local spots, not chains.** El Paso's Mexican food scene is exceptional and cheap. A full meal at a taquería costs $6-$10 compared to $15-$25 at chain restaurants.

**5. No state income tax.** Texas has no state income tax. Your take-home pay goes further here than in states like California or New York — that's an automatic 4-9% raise just from moving.

## FAQ

### What is a good salary to live in El Paso?
A gross salary of $35,000-$45,000/year (roughly $2,900-$3,700/month after taxes) is comfortable for a single person in El Paso. You can cover all expenses and still save 15-20%.

### Is El Paso cheaper than San Antonio?
Yes — El Paso tends to be 5-10% cheaper than San Antonio, particularly for rent. Both cities benefit from Texas's no-income-tax advantage.

### Is El Paso safe to live in?
El Paso consistently ranks among the safest large cities in the US. Despite being on the border, local crime rates are low. Neighborhoods like Westside and Upper Valley are especially family-friendly.

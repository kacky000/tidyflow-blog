## 5 Financial Tips for OTs in Pensacola

1. **Florida's no-income-tax advantage is real.** At $80K, you save ~$3,500–$4,000/year compared to working in South Carolina or North Carolina. Direct these savings to your student loans or Roth IRA.

2. **Target Baptist Health Care or Ascension Sacred Heart.** Both are large Pensacola hospital systems. Baptist Health Care is a nonprofit — Ascension is also nonprofit — making OTs working there potentially eligible for [PSLF](https://gettidyflow.com/blog/pslf-for-nurses-complete-guide).

3. **Consider school-based OT contracts.** Escambia County School District hires OTs as government employees, which counts as a qualifying employer for PSLF and often includes summer pay or year-round salaries.

4. **Budget for hurricane season costs.** Set aside $50–$100/month in a dedicated storm fund. Pensacola's Gulf Coast location means periodic evacuation costs, generators, or storm supplies aren't optional.

5. **Look into contract/travel OT work.** Pensacola's proximity to Gulf Coast resort communities creates seasonal travel therapy opportunities at $45–$60/hour, 13-week contracts, with housing stipends.

## Student Loan Strategy for OTs

OT graduates typically carry $80,000–$140,000 in student debt. In Pensacola:

- **SAVE plan** at $80K income: ~$540–$620/month
- **Standard 10-year** at $100K debt: ~$1,100/month
- **PSLF track**: Work at Baptist Health Care or Escambia County schools → forgiveness after 120 qualifying payments. For $120K debt, PSLF can mean $60,000–$80,000 forgiven tax-free.

Run the numbers carefully: PSLF often beats aggressive payoff for OTs with high debt loads.

## FAQ

**Is there a shortage of occupational therapists in Pensacola?**
Yes — the Pensacola metro has a consistent OT shortage, particularly in pediatric and geriatric settings. This gives experienced OTs strong negotiating leverage.

**Do Pensacola OTs get sign-on bonuses?**
Increasingly yes. $5,000–$15,000 sign-on bonuses are common at larger systems and home health agencies, especially for OTs with 2+ years experience.

**What's the PSLF situation at Ascension Sacred Heart?**
Ascension is a nonprofit 501(c)(3), so OTs working at Sacred Heart Pensacola are eligible for PSLF. Confirm your employer's EIN at studentaid.gov before assuming eligibility.

Savannah is one of America's most beautiful cities — and it's also more financially viable for healthcare workers than its tourism reputation suggests. For dental hygienists, the combination of a growing healthcare market, Georgia's moderate flat income tax, and solid PSLF-eligible employers creates a real opportunity. Here's your complete budget guide.

## What Dental Hygienists Earn in Savannah

BLS data shows dental hygienists in the Savannah metro area earn between **$61,000 and $75,000** annually. The median sits around **$66,000/year**. Memorial Health (now HCA) and St. Joseph's/Candler hospital-affiliated dental programs pay at the higher end; general dentistry private practices vary.

**Estimated monthly take-home (at $66K gross, Georgia — 5.49% flat state tax):**
- Federal income tax: ~$870/month
- Georgia state income tax: ~$302/month
- FICA (Social Security + Medicare): ~$421/month
- Health insurance: ~$158/month
- **Estimated net: ~$3,850–$4,100/month**

Georgia's 5.49% flat tax (as of 2024, phasing toward 4.99%) is competitive with surrounding states and significantly lower than South Carolina's top marginal rate.

## PSLF Eligibility in Savannah

Savannah's PSLF landscape requires careful employer selection:

- **St. Joseph's/Candler Health System** — Large nonprofit health system in Savannah; PSLF eligible. Dental and oral surgery programs
- **Memorial Health University Medical Center** — Now HCA (for-profit); **does NOT qualify for PSLF.** Verify employer status carefully
- **Coastal Georgia Area Community Mental Health Center / FQHCs** — Government and nonprofit community health; check 501(c)(3) or FQHC designation
- **Savannah VA Clinic (Charlie Norwood VA, Augusta)** — For full VA access, Augusta VA is the primary site, but Savannah outpatient clinic is federal employment
- **Chatham County Health Department** — Government employer, PSLF eligible

**Critical note:** Memorial Health Savannah was acquired by HCA (for-profit) — verify current employer status before relying on PSLF eligibility. St. Joseph's/Candler remains nonprofit and PSLF eligible.

**PSLF Math for a Savannah Dental Hygienist:**
- Debt: $42,000 at 6.5%
- SAVE plan payment at $66K: ~$332/month
- Standard 10-year payoff: ~$476/month
- PSLF monthly savings: ~$144/month + forgiven balance

## Savannah Job Market for Dental Hygienists

Savannah's healthcare market is growing as the city expands:

- **St. Joseph's/Candler**: Primary PSLF-eligible employer. Multiple service lines with dental program affiliations
- **Savannah area private practices**: Growing market driven by population growth. Competitive hourly rates in the $33–$40/hour range
- **Coastal Empire FQHCs**: Community health sites serving the Savannah region; PSLF eligible
- **Military-adjacent**: Fort Stewart (30 miles away) has a large military community — civilian dental positions at nearby bases qualify for PSLF

**Growing opportunity:** Savannah's port expansion and population growth are driving healthcare demand. New residents need dental care, and the hygienist market has been tight.

## Unique Savannah Financial Considerations

**Tourism premium on rent:** The Historic District adds 20–30% to rent vs. equivalent square footage in Pooler or Richmond Hill. Most dental hygienists can save $250–$400/month by choosing suburban locations.

**Georgia's tax trajectory:** Georgia is phasing its income tax from 5.75% toward 4.99% over several years. This will improve take-home pay slightly over time.

**Fort Stewart proximity:** A 30-minute drive to the base opens federal employment options. If civilian dental hygienist positions are available at Fort Stewart or Hunter Army Airfield, federal employment = automatic PSLF eligibility.

## FAQ

**Is Savannah affordable for a dental hygienist?**
Moderately. It's more expensive than inland SC and GA cities, but the lifestyle quality and growing job market offset the premium. Living in Pooler or Richmond Hill improves the financial picture significantly.

**Does Memorial Health Savannah qualify for PSLF?**
No longer — Memorial Health was acquired by HCA, a for-profit system. St. Joseph's/Candler is your primary nonprofit (PSLF-eligible) option in Savannah.

**How does Savannah compare to Jacksonville FL financially?**
Jacksonville is slightly better financially: zero state tax (vs. Georgia's 5.49%) and comparable rent. Jacksonville edges ahead by ~$300/month take-home at similar salary levels.

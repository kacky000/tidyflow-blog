Fayetteville's economy is dominated by Fort Liberty (formerly Fort Bragg) — the largest U.S. military installation by population — and that means an unusually concentrated pool of PSLF-eligible employers. But there are also traps: contractor companies sitting on base do NOT qualify, and Cape Fear Valley Health requires careful verification. ## Why Fayetteville Is Different

Most U.S. cities have a handful of PSLF-eligible employers. Fayetteville has dozens — but the majority are federal civilian or military-adjacent positions where contractor employment is common. The key distinction: **PSLF requires direct federal, state, local, or 501(c)(3) employment.** Contracting through a private company while working on base does NOT qualify.

## ⚠️ Critical: Verify Cape Fear Valley Health Carefully

Cape Fear Valley Health is the major non-military healthcare system in Fayetteville. **It IS a 501(c)(3) nonprofit** — but with these caveats:

1. **Direct employment vs. contracted staffing**: Some nurses, OTs, and PTs work in Cape Fear Valley facilities but are employed by staffing companies (Aya Healthcare, Cross Country Healthcare, etc.). These contractor employees do NOT qualify for PSLF.

2. **Affiliated practices**: Some specialty physician groups operate inside Cape Fear Valley facilities but are organized as separate for-profit LLCs. Verify your W-2.

3. **Annual certification**: With healthcare consolidation trends, file Form PSLF every year regardless of how stable your employer seems.

### What to Verify Before Accepting an Offer

1. **W-2 employer name and EIN** — confirm it matches a 501(c)(3) determination or federal agency
2. **Hours requirement** — must be 30+ hours/week (or considered "full-time" by the employer)
3. **Direct employment vs. staffing/contractor** — contractor positions do NOT count
4. **Annual Form PSLF filing** — establish the routine in your first week

## Active Military Service and PSLF

If you're active duty Army at Fort Liberty (or any branch), your military service automatically qualifies as PSLF employment. Additional considerations:

- **Deployment time counts** — combat tours don't pause your PSLF clock
- **You may qualify for HEROES Act benefits** that pause loan accrual during deployment
- **Combine with SCRA (Servicemembers Civil Relief Act)** for 6% interest rate cap on pre-service loans
- **Officer student loan repayment programs (LRP)** may pay loans directly — verify these don't conflict with PSLF strategy

## FAQ

**Does the Army count as a PSLF employer?**
Yes. Active duty service in any branch of the U.S. military is automatically qualifying federal employment.

**Does Cape Fear Valley Health qualify for PSLF?**
Yes, IF you are a direct W-2 employee of Cape Fear Valley Health System (501(c)(3) nonprofit). Contracted staffing positions and affiliated for-profit practices do NOT qualify.

**What about Methodist University?**
Methodist University is a private 501(c)(3) nonprofit and DOES qualify for PSLF. Strayer University (for-profit) does NOT.

**Can I work at Fort Liberty and not qualify for PSLF?**
Yes, if you're employed by a defense contractor (Leidos, Booz Allen, CACI, etc.) instead of being a direct federal employee. This is the most common PSLF mistake in Fayetteville. Always verify your W-2.

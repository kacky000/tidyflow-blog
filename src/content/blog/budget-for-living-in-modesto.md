# Budget for Living in Modesto, CA: Complete Monthly Cost Breakdown (2026)

If you're thinking about **living in Modesto, California**, you've found one of the Central Valley's most underrated affordable options. Cheaper than Stockton, far cheaper than the Bay Area, and sitting at the edge of Bay Area commuter range, **budgeting in Modesto** rewards those who plan ahead — especially for summer electricity bills. Here's exactly what a monthly budget looks like.

## Modesto, CA at a Glance

Modesto is the seat of Stanislaus County, located about 90 miles east of San Francisco in California's Central Valley. With around 220,000 residents, it's the 18th largest city in California. The local economy is anchored in agriculture (almonds, walnuts, dairy, wine grapes), food processing, and healthcare.

**Key facts for budgeters:**
- No local city income tax (California state tax applies)
- California state income tax: 1%–13.3%
- No rent control citywide
- Public transit via Modesto Area Express (MAX)
- ACE train to Bay Area available from neighboring Riverbank/Escalon

## Monthly Budget Breakdown for Modesto, CA

### Housing: $1,100–$1,900/month

Modesto consistently undercuts Stockton and Sacramento on rent, making it attractive for budget-conscious renters and remote workers holding Bay Area salaries.

| Housing Type | Monthly Cost |
|---|---|
| Studio apartment | $950–$1,200 |
| 1-bedroom apartment | $1,100–$1,500 |
| 2-bedroom apartment | $1,400–$1,900 |
| 3-bedroom house (rent) | $1,800–$2,400 |

Median home prices sit around $360,000–$410,000 (2026). Neighborhoods like Salida, Riverdale, and north Modesto offer lower crime rates and are popular with families.

**Budget tip:** Apartment complexes along Briggsmore Avenue and Coffee Road tend to offer competitive move-in specials, especially in fall and winter months when rental demand dips.

### Transportation: $200–$600/month

Modesto is car-dependent. The MAX bus network covers the city, but schedules are infrequent and routes don't reach many suburban job sites. A personal vehicle is nearly a necessity.

| Transportation Option | Monthly Cost |
|---|---|
| Car payment (used vehicle) | $300–$500 |
| Car insurance (California average) | $150–$250 |
| Gas (avg 800 miles/month) | $120–$180 |
| MAX bus monthly pass | $56 |
| Occasional rideshare | $50–$100 |

**Bay Area commuter note:** If you're commuting to the Bay Area, budget an additional $300–$550/month for the ACE train or I-580/I-205 tolls, BART fare, and parking. Many Modesto-to-Bay Area commuters work a hybrid schedule to limit the 2-hour-each-way haul.

### Food & Groceries: $350–$600/month

Modesto's agricultural surroundings keep grocery prices competitive. Stanislaus County is one of the nation's top dairy-producing counties, so dairy products in particular are very affordable.

| Food Category | Monthly Cost |
|---|---|
| Groceries (1 person) | $300–$450 |
| Dining out (budget-friendly) | $80–$180 |
| Coffee shops | $40–$70 |

Modesto has several ethnic grocery options (Asian, Mexican, Middle Eastern) that undercut major chain prices on produce and staples. The Downtown Modesto Farmers' Market (Thursday evenings) offers seasonal produce at excellent value.

### Utilities: $160–$300/month

This is where Modesto budgets get squeezed. Summer temperatures regularly hit 100°F–105°F, and running air conditioning from June through September is not optional for most households.

| Utility | Monthly Cost |
|---|---|
| Electricity (PG&E) | $80–$160 (summer spikes to $220+) |
| Gas (Pacific Gas & Electric) | $30–$60 |
| Water/Sewer (City of Modesto) | $50–$80 |
| Internet (1 Gbps) | $50–$80 |

**Budget warning:** Expect your July and August electricity bills to jump to $200–$280 if you keep your home at a comfortable temperature. Pre-save $100–$150 in May and June to cover this spike without stress.

### Healthcare: $150–$400/month

Modesto's main employer in healthcare is Doctors Medical Center and Memorial Medical Center. Employer-sponsored coverage is common in the healthcare and agriculture industries.

| Healthcare Option | Monthly Cost |
|---|---|
| Employer-sponsored insurance (employee share) | $150–$300 |
| Covered California Silver plan (individual) | $290–$480 |
| Basic prescriptions + copays | $30–$80 |

## Modesto-Specific Money-Saving Tips

**1. Time your lease for fall or winter.** Rental demand in Modesto drops sharply after summer. Landlords are more willing to negotiate or offer a free month when students aren't moving and Bay Area transplants aren't flooding the market. Target October–January for the best deals.

**2. Enroll in PG&E's CARE program.** If your income qualifies, Pacific Gas & Electric's CARE (California Alternate Rates for Energy) program can cut your electricity bill by 20–30%. This is a significant saving given Modesto's hot summers.

**3. Use the fruit stands.** Stanislaus County produces a massive share of California's nuts, stone fruits, and dairy. Farm-direct fruit stands along McHenry Avenue and the Crows Landing corridor offer prices far below grocery stores during harvest season (July–October).

**4. Skip downtown and cook at home.** Modesto's restaurant scene is improving but still limited. Cooking at home saves $150–$300/month compared to eating out regularly, and the proximity to farms makes quality ingredients cheap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Modesto CA affordable compared to other California cities?

Yes. Modesto is one of the more affordable options in California — rent runs 50–65% below San Francisco and 30–40% below Los Angeles. Compared to other Central Valley cities, it's slightly cheaper than Stockton and similar to Fresno.

### What salary do you need to live comfortably in Modesto?

A single person generally needs $42,000–$52,000 gross annual income to live comfortably in Modesto — covering rent, a car, food, and modest savings. Two-income households or roommate situations make it significantly easier.

### Is Modesto safe to live in?

Modesto has higher-than-average crime rates in some neighborhoods. Research specific areas before signing a lease. North Modesto (near Sylvan/Salida), the Riverdale area, and Coffee Road corridor tend to be safer than central and south Modesto.

Nashville is the largest job market in Tennessee for occupational therapists, but it's also the **headquarters of HCA Healthcare** — the biggest for-profit hospital chain in the country. The result: an OT in Nashville can ride PSLF to discharge or walk into the worst trap in the South. This guide breaks down salary, cost of living, and the employer-by-employer PSLF map.

## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Distinction: TriStar (HCA) vs VUMC

OTs in Nashville face the worst PSLF trap in the Southeast because **HCA Healthcare is headquartered here** and recruits aggressively.

- **Vanderbilt University Medical Center (VUMC)** — Independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit (since 2016) → fully PSLF-eligible.
- **Vanderbilt Stallworth Rehabilitation Hospital** — Part of VUMC nonprofit → PSLF-eligible.
- **Nashville General Hospital at Meharry** — Public/city-owned → PSLF-eligible.
- **Ascension Saint Thomas (multiple campuses)** — Catholic 501(c)(3) → PSLF-eligible.
- **Nashville VA Medical Center / Murfreesboro VA** — Federal → PSLF-eligible.
- **TriStar Centennial, Skyline, Summit, Hendersonville, StoneCrest, Southern Hills** — All **HCA Healthcare → NOT PSLF-eligible.**
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Franklin** — Publicly traded for-profit → NOT PSLF-eligible.
- **Select Specialty Hospital Nashville** — Select Medical for-profit → NOT PSLF-eligible.

**A rehab OT switching from Vanderbilt Stallworth (nonprofit) to Encompass Franklin (for-profit) for $8/hour more loses ~$100,000 in PSLF benefit over a career.**

## 5 Money Tips for OTs in Nashville

1. **Maximize TN no-state-tax advantage.** No state income tax = roughly **$350–$500/month** more take-home than GA, AL, or NC at OT salaries. Direct that entire bonus into 403(b) or Roth IRA. You can fully fund both with the tax savings alone.

2. **Verify EIN before signing any HCA-area role.** The TriStar brand is HCA. The Skyline / Centennial / Summit names sound corporate-neutral but every TriStar hospital is for-profit. Check the W-2 EIN against IRS exempt org search before accepting.

3. **Vanderbilt Stallworth Rehab is the PSLF gold standard.** If you're a rehab OT in Nashville with student debt, target Stallworth or Saint Thomas inpatient rehab. Both are 501(c)(3). Encompass Franklin (10 minutes south) is for-profit.

4. **Avoid Williamson County rent inflation.** Franklin and Brentwood look attractive on Zillow, but you'll add $200+/month in commute costs to most Nashville hospitals. Inglewood, East Nashville, and Donelson have similar walk score at 25–30% less rent.

5. **TSP match at VA = automatic 4% return.** Nashville VA Medical Center and Murfreesboro VA both employ OTs. Federal TSP includes a 4% automatic agency contribution, plus matching on the first 5% of your salary contribution. That's a guaranteed 9% return before market gains.

## Student Loan Considerations

OTs in Nashville typically carry **$90,000–$160,000** in debt (master's or doctoral OT degree). On IDR at $88,000 income:
- SAVE plan payment: ~$420–$580/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$1,050–$1,800/month

10-year PSLF math at Nashville median OT salary:
- Average debt: $120,000
- 120 SAVE payments at ~$500/month = $60,000 paid in
- PSLF discharges remaining ~$60,000+ accrued interest
- **Net benefit: $80,000–$140,000 over a career**

## FAQ

**Is Nashville a strong PSLF market for OTs?**
Yes — VUMC alone is one of the largest 501(c)(3) employers in the Southeast. Plus Saint Thomas, VA, and city hospital. But you must actively avoid the HCA/TriStar half of the market.

**Why is Vanderbilt independent if Vanderbilt University is the parent?**
Vanderbilt University Medical Center became legally separate from Vanderbilt University in 2016. It's now its own 501(c)(3) — still PSLF-eligible.

**Should I take the TriStar signing bonus?**
On 10-year PSLF math, a $10,000 TriStar signing bonus often loses to $80,000–$100,000 in eventual VUMC or Saint Thomas PSLF discharge. Almost never worth it if you have $80k+ debt.

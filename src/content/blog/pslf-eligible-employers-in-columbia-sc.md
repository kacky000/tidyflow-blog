Columbia, South Carolina is the state capital and home to several major employers — including Prisma Health, the VA, University of South Carolina, and numerous state government agencies. For healthcare workers, social workers, educators, and government employees, Columbia is an excellent PSLF market.

This guide lists the PSLF-eligible employers in Columbia, SC and explains how to maximize forgiveness in South Carolina's capital region.

## PSLF-Eligible Hospitals and Health Systems in Columbia, SC

### ✅ Prisma Health Midlands (formerly Palmetto Health)
**Type:** Nonprofit health system
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (501(c)(3))
**Hospitals included:**
- Prisma Health Richland Hospital
- Prisma Health Baptist Hospital
- Prisma Health Children's Hospital
- Prisma Health Baptist Parkridge Hospital

Prisma Health is Columbia's dominant healthcare employer and one of the largest nonprofit systems in South Carolina. Nurses, OTs, social workers, respiratory therapists, and all direct employees qualify for PSLF.

This is the single most important PSLF employer in Columbia. Most healthcare workers in the metro should target Prisma Health if PSLF is a priority.

### ✅ William Jennings Bryan Dorn VA Medical Center
**Type:** Federal government / Department of Veterans Affairs
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (federal government)
**Who qualifies:** Civilian federal employees — nurses, social workers, OTs, psychologists, dietitians hired directly by the VA.

The Dorn VA is particularly valuable for PSLF because:
1. Federal government = automatic eligibility, no 501(c)(3) verification needed
2. VA also has its own education debt reduction programs for nurses
3. VA salaries are competitive with GS pay scale plus locality pay

### ✅ SC Department of Health and Environmental Control (DHEC)
**Type:** State government agency
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (government employer)
**Who qualifies:** Public health nurses, environmental health specialists, social workers, epidemiologists employed by DHEC.

DHEC is a major employer of public health nurses and epidemiologists in South Carolina. State government employment is automatic PSLF qualification.

### ✅ University of South Carolina (USC)
**Type:** Public university / government employer
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (government employer)
**Who qualifies:** All direct USC employees — faculty, staff, healthcare workers at the student health center, counselors.

USC is a large public university and government employer. Student health center nurses, counselors, and social workers employed by USC qualify for PSLF.

### ✅ Richland County School District / Lexington County Schools
**Type:** Government employer (public school district)
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (government employer)
**Who qualifies:** School nurses, school social workers, school OTs, and all certified teachers employed by the districts.

School districts are government employers — among the clearest PSLF qualifications available. Columbia-area school nurses and social workers earn PSLF while serving students.

### ✅ Community Healthcare, Inc. (FQHCs in Columbia)
**Type:** Federally Qualified Health Center
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (501(c)(3))
**Who qualifies:** All clinical staff at FQHC locations serving low-income patients in Columbia.

## South Carolina State Government and PSLF

South Carolina state government is a massive PSLF employer that's often overlooked outside healthcare:

- **SC Department of Social Services**: Social workers qualify
- **SC Department of Mental Health**: Counselors, social workers qualify
- **SC Law Enforcement Division (SLED)**: State law enforcement employees qualify
- **SC Department of Corrections**: Employees qualify
- **SC Department of Transportation**: Engineers, planners qualify

Any state government job in Columbia qualifies for PSLF. If you're a government employee in Columbia — in any department — submit your Employment Certification Form immediately.

## FAQ

**Does MUSC Health in Columbia qualify for PSLF?**
MUSC Health in Columbia operates through its relationship with the Medical University of SC. MUSC itself is a public university. However, some MUSC Health facilities in Columbia operate differently than the main Charleston campus. Verify your specific employer EIN at studentaid.gov before relying on PSLF.

**Are SC state employees automatically PSLF-eligible?**
Yes — all state government employees qualify for PSLF. Submit the Employment Certification Form to make it official and start tracking qualifying payments.

**What happened to Providence Hospital's PSLF status?**
HCA Healthcare acquired Providence Hospital in Columbia. HCA is the nation's largest for-profit hospital chain. HCA employees do NOT qualify for PSLF. If you previously worked at Providence assuming it was a Catholic nonprofit, your payments during HCA ownership do not count.

## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Information: Huntsville Hospital Health System

**This is the most important paragraph in this guide.**

Huntsville Hospital Health System (HH) is a **501(c)(3) nonprofit** → PSLF-eligible. This makes it the dominant PSLF employer in north Alabama:

- **Huntsville Hospital** (main campus)
- **Madison Hospital**
- **Decatur Morgan Hospital** (affiliated, verify W-2)
- **HH Heart Center**
- **Crestwood Medical Center** — Owned by HCA Healthcare → **for-profit, does NOT qualify for PSLF**

**Crestwood is the critical trap.** It's a major hospital in Huntsville that hires dental hygienists and oral health staff, but it's owned by HCA Healthcare (publicly traded for-profit). Many hygienists assume any "hospital job" qualifies for PSLF — Crestwood does not.

**Aerospace contractor trap (similar to Fort Liberty pattern)**: Many defense contractors near Redstone Arsenal (Lockheed Martin, Boeing, Northrop Grumman, Raytheon, SAIC, Leidos) offer corporate dental benefits — but employment is **for-profit private sector** and does NOT count toward PSLF. Working at the contractor's on-site clinic doesn't make you a federal employee.

## 5 Money Tips for Dental Hygienists in Huntsville

1. **Choose Huntsville Hospital over Crestwood for PSLF.** Same city, same job type, opposite PSLF status. Verify before accepting offers. If salary at Crestwood is $5,000 higher but you lose $40,000 in PSLF forgiveness, the math doesn't work.

2. **Aerospace dental clinics are a trap for PSLF seekers.** Working at the Redstone clinic for Lockheed or Boeing is corporate W-2 employment. Even if the patients are federal employees, your W-2 is not federal/nonprofit.

3. **Madison vs Huntsville rent calculation.** Madison (separate city, ~10 min west) is slightly cheaper than central Huntsville. Commutes are easy. Many hygienists working at Madison Hospital live in Madison itself for a 5-minute drive.

4. **Plan for tornado season.** North Alabama is in Tornado Alley's eastern extension. Severe weather can cause property damage and brief job disruptions. Renter's insurance is essential. $1,000+ separate weather fund recommended.

5. **Consider income growth ceiling.** Huntsville's job market is strong but specialized. Hygienist wages here have less ceiling than Atlanta or Nashville. If you plan to relocate later, save aggressively now while costs are still moderate.

PSLF eligibility in Huntsville:
- **Huntsville Hospital Health System**: 501(c)(3) — PSLF eligible.
- **Crestwood Medical Center (HCA)**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.
- **Madison Hospital (HH affiliate)**: PSLF eligible.
- **Defense contractor clinics (Lockheed, Boeing, Raytheon, SAIC)**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.
- **Federal civilian dental (Redstone Arsenal Health Clinic, VA Huntsville Outpatient)**: Direct federal employment → PSLF eligible.
- **Madison County Health Department / FQHCs**: PSLF eligible.
- **Private dental practices, DSOs**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.

## FAQ

**Why is Huntsville's job market different from other Alabama cities?**
Defense and aerospace. NASA Marshall, Redstone Arsenal, and contractors employ ~40,000+ engineers and support staff. That pushes up local wages across all sectors — including dental hygiene.

**Does working at a defense contractor's on-site dental clinic count for PSLF?**
No. Your W-2 employer is the contractor (Lockheed, Boeing, SAIC, etc.) — for-profit private companies. Federal employees as patients don't change your employer's tax status.

**How does Huntsville compare to Birmingham for dental hygienists?**
Huntsville pays slightly more but has fewer PSLF-eligible employers (Huntsville Hospital vs UAB's much larger footprint). Birmingham is better for PSLF strategy; Huntsville is better for take-home if you don't have student loans.

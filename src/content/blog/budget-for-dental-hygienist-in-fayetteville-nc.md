## 5 Tips for Dental Hygienists in Fayetteville

1. **Target military family patients.** Fort Liberty brings a constant flow of military families who value dental care. Dental offices near Yadkin Road or Skibo Road serve this population well, and these offices typically offer competitive pay and benefits.

2. **Commute to Raleigh for higher wages.** Raleigh is 65 miles north. Some Fayetteville hygienists work 3-4 days/week in Raleigh ($68,000–$82,000/year range) while living in Fayetteville for cheaper housing. The net math often works.

3. **Take advantage of NC's tax-free military retirement.** If your spouse is active military or retiring, NC doesn't tax military retirement pay — giving your household budget extra flexibility.

4. **PSLF through Cape Fear Valley Health.** Cape Fear Valley Medical Center is a county-owned nonprofit. Dental hygienists working in their system or affiliated clinics may qualify for [PSLF forgiveness](https://gettidyflow.com/blog/pslf-for-nurses-complete-guide).

5. **Build a 6-month emergency fund.** Military base communities can see population shifts during deployments or base reorganizations. A larger-than-usual emergency fund provides security against these fluctuations.

## FAQ

**Is Fayetteville, NC a good place to work as a dental hygienist?**
Yes — strong demand from military and civilian populations, affordable living, and opportunities at nonprofit health systems create a solid professional environment.

**How does North Carolina income tax affect dental hygienist pay?**
NC's flat 4.5% rate (dropping toward 4% by 2026) costs dental hygienists roughly $225–$280/month compared to zero-tax Florida. Still much lower than high-tax states.

**What neighborhoods should dental hygienists consider in Fayetteville?**
Haymount, Vanstory Hills, and areas near Westover Hills offer good value. Avoid flood-prone low areas near the Cape Fear River.

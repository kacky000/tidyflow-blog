Atlanta is one of the most strategically rich OT markets in the country — and it's almost entirely because of **Shepherd Center**. Shepherd is an independent nonprofit specialty rehabilitation hospital focused on SCI and ABI, and it's one of the top three places in the United States to build a high-acuity OT career while staying PSLF-eligible. Combined with Emory, CHOA, the Atlanta VA, Wellstar, and Piedmont, Atlanta has more PSLF-eligible OT seats than any city in the Southeast.

## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Distinction: Shepherd Center is the Crown Jewel

Atlanta's OT PSLF map is the deepest in the Southeast — and Shepherd Center is the centerpiece.

- **Shepherd Center** — Independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit specialty rehab (SCI, ABI, MS, pain) → **PSLF-eligible**. Top-5 SCI/ABI rehab program in the U.S. OT pay ~$82k–$108k. Tuition reimbursement, strong CE budget, and arguably the best inpatient OT learning environment in the Southeast.
- **Emory Healthcare** (Emory University Hospital, Emory Midtown, Emory Rehabilitation Hospital partnership, Emory Decatur, Emory Saint Joseph's, Emory Johns Creek) — 501(c)(3) nonprofit academic system → **PSLF-eligible**. OT range $78k–$100k.
  - ⚠️ **Emory Rehabilitation Hospital** is a joint venture with **Select Medical (NYSE: SEM)**. Confirm which entity is on your W-2 — Select Medical entity employment is NOT PSLF-eligible even if the building says "Emory."
- **Children's Healthcare of Atlanta (CHOA)** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit pediatric specialty → **PSLF-eligible**. Pediatric OT, NICU OT, outpatient peds. OT range $76k–$96k.
- **Wellstar Health System** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit → **PSLF-eligible**. OT range $74k–$94k.
- **Piedmont Healthcare** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit → **PSLF-eligible**. OT range $74k–$96k.
- **Northside Hospital** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit → **PSLF-eligible**.
- **Grady Health System** — 501(c)(3) safety-net nonprofit → **PSLF-eligible**. Heavy acuity, strong training.
- **Atlanta VA Health Care System (Decatur)** — Federal → **PSLF-eligible**. Polytrauma, SCI/D programs.
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital (multiple Atlanta locations)** — Publicly traded (NYSE: EHC) → **for-profit, NOT PSLF-eligible.**
- **Select Specialty Hospital (LTACH, multiple Atlanta sites)** — Select Medical (NYSE: SEM) → **for-profit, NOT PSLF-eligible.**
- **HCA Healthcare (multiple metro Atlanta sites)** — Publicly traded (NYSE: HCA) → **for-profit, NOT PSLF-eligible.**

**The trap layer**: Encompass Health Rehab, Select Specialty, and HCA all aggressively recruit OTs at $3–6/hour above nonprofit base. For OTs with $100k+ debt, the 10-year PSLF math beats the wage premium by $60k–$120k.

## 5 Money Tips for Occupational Therapists in Atlanta

1. **Shepherd Center is the career-defining play.** If you want SCI/ABI specialty depth AND PSLF, Shepherd is the answer. Few facilities in the U.S. combine top-5 program quality with nonprofit PSLF status.

2. **Verify Emory Rehab joint-venture status.** Emory Rehabilitation Hospital is partnered with Select Medical. Ask HR which entity issues your W-2. Select Medical entities are NOT PSLF-eligible regardless of "Emory" branding.

3. **Avoid the Encompass / Select trap.** Both are publicly traded for-profits. Their LTACH and inpatient rehab Atlanta sites pay above nonprofit base, but the PSLF gap is worth $60k–$120k on 10-year debt.

4. **Atlanta VA is the underrated win.** Federal employment + polytrauma/SCI rehab + PSLF + GS pay scale + TSP match. Especially strong if you want SCI work without Shepherd's pace.

5. **TN border commute possible for north metro OTs.** OTs working Wellstar Kennestone, Piedmont Cartersville, or Northside Cherokee can commute from TN border counties for $0 state tax — saves ~$4,500/year on $89k.

## Student Loan Considerations

In-state OT programs (Georgia State, Mercer, Augusta University, Brenau) graduate students with **$80,000–$140,000** in federal loans. OTD programs push debt to $140,000–$200,000.

On IDR at $89,000 income (GA):
- SAVE plan payment: ~$470–$620/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$900–$2,000/month

PSLF eligibility map for Atlanta:
- **Shepherd Center, Emory (direct W-2), CHOA, Wellstar, Piedmont, Northside, Grady, Atlanta VA, FQHCs (Mercy Care, Oakhurst, West End)**: PSLF eligible.
- **Encompass Health Rehab, Select Specialty, HCA, Emory Rehab JV (Select W-2), private SNFs, private contract therapy**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.

## FAQ

**Is Atlanta a good city for new OTs?**
Yes — arguably the best PSLF-eligible OT market in the Southeast. Shepherd Center alone makes it worth considering, and the surrounding nonprofit hospital network is unusually deep.

**Why is Shepherd Center such a big deal?**
Shepherd is a freestanding nonprofit specialty rehab focused on spinal cord injury, acquired brain injury, MS, and chronic pain. It's consistently top-5 in U.S. News rehab rankings and is one of the few SCI specialty programs in the country that's nonprofit and PSLF-eligible.

**Why doesn't Encompass Health Rehab qualify for PSLF?**
Encompass Health (NYSE: EHC) is publicly traded for-profit. Its rehab hospitals are for-profit regardless of clinical mission.

**Should I avoid Emory Rehabilitation Hospital entirely?**
Not necessarily — clinical quality is high. But verify which entity issues your W-2. If it's a Select Medical entity, your employment does NOT count toward PSLF. Ask before you sign.

# Physician Assistant Budget Template for San Diego, CA (2026)

Physician Assistants in San Diego earn some of the highest PA salaries in the country — but San Diego's cost of living is equally exceptional. If you're a PA working in the San Diego metro area, this guide breaks down exactly how to budget your income for long-term financial success.

## Physician Assistant Salary in San Diego

The median annual salary for a Physician Assistant in San Diego is **$135,000–$155,000** depending on specialty and years of experience:

| Specialty | Average Annual Salary |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Emergency Medicine | $155,000–$175,000 |
| Orthopedics | $140,000–$160,000 |
| Primary Care | $120,000–$140,000 |
| Dermatology | $125,000–$145,000 |
| Surgery | $145,000–$165,000 |

**Take-home pay estimate (single filer, $145,000 gross):**
- Federal + California state tax: ~34%
- Monthly gross: ~$12,083
- Monthly take-home: **~$7,975**

## Student Loan Strategy for San Diego PAs

Most PAs graduate with $100,000–$150,000 in student debt. In San Diego, two strong strategies:

**Option 1: PSLF (Public Service Loan Forgiveness)**
- If you work at a county hospital, federally qualified health center, or nonprofit health system
- Make 120 qualifying payments on IBR/PAYE plan
- Remaining balance forgiven tax-free
- Qualifying employers in San Diego: UC San Diego Health, Sharp Memorial Hospital (select departments), Scripps Mercy Hospital

**Option 2: Aggressive Payoff**
- Refinance to 5–7% fixed rate
- Target payoff in 5–7 years
- Frees up $400–$800/month for investment

## Building Wealth as a San Diego PA

At $145,000/year, you're in prime position to build significant wealth — if you control housing costs:

**Year 1–3 Goals:**
- Max out 403(b)/401(k) to employer match ($23,500 limit in 2026)
- Build 3-month emergency fund ($22,000–$24,000)
- Aggressively pay down student loans

**Year 3–7 Goals:**
- Max HSA ($4,300/year for individual)
- Open Roth IRA ($7,000/year)
- Save for San Diego home down payment ($80,000–$120,000 for a condo)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is San Diego worth it on a PA salary?**
Yes — PA salaries in San Diego are high enough to build wealth, especially if you keep housing costs under $2,500/month. With proper budgeting, you can save $1,500–$2,000/month even in a high cost-of-living city.

**Should I rent or buy in San Diego as a PA?**
With median home prices at $800,000+, buying requires a $160,000+ down payment. Most PAs spend 3–5 years in San Diego renting before buying — use that time to aggressively pay down loans and build savings.

**Which San Diego hospital systems qualify for PSLF?**
UC San Diego Health and federally qualified health centers qualify. Scripps and Sharp have mixed eligibility — verify each specific position at the PSLF employer search tool before accepting any offer.

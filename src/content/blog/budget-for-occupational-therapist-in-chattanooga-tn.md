## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Distinction: Erlanger vs CHI Memorial vs Parkridge vs Siskin

Chattanooga has the most PSLF-clear hospital map of any TN city:

- **Erlanger Health System** — Hamilton County **public hospital** (governmental) → fully PSLF-eligible. The flagship for high-acuity inpatient OT.
- **CHI Memorial Hospital (CommonSpirit Health)** — Catholic 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible. Solid inpatient and outpatient OT volume.
- **Siskin Hospital for Physical Rehabilitation** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit specialty inpatient rehab → PSLF-eligible. **One of the cleanest PSLF inpatient rehab gigs in the Southeast** — most other regional rehab is Encompass Health.
- **T.C. Thompson Children's at Erlanger** — Public → PSLF-eligible. Pediatric OT.
- **Parkridge Medical Center, Parkridge East, Parkridge West** — Owned by **HCA Healthcare** → **for-profit, NOT PSLF-eligible.**
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Chattanooga** — Publicly traded (NYSE: EHC) → **for-profit, NOT PSLF-eligible.**
- **Select Specialty Hospital Chattanooga** — Select Medical (NYSE: SEM) → **for-profit, NOT PSLF-eligible.**
- **Chattanooga VA Clinic** — Federal → PSLF-eligible.

**The trap layer**: Encompass Health Rehab and Siskin Hospital are 5 miles apart — both inpatient rehab — completely different PSLF status. Many OTs are recruited between them without understanding the tax distinction.

## 5 Money Tips for Occupational Therapists in Chattanooga

1. **Siskin Hospital is one of the best PSLF rehab gigs in the South.** Most regional inpatient rehab is Encompass Health (for-profit). Siskin is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit. PSLF-eligible. Highly recommended for OTs with debt.

2. **Erlanger = public hospital = PSLF gold.** A Hamilton County hospital authority counts as governmental for PSLF. Erlanger Baroness is the level 1 trauma center.

3. **Avoid the Parkridge trap.** Parkridge is HCA. Like TriStar (Nashville), the brand looks generic but is publicly traded for-profit.

4. **Avoid the Encompass / Select trap.** Encompass Health (NYSE: EHC) and Select Specialty (NYSE: SEM) are publicly traded. Their rehab facilities in Chattanooga are NOT PSLF-eligible.

5. **Lookout Mountain GA arbitrage may not work.** Lookout Mountain is technically two cities (one in TN, one in GA). If you live on the GA side, you owe GA state income tax (5.39%), which often outweighs the housing savings.

## Student Loan Considerations

UT-Chattanooga OT program (MS/OTD) graduates carry **$80,000–$140,000** in federal loans. Out-of-state OTD grads carry $140,000–$200,000.

On IDR at $76,000 income (TN):
- SAVE plan payment: ~$390–$510/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$900–$1,800/month

PSLF eligibility map for Chattanooga:
- **Erlanger (public), CHI Memorial (CommonSpirit), Siskin Hospital, T.C. Thompson Children's, FQHCs, Chattanooga VA**: PSLF eligible.
- **Parkridge (HCA), Encompass Health Rehab, Select Specialty, private SNFs/outpatient**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.

## FAQ

**Is Chattanooga a good city for new occupational therapists?**
Yes — arguably the best PSLF clarity of any TN city, with Erlanger (public) + CHI Memorial (nonprofit) + Siskin (nonprofit rehab) all qualifying.

**Why doesn't Parkridge Medical Center qualify for PSLF?**
Parkridge is HCA Healthcare's Chattanooga brand. HCA is publicly traded for-profit. The hospital being large or modern does not change tax status.

**Is Siskin Hospital really an independent nonprofit?**
Yes. Siskin Hospital for Physical Rehabilitation is a 501(c)(3) independent nonprofit specialty rehab hospital. It's one of the few non-Encompass options in the region.

Jacksonville, Florida is home to several major hospital systems, but not all of them qualify for Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF). This guide lists every PSLF-eligible hospital and health employer in the Jacksonville area so nurses, social workers, occupational therapists, and other healthcare professionals can make informed career decisions.

## PSLF Quick Refresher

PSLF forgives federal student loan balances after 10 years (120 qualifying payments) of full-time work at a qualifying employer. The key requirements:

- Federal Direct Loans (or consolidated into Direct)
- Income-Driven Repayment plan (SAVE, PAYE, IBR, or ICR)
- Full-time employment (30+ hours/week) at a qualifying employer
- 120 qualifying monthly payments

For hospital employees, qualifying means working at a **government-owned hospital** or a **nonprofit hospital with 501(c)(3) status**.

## PSLF-Eligible Hospitals in Jacksonville, FL

### ✅ UF Health Jacksonville
**Type:** Academic medical center / University of Florida Health System
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (nonprofit, 501(c)(3))
**Who qualifies:** Nurses, social workers, OTs, PTs, respiratory therapists, case managers, dietitians, and all clinical staff employed directly by UF Health.

UF Health Jacksonville is one of the largest PSLF-qualifying employers in Northeast Florida. Its affiliation with the University of Florida (public university system) reinforces its nonprofit status. It is also a Level I Trauma Center, offering specialized nursing and therapy opportunities.

### ✅ Baptist Health System (Jacksonville)
**Type:** Nonprofit hospital system
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (nonprofit, 501(c)(3))
**Hospitals included:**
- Baptist Medical Center Jacksonville
- Baptist Medical Center Beaches
- Baptist Medical Center Nassau
- Wolfson Children's Hospital
- Baptist Medical Center South

**Who qualifies:** All direct employees of Baptist Health (not agency or contract staff). Baptist is one of Jacksonville's most trusted healthcare employers and a major PSLF pipeline for nurses and allied health professionals.

### ✅ Ascension St. Vincent's Health System
**Type:** Catholic nonprofit system
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (nonprofit, 501(c)(3))
**Hospitals included:**
- Ascension St. Vincent's Riverside
- Ascension St. Vincent's Southside
- Ascension St. Vincent's Clay County
- Ascension St. Vincent's Behavioral Health

**Who qualifies:** All direct Ascension employees. Catholic Health initiatives operate as nonprofits nationwide. Ascension is among the largest nonprofit health systems in the US.

### ✅ Naval Hospital Jacksonville
**Type:** Federal government / U.S. Navy
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (federal government employer)
**Who qualifies:** Civilian federal employees (not active military, not contractors). If you're a nurse, social worker, or therapist hired directly as a GS-series civilian employee, you qualify.

**Note:** Military active duty members are on a different loan forgiveness track (not PSLF). Civilian contractors do NOT qualify — only direct government hires.

### ✅ Department of Veterans Affairs (Malcom Randall / Jacksonville VAMC)
**Type:** Federal government
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (federal government employer)
**Who qualifies:** All VA direct employees. VA employees often combine PSLF with VA's own education debt reduction program for nurses.

### ✅ Community Health Centers of Jacksonville (FQHCs)
**Type:** Federally Qualified Health Center
**PSLF Status:** ✅ Eligible (nonprofit, 501(c)(3))
**Who qualifies:** Clinical and non-clinical staff employed at FQHC locations. FQHCs receive federal funding to serve underserved populations — all are nonprofit by definition.

## How to Verify PSLF Eligibility in Jacksonville

Before accepting any position:

1. **Ask HR** if the employer is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit or government entity
2. **Search studentaid.gov** PSLF employer search tool
3. **Look up the EIN** on ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer
4. **Submit an Employment Certification Form** on your first day — don't wait

For government employers (Navy, VA), verification is straightforward — all government agencies qualify automatically.

## The PSLF Math for Jacksonville Healthcare Workers

At $72,000 income (typical RN salary in Jacksonville):
- SAVE plan monthly payment: ~$445
- 10-year total paid: ~$53,400
- If starting debt was $100,000, balance at 10 years on SAVE: ~$70,000+
- **Forgiven amount: $70,000+ tax-free**

Compare to standard 10-year repayment at $100K debt: total paid ~$127,000. PSLF saves roughly $73,000.

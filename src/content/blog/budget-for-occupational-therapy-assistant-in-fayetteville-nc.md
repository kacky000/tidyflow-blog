Fayetteville's OTA market is unusual: Fort Liberty (formerly Fort Bragg) creates massive federal civilian OTA demand at Womack Army Medical Center and the VA, while Cape Fear Valley Health and Cumberland County Schools round out the market. For OTAs, this means an unusually high concentration of PSLF-eligible employers — and an associate-degree debt load that's manageable from day one.

## Why Fayetteville Is Strong for OTAs

Most cities of Fayetteville's size have limited PSLF-eligible employers for OTAs. Fayetteville is different:

- **Womack Army Medical Center** — Federal government (Department of the Army)
- **Fayetteville VA Medical Center** — Federal government (VA)
- **Cape Fear Valley Health** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit (verify W-2)
- **Cumberland County Schools** — Local government (school-based OTA roles)
- **CommWell Health** — FQHC (Federally Qualified Health Center)

For OTAs with associate-degree debt, the PSLF math isn't always compelling (debt is too low), but the combination of stable federal/state employment, no commute traffic, and Fayetteville's low cost of living creates strong take-home savings rates.

## Average OTA Salary in Fayetteville

OTAs in the Fayetteville MSA earn between **$42,000–$54,000 per year**, with a median near $48,000. Federal civilian positions at Womack AMC and Fayetteville VAMC follow GS pay scales (often GS-7 to GS-9 for OTAs, ~$46,000–$62,000 with locality pay).

## 5 Money Tips for OTAs in Fayetteville

1. **Federal civilian positions at Womack or VA come with TSP match.** Federal employees can contribute to TSP (Thrift Savings Plan) with 5% employer match. On a $54,000 salary, that's $2,700/year in free retirement money. This alone often exceeds the lifetime PSLF value at the OTA debt level.

2. **Maximize Cape Fear Valley Health if PSLF debt is high.** If you finished an OTA program with $25,000–$28,000 in debt and your income is $44,000–$48,000, PSLF at Cape Fear Valley Health (501(c)(3)) can save ~$15,000 over 10 years. Not life-changing, but worth pursuing if you'd stay there anyway.

3. **Beware contractor traps at Fort Liberty.** Like with OTs and nurses, some Womack AMC therapy positions are staffed by contractor companies (Leidos, Booz Allen, CACI). **These do NOT qualify for PSLF.** Always verify your W-2 employer is the U.S. Army directly.

4. **Pursue Cumberland County Schools OTA roles.** School-based OTA positions through Cumberland County Schools qualify for PSLF as local government employment. School year schedule + state benefits + PSLF makes this an underrated career path.

5. **OTA-to-OT bridge financial planning.** Fayetteville State University doesn't offer MOT. Closest options: UNC Chapel Hill (highly competitive), East Carolina University (Greenville NC), Methodist University, Lenoir-Rhyne. Plan for $60,000–$90,000 additional debt + relocation.

## Student Loan Strategy

The average OTA graduates with $15,000–$28,000 in student debt. On a $48,000 income:
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$180–$320/month
- SAVE plan payment: ~$130–$180/month
- Aggressive 3-year payoff: ~$500–$800/month
- PSLF at Womack/Cape Fear Valley: $180/month for 10 years = $21,600 paid; remaining forgiven

**The PSLF math at OTA debt levels is marginal but meaningful.** If you're staying in federal/501(c)(3) employment anyway, PSLF saves $5,000–$10,000. Not worth changing careers for, but worth filing Form PSLF annually if you qualify.

## Active Military Spouse Considerations

Many OTAs in Fayetteville are military spouses. Relevant programs:

- **MyCAA (Military Spouse Career Advancement Account)**: Up to $4,000 toward OTA education
- **Public Service Loan Forgiveness via military spouse employment**: If you work for a 501(c)(3) or federal employer, your individual PSLF clock runs independently of your spouse's military service
- **Tricare healthcare coverage**: Often included via spouse's military benefits — major budget savings
- **Free Tax Filing through MilTax**: Use Military OneSource for free filing

## FAQ

**Is OTA a good career for military spouses at Fort Liberty?**
Yes. Federal civilian positions at Womack AMC and the VA prioritize military spouses (Executive Order 13832). Position openings are common. The combination of MyCAA funding for OTA education + EO 13832 hiring priority + Tricare coverage makes this one of the strongest military spouse career paths.

**Does Cape Fear Valley Health employ OTAs?**
Yes. Cape Fear Valley Health employs OTAs in inpatient rehab, outpatient therapy, and home health. Verify your W-2 is Cape Fear Valley Health (501(c)(3)) and not a staffing company.

**Should I pursue an MOT bridge after working as an OTA at Fort Liberty?**
Financially: probably yes if you want long-term career growth. The OT salary at Womack AMC is $25,000+ higher than OTA, and PSLF on combined debt (associate + master's) can be valuable. Time-wise: 2–3 years of MOT plus potential relocation.

**What about Methodist University for OTA-to-OT bridge?**
Methodist University in Fayetteville offers an OTD program. As a private 501(c)(3), it qualifies for PSLF if you're employed there. Tuition is ~$33,000/year — significant additional debt.

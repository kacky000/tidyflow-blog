# Occupational Therapist Budget Template for Tacoma, WA (2026 Guide)

A workable **occupational therapist budget for Tacoma, WA** in 2026 has to navigate three intertwined realities: the cost of living around **Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM)** with its 40,000-soldier community, the **no Washington state income tax** advantage, and a deep PSLF-eligible employer base anchored by **Madigan Army Medical Center**, **MultiCare Health System**, **Virginia Mason Franciscan Health**, and **Western State Hospital**. This guide gives Tacoma OTs salary benchmarks, a sample monthly budget, a JBLM military-spouse playbook, and the complete PSLF employer map for Pierce County.

## 1. Average Occupational Therapist Salary in Tacoma

| Experience Level | Annual Salary (Pierce County BLS) | Hourly |

Combined with **no state income tax** in Washington, an OT at $100,000 nets approximately $6,700/mo — meaningfully more than the same salary in Oregon or California.

## 2. Cost of Living Overview

Tacoma is the affordable end of the I-5 corridor but still well above national median.

- **Housing:** 1BR $1,600-2,000/mo; 2BR $2,000-2,600/mo; 3BR house $2,800-3,500/mo
- **Utilities:** $190-260/mo (heating dominant)
- **Groceries:** $500-580/mo single, $880-1,050 couple
- **Transportation:** Sound Transit usable; gas $160-220/mo, insurance $145-190/mo
- **Healthcare premiums:** $140-240/mo

## 4. Military Spouse Network Strategy

JBLM is one of the largest joint bases in the U.S., and Tacoma OTs who connect with it get systemic advantages:

- **Washington state expedites OT license endorsement** for military spouses (often <30 days vs months elsewhere).
- **EFMP (Exceptional Family Member Program)** at JBLM generates steady pediatric OT referrals into civilian practices.
- **MSEP (Military Spouse Employment Partnership)** maintains an active OT employer list around Lakewood and DuPont.
- **MyCAA** funds (E-1 to E-5 spouses) cover Washington OT renewal CEs and specialty certs (CHT, NDT, SIPT).
- VA Puget Sound (Tacoma & American Lake) hires military-spouse OTs preferentially due to cultural fluency.
- **Western State Hospital** mental-health OT roles often have flexible PCS-aware scheduling.

## 5. PSLF Eligible Employers in Tacoma

Confirmed government and 501(c)(3) nonprofit employers qualifying for **Public Service Loan Forgiveness**:

- **Madigan Army Medical Center** — federal/Army hospital at JBLM, with inpatient rehab, hand therapy, and SCI/TBI programs
- **MultiCare Health System** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit; Tacoma General, Mary Bridge Children's, Good Samaritan
- **Virginia Mason Franciscan Health (CHI Franciscan)** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit including St. Joseph Medical Center
- **Western State Hospital** — Washington state psychiatric hospital (large mental-health OT employer)
- **Tacoma Public Schools** — school-based OT for ~28,000 students
- **VA Puget Sound Health Care System – American Lake Division** — federal employer, large outpatient OT
- **Sea Mar Community Health Centers** — FQHC (NHSC + PSLF stack-eligible)
- **JBLM Civilian OT positions** — federal civilian roles at Madigan rehab clinics

## 6. Savings Strategy for Tacoma OTs

1. **Capture the no-state-tax dividend** by auto-routing 5-7% (what you'd lose in OR or CA) into Roth.
2. **NHSC + PSLF stack** at Sea Mar or similar FQHC — combined this can erase $100k+ in loans.
3. **Build a 6-month emergency fund** ($25-30k) before aggressive investing — Tacoma rent makes job gaps expensive.
4. **HSA before Roth** if you have an HDHP — no WA state tax to recapture means HSA is triple-tax-free with no offset.
5. Compare your trajectory in lower-COL markets like [Occupational Therapist Budget for Killeen, TX](/blog/budget-for-occupational-therapist-in-killeen-tx/) to weigh moves carefully.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: Do school OTs at Tacoma Public Schools qualify for PSLF?**
A: Yes — TPS is a government employer, so direct W-2 employment qualifies. Contract OTs via staffing agencies typically do not.

**Q: Is there an OT signing bonus market in Tacoma?**
A: Hospital roles at Madigan, MultiCare, and Virginia Mason Franciscan often offer $5-20k signing bonuses for CHT, NDT, or neuro specialty certifications.

**Q: Rent or buy in Tacoma as an OT?**
A: Buy only if staying 5+ years and your spouse isn't active-duty PCS-vulnerable. Median Tacoma home is ~$510k; mortgage with 20% down comes in close to rent at current rates.

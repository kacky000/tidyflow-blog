Magee Rehabilitation Hospital, headquartered in Center City Philadelphia, is one of the nation's premier spinal cord injury (SCI) rehabilitation flagships. Founded in 1958 and federally designated as an SCI Model System center, Magee became part of the Jefferson Health system in 2021 and is now formally branded "Magee Rehabilitation Hospital at Jefferson." Because Jefferson Health is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, every clinical role at Magee continues to qualify for Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF). For occupational therapists, physical therapists, respiratory therapists, speech-language pathologists, rehab nurses, and assistants carrying graduate-school debt, Magee is one of the most strategically valuable employers in the Mid-Atlantic — and one of the few remaining nonprofit firewalls against the Encompass Health and Select Medical for-profit chains expanding aggressively across Pennsylvania.

## 30-Second Summary

- Magee Rehabilitation became part of Jefferson Health (a 501(c)(3) nonprofit) in 2021, preserving PSLF eligibility for every clinician on payroll.
- W-2 employer entity is typically "Thomas Jefferson University Hospitals, Inc." or the legacy "Magee Rehabilitation Hospital" tax ID, depending on hire date.
- Main hospital: 96-bed inpatient SCI/TBI/Stroke flagship in Center City Philadelphia.
- Federally funded SCI Model System designation — one of only ~14 in the entire United States.
- Acts as the nonprofit counterweight to Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Greater Philadelphia and Select Medical's growing Mid-Atlantic LTACH footprint.

## Magee Rehabilitation: Structure and History

### A Brief Timeline

- **1958** — Founded as Magee Memorial Hospital for the Disabled in Center City Philadelphia, focused on polio survivors and traumatic injury recovery.
- **2008** — Awarded SCI Model System designation by the National Institute on Disability, Independent Living, and Rehabilitation Research (NIDILRR).
- **2018–2021** — Phased clinical and financial integration with Thomas Jefferson University Hospitals.
- **2021** — Fully integrated and rebranded as "Magee Rehabilitation Hospital at Jefferson," unified under the Jefferson Health 501(c)(3) umbrella.

### Locations Today

- **Main Hospital** — 96-bed inpatient rehabilitation facility on Race Street in Center City Philadelphia.
- **Outpatient Network** — multiple suburban outpatient clinics across Bucks, Montgomery, Delaware, and Chester counties.
- **Riverfront Outpatient Center** — serves the Camden/South Jersey border for cross-state SCI follow-up.

### Specialty Programs

- Spinal Cord Injury (SCI) Center — the federally funded Model System program with prospective research enrollment.
- Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) Center — Commission on Accreditation of Rehabilitation Facilities (CARF) accredited.
- Stroke Recovery Program — full continuum from acute to outpatient.
- Cardiac and Pulmonary Rehab.
- Pediatric Rehab in clinical partnership with Children's Hospital of Philadelphia (CHOP).
- Magee Wheelchair Sports — adaptive athletics, including wheelchair rugby and basketball, run by Certified Therapeutic Recreation Specialists (CTRS).

## Jefferson Health Entity Structure and PSLF Eligibility

Jefferson Health, anchored academically by Thomas Jefferson University, operates a sprawling 501(c)(3) family. Every entity below is nonprofit and PSLF-qualifying:

- Thomas Jefferson University Hospitals, Inc.
- Thomas Jefferson University Hospital (TJUH) — main academic medical center.
- Magee Rehabilitation Hospital.
- Jefferson Methodist Hospital.
- Jefferson Frankford Hospital.
- Jefferson Torresdale Hospital.
- Einstein Healthcare Network (merged with Jefferson in 2021).

For PSLF purposes, what matters is the EIN on the W-2 and the employer's 501(c)(3) status — both of which check out across the Jefferson family. New hires should always confirm their specific entity name on the offer letter before submitting their PSLF Employment Certification Form.

## Career Paths at Magee — Pay Ranges and Responsibilities

Pay bands below reflect the Philadelphia metro market for 2026 and assume full-time inpatient or outpatient rehab roles at Magee.

- Pay: $72,000–$88,000.
- Specialty in SCI and TBI rehab is highly valued; opportunities exist in adaptive equipment, driving rehab, and Model System research.

### Physical Therapist (PT, DPT)
- Pay: $74,000–$90,000.
- Frequent participation in SCI Model System research and gait/locomotor training protocols.

### Occupational Therapy Assistant (OTA)
- Pay: $58,000–$72,000.
- Inpatient SCI/TBI ADL retraining and outpatient orthotic management.

### Physical Therapist Assistant (PTA)
- Pay: $56,000–$70,000.
- Locomotor training, transfer training, and outpatient orthopedic follow-up.

### Speech-Language Pathologist (SLP)
- Pay: $70,000–$86,000.
- Heavy TBI and stroke aphasia caseload plus AAC (augmentative and alternative communication) work with cervical-level SCI.

### Respiratory Therapist (RT)
- Pay: $68,000–$84,000.
- Substantial cervical SCI pulmonary care, ventilator weaning, and tracheostomy management.

### Registered Nurse — CRRN
- Pay: $78,000–$98,000.
- Certified Rehabilitation Registered Nurse (CRRN) certification strongly preferred and partially reimbursed.

### Physiatrist (PM&R MD)
- Pay: $260,000–$360,000.
- Joint appointments with Sidney Kimmel Medical College at Thomas Jefferson University, including residency teaching.

### Rehabilitation Psychologist / Counselor
- Pay: $72,000–$95,000.
- Adjustment-to-disability counseling, pain psychology, and SCI/TBI cognitive rehab.

### Recreation Therapist (CTRS)
- Pay: $52,000–$66,000.
- Magee Wheelchair Sports program, community reintegration, and adaptive recreation.

### Case Manager / Social Worker (LCSW)
- Pay: $62,000–$78,000.
- Discharge planning across SCI, TBI, and stroke caseloads with strong payer-mix complexity.

## Why Magee Is the Mid-Atlantic SCI Stronghold

## PSLF Certification Step-by-Step at Magee

1. Confirm your employer entity on your offer letter — typically "Magee Rehabilitation Hospital" or "Thomas Jefferson University Hospitals, Inc."
2. Use the PSLF Employer Search Tool on studentaid.gov to verify the EIN as nonprofit.
3. Apply for an income-driven repayment (IDR) plan on studentaid.gov as soon as you start.
4. File the PSLF Employment Certification Form at least once per year, and again whenever your employer entity changes (e.g., entity restructuring within Jefferson).
5. After 120 qualifying monthly payments, submit the final PSLF application.

## Real PSLF Math Example: A New Magee OT

## FAQ

**Q: I worked at Magee before the 2021 Jefferson integration. Do those payments still count toward PSLF?**
Yes, as long as you were employed by Magee Rehabilitation Hospital (a 501(c)(3) at that time) and were on a qualifying repayment plan with Direct Loans. Submit an updated Employment Certification Form to reflect both the pre-integration and post-integration W-2 employers; the payments should remain continuous.

**Q: I worked at Einstein Healthcare before Jefferson absorbed it. Does my PSLF count survive the merger?**
In nearly all cases, yes. Einstein Healthcare Network was already a 501(c)(3) before the 2021 Jefferson combination. File a fresh PSLF certification with the new Jefferson entity name to formally bridge your record.

**Q: I want to do SCI specialty at Magee and later transfer to Shepherd or Shirley Ryan AbilityLab. Will my PSLF count carry?**
Yes — all three are 501(c)(3) nonprofits. Your qualifying payments aggregate across any combination of nonprofit and government employers. Just re-certify employment each time you change employers.

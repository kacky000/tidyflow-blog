Birmingham is one of the best PSLF cities for occupational therapists in the entire South — UAB Health alone runs the largest concentration of academic medical center OT positions in Alabama, all PSLF-qualifying. This guide breaks down Birmingham's OT compensation, cost of living, and the UAB dominance that makes it a PSLF magnet.

OTs in the Birmingham-Hoover MSA earn between **$75,000–$95,000 per year**, with a median near $82,000. UAB acute care, neuro rehab, and pediatric OT positions earn at the upper end. School district and outpatient sit at median.

## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Advantage: UAB Health System Dominance

**This is the most important paragraph in this guide.**

UAB (University of Alabama at Birmingham) is a **public state university**, so its health system is fully PSLF-eligible:

- **UAB Hospital** (Level 1 trauma center, neuro rehab, acute care OT)
- **Spain Rehabilitation Center** at UAB (one of the top inpatient rehab facilities in the Southeast — major OT employer)
- **Children's of Alabama** (501(c)(3) nonprofit affiliated with UAB — PSLF-eligible)
- **UAB Highlands**
- **UAB Callahan Eye Hospital**

This is rare. UAB Health + Children's of Alabama gives Birmingham OTs more PSLF-qualifying positions than most Southeastern cities combined.

**For-profit trap**:
- **Grandview Medical Center** — Owned by Community Health Systems (CHS, publicly traded for-profit) → does NOT qualify for PSLF.
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Birmingham** — Encompass Health (formerly HealthSouth) is publicly traded for-profit → does NOT qualify for PSLF.

The Encompass trap is brutal for OTs because Encompass is a major national rehab brand and many OTs assume "rehab hospital" = nonprofit. It's not. Always verify the legal employer entity.

## 5 Money Tips for Occupational Therapists in Birmingham

1. **Target Spain Rehabilitation Center for PSLF + specialty experience.** Spain Rehab at UAB is regionally famous for neuro and spinal cord rehab — strong CV builder AND PSLF-qualifying. Best of both worlds for inpatient-track OTs.

2. **Avoid Encompass Health Rehabilitation as a PSLF strategy.** Same job, opposite tax status. If you're committed to PSLF, going from Spain Rehab to Encompass mid-career stops the qualifying clock entirely.

3. **Live in Avondale or Crestwood for the UAB commute.** Both are 10–15 minutes to UAB campus, under $1,200/month for a 1BR, with restaurants and walkable amenities. Mountain Brook is overpriced relative to UAB commute.

4. **Stack 403(b) + Roth IRA at $82k income.** Birmingham's low rent gives OTs runway to max both. UAB's 403(b) match typically vests at 2 years — don't leave early without confirming forfeitures.

5. **Watch the Brookwood Baptist + Orlando Health story.** Brookwood Baptist Health was acquired by Orlando Health in 2024. PSLF eligibility likely continues but verify with HR. Acquisitions can restructure subsidiary entities silently.

## Student Loan Considerations

OTs typically graduate with $80,000–$140,000 in student debt. On an IDR plan at $82,000 income:
- SAVE plan payment: ~$390–$480/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$900–$1,500/month

PSLF saves OTs in Birmingham **$60,000–$130,000** over 10 years vs standard repayment.

PSLF eligibility in Birmingham:
- **UAB Health System (UAB Hospital, Spain Rehab, etc.)**: Public university — PSLF eligible.
- **Children's of Alabama**: 501(c)(3) — PSLF eligible.
- **Brookwood Baptist Health (now Orlando Health)**: Verify post-acquisition status.
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation of Birmingham**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.
- **Grandview Medical Center (CHS)**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.
- **Birmingham City Schools / Jefferson County Schools**: PSLF eligible.
- **Jefferson County Department of Health / FQHCs**: PSLF eligible.
- **Private OT clinics, DSO chains**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.

## FAQ

**Why is Birmingham especially strong for OT PSLF?**
UAB Health + Spain Rehab + Children's of Alabama form one of the deepest concentrations of nonprofit OT employment in the South. Few metros offer this depth in a single zip-code cluster.

**Does Encompass Health really not qualify for PSLF?**
Correct — Encompass Health is publicly traded (NYSE: EHC), a for-profit company. The "rehab hospital" label confuses many OTs. Always check the W-2 employer tax status, not the building name.

**How does Birmingham compare to Atlanta or Nashville for OTs?**
Birmingham pays less than Atlanta/Nashville but rent is dramatically cheaper. Net savings rate is often better in Birmingham. For PSLF, Birmingham's UAB concentration is comparable to Emory in Atlanta — both excellent.

Tacoma, Washington has one of the **strongest PSLF maps of any mid-sized city in the United States**. Nearly every major hospital is nonprofit, the largest single employer is a federal military base, and the public sector (city, county, state, university) is robust. If you have student debt and you work in Pierce County, your odds of landing a PSLF-eligible job are far higher than the national average.

This guide maps every major employer.

## Why Tacoma Is Unusually PSLF-Friendly

Three structural reasons:

1. **Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM)** is the largest military installation on the US West Coast (~40,000 active-duty plus ~14,000 civilians). Massive federal employment footprint.
2. **MultiCare Health System** (locally founded nonprofit) and **Virginia Mason Franciscan Health / CHI Franciscan** (Catholic nonprofit) together dominate the South Sound healthcare market. There is **no major for-profit hospital chain** operating in Tacoma.
3. **Tacoma Public Schools, Pierce County, City of Tacoma, and the University of Washington Tacoma campus** are all major PSLF-eligible employers.

## ✅ PSLF-Eligible Employers in Tacoma and Pierce County

### Federal (JBLM and military medicine)

- **Madigan Army Medical Center (MAMC)** — Federal Army medical center on JBLM. One of the largest military hospitals in the western US. Hires civilian RNs, NPs, PAs, OTs, PTs, pharmacists, social workers, mental health providers, and clerical staff on the federal GS scale. **PSLF-eligible.**
- **Joint Base Lewis-McChord civilian workforce** — IT, engineering, child & youth services, education, MWR, environmental, contracting. Federal → PSLF-eligible.
- **Veterans Affairs — VA Puget Sound (American Lake VA in Lakewood)** — Federal. PSLF-eligible. Hires across most healthcare and administrative roles.
- **US Postal Service, federal courthouses, IRS, SSA, Customs offices in Pierce County** — Federal → PSLF-eligible.

### Nonprofit Hospital Systems

- **MultiCare Health System** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit, headquartered in Tacoma. Includes Tacoma General Hospital, Mary Bridge Children's Hospital, Allenmore Hospital, Auburn Medical Center, Good Samaritan Hospital (Puyallup), Covington Medical Center, Deaconess Hospital (Spokane). **PSLF-eligible system-wide.** Largest civilian nonprofit employer in the South Sound.
- **Virginia Mason Franciscan Health (CHI Franciscan)** — Catholic 501(c)(3) nonprofit (part of CommonSpirit Health). Includes St. Joseph Medical Center (Tacoma), St. Anthony Hospital (Gig Harbor), St. Clare Hospital (Lakewood), St. Francis Hospital (Federal Way), St. Elizabeth Hospital (Enumclaw). **PSLF-eligible.**
- **Seattle Children's Hospital — South Clinic in Federal Way** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit. PSLF-eligible.

### Public / Government Healthcare

- **Tacoma-Pierce County Health Department** — Public. PSLF-eligible. Public health nurses, epidemiologists, environmental health, WIC, communicable disease.
- **Western State Hospital (Lakewood)** — Washington State psychiatric hospital. PSLF-eligible.

### FQHCs and Community Health Centers

- **Sea Mar Community Health Centers** (Tacoma, Lakewood, Puyallup) — 501(c)(3) FQHC network. PSLF-eligible.
- **Community Health Care (CHC) of Tacoma** — FQHC. PSLF-eligible.
- **Sound** (formerly Sound Mental Health) — 501(c)(3) nonprofit behavioral health, with Pierce County offices. PSLF-eligible.
- **MultiCare Behavioral Health** — Part of MultiCare → PSLF-eligible.

### Public Schools

- **Tacoma Public Schools (TPS)** — Largest district in Pierce County (~29,000 students). Teachers, paraprofessionals, school nurses, counselors, SLPs, OTs, PTs, librarians, custodial, transportation. PSLF-eligible.
- **Bethel School District, Puyallup SD, Franklin Pierce SD, Clover Park SD, University Place SD, Sumner-Bonney Lake SD, Peninsula SD, Gig Harbor schools, Steilacoom SD, Fife SD** — All public → PSLF-eligible.

### Public Higher Education

- **University of Washington Tacoma (UW Tacoma)** — Public university. PSLF-eligible.
- **Tacoma Community College (TCC), Pierce College (Lakewood/Puyallup), Bates Technical College** — Public community/technical colleges. PSLF-eligible.
- **Pacific Lutheran University (PLU)** — 501(c)(3) Lutheran nonprofit. PSLF-eligible.
- **University of Puget Sound** — 501(c)(3) private nonprofit. PSLF-eligible.

### Local Government

- **City of Tacoma, City of Lakewood, City of Puyallup, City of Federal Way, City of Gig Harbor, City of University Place, City of Fife, City of Auburn (Pierce County portion)** — Municipal. PSLF-eligible.
- **Pierce County Government** — Sheriff, courts, public defender, health department, human services, library system. PSLF-eligible.
- **Pierce Transit, Sound Transit** — Public transit. PSLF-eligible.

### Nonprofit Sector

- **MetroParks Tacoma, Tacoma Public Library (part of City), Tacoma Art Museum (501(c)(3)), Point Defiance Zoo (MetroParks/501(c)(3))** — Public or nonprofit. PSLF-eligible (verify each individually).

## ❌ NOT PSLF-Eligible in Tacoma

- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Western Washington** — NYSE: EHC, publicly traded → NOT PSLF.
- **Select Specialty Hospital — Tacoma** — Select Medical, NYSE: SEM → NOT PSLF.
- **DSO dental chains (Aspen Dental, Heartland Dental, Pacific Dental Services, Western Dental)** — for-profit → NOT PSLF.
- **For-profit urgent cares, surgery centers, dialysis centers (DaVita NYSE: DVA, Fresenius NYSE: FMS)** — for-profit → NOT PSLF.
- **Private physician groups, private therapy clinics, private veterinary practices** — for-profit → NOT PSLF.

## ⚠️ Brand-Name Notes

- **"Virginia Mason Franciscan Health"** is a 2021 merger of Virginia Mason (Seattle nonprofit) and CHI Franciscan (Tacoma Catholic nonprofit, part of CommonSpirit). Confusing name; still a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. **PSLF-eligible.**
- **"MultiCare"** sounds corporate but is a genuine 501(c)(3) — locally founded, locally governed. PSLF-eligible.
- **"Sound" / "Sound Mental Health"** rebranded but remains 501(c)(3). PSLF-eligible.
- **"Madigan Army Medical Center"** is a federal Army hospital — civilian employees are federal employees, fully PSLF-eligible.

## FAQ

**Q: I'm active-duty Army at JBLM. Do my deployment years count for PSLF?**
Yes. Active military service is federal employment for PSLF purposes. While on IDR with a $0 qualifying payment (common during low-income early military years), each month still counts toward your 120 qualifying payments. Submit ECF annually.

**Q: MultiCare or Virginia Mason Franciscan — which is the better PSLF employer?**
Both are 501(c)(3) nonprofits → equivalent PSLF status. Pick based on commute, role, schedule, and culture, not PSLF status.

**Q: I work at a dental DSO in Tacoma. Can I move to a PSLF-eligible employer?**
Yes — dental hygienists and dentists at Sea Mar, CHC of Tacoma, the Tacoma-Pierce County Health Department, or any FQHC are PSLF-eligible. FQHCs often layer **HRSA NHSC loan repayment on top of PSLF** for dentists and hygienists in HPSA areas.

## 5 Action Items for Tacoma-Area Workers

1. **MultiCare and Virginia Mason Franciscan are your two nonprofit hospital anchors.** Almost any clinical job in either system qualifies.
2. **Madigan and JBLM civilian jobs are the strongest federal anchor.** Best stability, best PSLF clarity.
3. **Tacoma Public Schools and Pierce County government** are both huge PSLF employers — not just teachers.
4. **Avoid Encompass Health, Select Specialty, and DSO dental chains.** All for-profit, all disqualify PSLF.
5. **Washington has no state income tax.** Combine PSLF with maxing Roth IRA + 457(b) (if at a public employer) — your tax-advantaged retirement compounds during the PSLF window.

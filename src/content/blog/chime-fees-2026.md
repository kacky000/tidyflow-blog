## Where Chime Does Cost Money

No account is completely free in every scenario. These are the real costs you might encounter with Chime.

### Out-of-Network ATM Withdrawals: $2.50

Use an ATM outside the MoneyPass or Visa Plus Alliance network and Chime charges a flat **$2.50**. On top of that, the ATM operator often adds their own surcharge — typically $2 to $3. Combined, one out-of-network withdrawal can run you **$4.50 to $5.50**.

The fix is simple: use the Chime app's ATM locator before you withdraw. The network is large enough that most urban and suburban areas have multiple free options within a short drive.

### Cash Deposits at Retail Locations: Up to $4.95

Chime does not have branches, so depositing cash means visiting a retail partner (Walgreens, CVS, 7-Eleven, etc.) through the Green Dot network. The retail location charges a fee — typically **$3.50 to $4.95 per deposit** — and Chime has no control over it.

If you regularly deposit cash, this fee adds up fast. Depositing cash twice a week at $4.95 each time costs you roughly **$40 per month**. For cash-heavy users, a traditional bank with free branch deposits or a neobank with better cash deposit terms may be a smarter fit.

### Expedited Card Replacement: $5

Lost your debit card? Chime ships a standard replacement for free, but if you need it faster, the rush delivery option costs **$5**. Not a huge deal, but worth knowing before you panic-order.

### International ATM Withdrawals

Chime treats international ATMs the same as out-of-network domestic ones: **$2.50 from Chime** plus whatever the local machine charges. On top of that, Visa's exchange rate applies. Chime does not add a separate foreign transaction fee on purchases, but ATM withdrawals abroad can still be surprisingly expensive once the operator fee and currency conversion stack up.

### Wire Transfers: Not Available

Chime does not support wire transfers — inbound or outbound. This is not a fee; it is a missing feature. If you need to send or receive a wire (for a real estate closing, international payment, etc.), you will need a different account.

## SpotMe Overdraft: Is It Really Free?

SpotMe is one of Chime's flagship features, and it works differently from traditional overdraft protection.

**How it works:** If an eligible debit card purchase or ATM withdrawal would overdraw your account, SpotMe covers it up to your limit — anywhere from **$20 to $200** — with no overdraft fee, no interest, and no late charge. Chime automatically deducts the negative balance from your next deposit.

**Eligibility requirements:**
- You must receive at least **$200 in qualifying direct deposits** per month
- Your SpotMe limit starts small (often $20) and increases over time based on your account activity, deposit history, and spending patterns
- Not every transaction type is covered — recurring bill payments and ACH transfers are generally excluded

**The catch that isn't a catch:** There is no fee. Period. But Chime does prompt you to leave an optional "tip" after SpotMe covers a transaction. You can tip $0 with zero consequences to your account or your SpotMe limit. The tip is genuinely optional — Chime has stated it does not affect your limit — but the prompt exists and some users feel social pressure to leave one.

Compared to traditional banks that charge **$35 per overdraft**, SpotMe is a major upgrade. Even using it a few times a year saves you $100+ in fees you would have paid elsewhere.

## How to Avoid Every Chime Fee

Chime's fee structure is already lean, but you can bring your actual costs to exactly $0 with these habits:

1. **Use the ATM locator.** Open the Chime app before every withdrawal and find a MoneyPass or Visa Plus Alliance ATM. This eliminates the $2.50 out-of-network fee entirely.

2. **Avoid cash deposits.** Use direct deposit, mobile check deposit, or ACH transfer to fund your account instead of walking into a retail location. If you must deposit cash, batch it into fewer, larger deposits to reduce per-transaction fees.

3. **Set up direct deposit.** This unlocks SpotMe (free overdraft coverage), early paycheck access (up to two days early), and ensures you never pay an overdraft fee.

4. **Skip the expedited card replacement.** If you lose your card, order the free standard replacement unless it is truly urgent. Use Chime's virtual card number for online purchases while you wait.

5. **Plan for international travel.** Chime's debit card works abroad with no foreign transaction fee on purchases, but pull cash from ATMs sparingly to avoid the $2.50 + operator fee stack.

6. **Do not tip on SpotMe unless you want to.** The $0 tip option has no penalty. Your SpotMe limit is based on your account history, not your tip amount.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Chime actually free?

For most everyday use, yes. There is no monthly fee, no minimum balance, no overdraft fee with SpotMe, and no charge for in-network ATMs. The only costs you might hit are out-of-network ATM withdrawals ($2.50), cash deposits at retail locations (up to $4.95), and expedited card replacements ($5). All three are avoidable with minor adjustments to your routine.

### Does Chime charge for overdraft?

No. SpotMe covers eligible overdrafts up to $200 with no fee, no interest, and no late charge. You need at least $200 in monthly qualifying direct deposits to activate SpotMe. Chime asks for an optional tip after each SpotMe transaction, but tipping $0 has no consequences.

### Can I use Chime internationally?

Yes. Chime's Visa debit card works for purchases abroad with no foreign transaction fee from Chime (Visa's exchange rate applies). ATM withdrawals outside the US follow the out-of-network rate: $2.50 from Chime plus whatever the local ATM operator charges. Chime does not support international wire transfers.

### Does Chime have hidden fees?

No genuinely hidden fees, but a few that catch people off guard: the out-of-network ATM charge, the cash deposit fee at retail locations, and the expedited card replacement cost. Chime is transparent about all three in their fee schedule — they just do not headline them in the marketing.

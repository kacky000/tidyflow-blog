Raleigh-Durham is one of the strongest PSLF markets in the entire Southeast for healthcare professionals — Duke and UNC are both PSLF-eligible, and the Triangle's tech-fueled wages have pushed up healthcare salaries too. For OTAs, this is the gold standard for student loan strategy. Here's the breakdown.

## Average OTA Salary in Raleigh-Durham

OTAs in the Raleigh-Cary + Durham-Chapel Hill MSAs earn between **$62,000–$78,000 per year**, with a median near $68,000. Duke University Health System and UNC Health both pay at the upper end. School district and outpatient sit near median.

## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Advantage: Duke + UNC + VA + Public Schools

**This is the most important paragraph in this guide.**

Raleigh-Durham has more PSLF-eligible OTA employers than almost any market in the Southeast:

- **Duke University Health System**: 501(c)(3) nonprofit affiliated with Duke University → PSLF-eligible. Includes Duke University Hospital, Duke Raleigh Hospital, Duke Regional Hospital, Duke Children's, Duke Rehabilitation Institute.
- **UNC Health (UNC Health Care)**: Public university system → PSLF-eligible. Includes UNC Medical Center, UNC REX Hospital (Raleigh), UNC Hospitals Hillsborough, UNC Children's. Plus extensive outpatient and rehab network.
- **Durham VA Medical Center**: Federal VA → PSLF-eligible.
- **WakeMed Health & Hospitals**: 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible. Major OTA employer in Raleigh.
- **Wake County Public Schools, Durham Public Schools, Chapel Hill-Carrboro CSD**: Public school districts → PSLF-eligible.
- **TriHealth (smaller outpatient nonprofit network)**: 501(c)(3) → PSLF-eligible.

**For-profit traps:**
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Raleigh**: For-profit (NYSE: EHC) → does NOT qualify.
- **HealthSouth-branded properties (now Encompass)**: For-profit → does NOT qualify.
- **Private OT clinics, DSO-style chains**: For-profit.

Duke + UNC + WakeMed + Durham VA gives the Triangle four major PSLF-qualifying systems in one metro. That's unusually deep.

## 5 Money Tips for OTAs in Raleigh-Durham

1. **Stack PRN across Duke + UNC + WakeMed.** All three are PSLF-eligible. If you can hit 30+ hours/week combined, you keep your PSLF clock running. This is one of the few markets where multi-employer stacking is genuinely strategic.

2. **Duke Rehabilitation Institute is a premier OTA experience builder.** It's a top-ranked rehab facility in the Southeast — strong CV signal AND PSLF-qualifying. Apply early in your career.

3. **Avoid Encompass Health Rehab of Raleigh as a PSLF strategy.** Same OTA caseload as Duke Rehab Institute. Opposite tax status. PSLF foregone over 10 years can be $20,000+.

4. **Live in Cary, Apex, or Garner for rent arbitrage.** All within 20–30 minutes of major Raleigh hospitals. Rent is $200–$400/month cheaper than North Hills or downtown Raleigh.

5. **Maximize retirement during the early years.** Triangle's high job density makes career mobility easy — switching between Duke, UNC, and WakeMed for advancement is common. Contribute to retirement aggressively now while rent is still manageable for an OTA salary.

## Student Loan Considerations

OTAs typically graduate with $20,000–$40,000 in student debt. On an IDR plan at $68,000 income:
- SAVE plan payment: ~$250–$310/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$220–$450/month

PSLF saves OTAs in Raleigh-Durham **$15,000–$30,000** over 10 years. The PSLF environment is so favorable that almost any OTA hospital job qualifies — making job hopping easier than in markets like Pensacola or Mobile.

PSLF eligibility in Raleigh-Durham:
- **Duke University Health System**: 501(c)(3) — PSLF eligible.
- **UNC Health Care**: Public university — PSLF eligible.
- **WakeMed Health & Hospitals**: 501(c)(3) — PSLF eligible.

- **Encompass Health Rehab of Raleigh**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.

## FAQ

**Why is the Triangle especially strong for OTA PSLF?**
Four major PSLF-eligible health systems (Duke, UNC, WakeMed, Durham VA) within a 30-minute radius. Plus public school districts and FQHCs. The job density is unusual for any specialty, let alone OTAs.

**Should I work at UNC or Duke for PSLF?**
Both qualify. Career-wise, UNC may offer more public-health-oriented OT pathways; Duke leans research/specialty. For pure PSLF, either is equivalent. Your specialty interest should drive the choice.

**How does Raleigh-Durham compare to Charlotte for OTAs?**
Raleigh-Durham has more PSLF-eligible options (Duke + UNC + WakeMed + Durham VA) than Charlotte (Advocate/Atrium + Novant). Charlotte pays slightly more but Raleigh-Durham has better PSLF strategic depth.

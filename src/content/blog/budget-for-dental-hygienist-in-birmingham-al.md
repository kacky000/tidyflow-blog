Birmingham is arguably the best PSLF city for dental hygienists in the Deep South — the University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB) health system is one of the largest employer concentrations of PSLF-eligible jobs in any Southern metro. This guide walks through Birmingham's hygienist salary, cost of living, and why UAB Health's dominance matters for your loan forgiveness math.

## Average Dental Hygienist Salary in Birmingham

Dental hygienists in the Birmingham-Hoover MSA earn between **$58,000–$74,000 per year**, with a median near $64,000. UAB-affiliated dental clinics pay at the upper end. Independent private practices in the suburbs (Hoover, Vestavia Hills) sit in the middle.

## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Advantage: UAB Health System Dominance

**This is the most important paragraph in this guide.**

The University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB) is a **public state university**, which means its entire health system qualifies for PSLF:

- **UAB Hospital** (one of the largest academic medical centers in the U.S.)
- **UAB Callahan Eye Hospital**
- **UAB School of Dentistry clinics**
- **UAB Medical West**
- **UAB Highlands**
- **UAB-affiliated faculty practices** (Confirm via W-2; some specialty groups are organized separately.)

This dominance is rare. In most cities, hygienists must hunt for PSLF-eligible employers. In Birmingham, UAB alone offers thousands of nonprofit/public-sector dental jobs.

**For-profit warning**: Brookwood Baptist Health (now part of Orlando Health, a 501(c)(3)) is a more complex case after recent ownership changes — verify status with HR. Grandview Medical Center is owned by Community Health Systems (for-profit) → does NOT qualify for PSLF.

## 5 Money Tips for Dental Hygienists in Birmingham

1. **Target UAB Health early in your career.** If you have student loans and want PSLF, UAB's hiring pipeline is the single best option in Alabama. Their dental school clinics, hospital dental services, and oral surgery groups all qualify.

2. **Watch the Brookwood Baptist Health acquisition story.** Brookwood Baptist Health was acquired by Orlando Health (a 501(c)(3) nonprofit) in 2024. Your PSLF eligibility likely continues — but verify with HR annually using Form PSLF.

3. **Avoid Grandview if you need PSLF.** Grandview Medical Center is owned by Community Health Systems, which is a publicly traded for-profit hospital company. Working there does not qualify for PSLF, even if the work is identical to UAB.

4. **Live in Avondale or Highland Park for the best cost-to-commute ratio.** Mountain Brook is beautiful but expensive. Avondale offers urban amenities under $1,200/month for a 1BR and is a 10-minute drive to UAB.

5. **Build a 4-month emergency fund.** Birmingham's dental market has DSO consolidation pressure (Heartland, Aspen, Smile Brands). Private practices may close or sell with little notice. A buffer matters.

## Student Loan Considerations

The average dental hygienist graduates with $20,000–$45,000 in student debt. On an IDR plan at $64,000 income:
- SAVE plan payment: ~$230–$280/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$260–$500/month

PSLF eligibility in Birmingham:
- **UAB Health System**: Public university — PSLF eligible (the dominant option here).
- **Brookwood Baptist Health**: Now Orlando Health (nonprofit) — likely eligible post-acquisition, but verify.
- **Grandview Medical Center**: For-profit (CHS) — does NOT qualify.
- **Children's of Alabama**: 501(c)(3) — PSLF eligible.
- **Jefferson County Department of Health / FQHCs**: PSLF eligible.
- **Private dental practices, DSOs**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.

## FAQ

**Why is Birmingham unusually good for PSLF?**
Because UAB is a major public academic medical center with thousands of clinical positions. Most cities don't have one dominant public health system — Birmingham does.

**Does Brookwood Baptist still qualify for PSLF after the Orlando Health acquisition?**
Likely yes — Orlando Health is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. But always verify with HR using Form PSLF annually. Acquisitions can restructure subsidiary entities, and your W-2 employer determines eligibility.

**How does Birmingham compare to other Alabama metros for dental hygienists?**
Birmingham has more PSLF-eligible jobs than Mobile or Huntsville thanks to UAB. Salary is comparable to Huntsville but housing is cheaper.

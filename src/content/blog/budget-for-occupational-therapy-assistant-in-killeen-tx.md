# OTA Salary & Budget in Killeen TX 2026: Fort Hood + PSLF-Eligible Employers

If you're an **occupational therapy assistant in Killeen Fort Hood** area — or you're a military spouse weighing OTA as a PCS-friendly career — Killeen is one of the most underrated PSLF cities in the country. **OTA salary in Killeen** sits in the mid-$50s to low-$60s, but the cost of living is so low that aggressive loan payoff (or PSLF forgiveness) is genuinely achievable here. This guide breaks down exactly which employers qualify, where the traps are, and how a real OTA budget looks in 2026.

## Top PSLF-Eligible Employers Near Fort Hood

Public Service Loan Forgiveness wipes the remaining balance after 120 qualifying payments at a 501(c)(3) or government employer. These are the safest bets in the Killeen/Temple corridor:

### 1. CRDAMC — Carl R. Darnall Army Medical Center (Federal Government)
The on-post Army hospital. As a **civilian Department of the Army (DA) employee**, your W-2 is federal government → automatic PSLF qualifying. Important: contractor positions (Leidos, MedPro) do **not** count, even if you work the same shift in the same building. Always confirm your hire is GS or Title 38 civilian, not contracted.

### 2. Baylor Scott & White Memorial Medical Center — Temple (501(c)(3))
30 minutes north in Temple. Largest non-profit health system in Central Texas. Strong rehab and inpatient OT/OTA pipeline. Confirmed 501(c)(3) — full PSLF qualifying.

### 3. Seton Medical Center Harker Heights (501(c)(3) — Ascension)
Right next door to Killeen. Part of Ascension's non-profit network. Smaller acute-care facility, fewer OTA roles but solid PSLF qualifying employer.

### 4. Bell County MHMR (Government — Mental Health/Mental Retardation Authority)
County government entity serving developmental disabilities and behavioral health. OTA roles exist in adult day programs and pediatric early-intervention. Government employer → PSLF qualifying.

### 5. Killeen ISD / Copperas Cove ISD (Government — School OT)
School-based OTA positions through the ISDs are public-school government roles, fully PSLF eligible. Pay is lower (~$50-$55K) but you get summers, pension contributions, and the cleanest PSLF paperwork in town.

## Acquisition Trap Warning

Texas is a hot market for private equity rehab rollups, and Killeen is no exception. Watch out for:

- **AdventHealth Central Texas** — Technically 501(c)(3), but Adventist Health System's PSLF qualification has been challenged in cases where teaching/research mission isn't clearly documented. Get written PSLF certification before signing.
- **HCA Healthcare facilities** (including Round Rock, ~60 min south) — **For-profit**. Never qualifies for PSLF regardless of how the recruiter spins it.
- **Encompass Health, Select Medical, Kindred** — Travel and contract OTA roles in the region. **For-profit specialty rehab chains.** Same warning.
- **Staffing agencies** (Aureus, AMN, Jackson) — Even when placing you at a 501(c)(3) hospital, **your W-2 is from the staffing firm = for-profit = no PSLF credit.** This is the #1 trap for OTAs trying to forgive loans.

**Rule of thumb:** If the recruiter won't sign the PSLF Employment Certification Form (ECF), walk away.

## Military Spouse OTA Career Path

Fort Hood is one of the largest Army installations in the country, which makes Killeen uniquely valuable for **military spouse OTA careers**:

- **MyCAA Scholarship** — Up to $4,000 for spouses of E-1 to E-6, W-1 to W-2, O-1 to O-3. Covers OTA program tuition at Central Texas College (CTC) and Temple College. CTC's OTA program is specifically structured around PCS timelines.
- **BAH stack** — If your service member is E-5 with dependents, BAH (~$1,650) covers rent entirely. Your OTA income becomes 100% discretionary/savings.
- **PCS portability** — OTA licensure compacts now cover most CONUS states. Your next duty station won't reset your career.
- **TRICARE eliminates the #1 OTA budget killer** — healthcare premiums. Your civilian peers spend $400-$600/mo. You spend $0.
- **VA Vocational Rehabilitation (Chapter 31)** — If your spouse has a service-connected disability, you may also access Chapter 35 dependent education benefits.

This is why so many military spouses end up as OTAs: the math is overwhelming.

## PSLF Forgiveness Math (Killeen Example)

Assume $45,000 in loans at 6.8%, starting OTA salary $58,000, joining CRDAMC or Baylor Scott & White immediately after graduation:

- **SAVE/IBR payment Year 1:** ~$220/mo
- **Total paid over 120 months:** ~$36,000 (payments rise with income)
- **Remaining balance forgiven at month 120:** ~$32,000 (interest accrual)
- **Effective interest rate:** ~3% after forgiveness
- **Time horizon:** 10 years from first qualifying payment

Compare to standard 10-year repayment ($518/mo for 120 months = $62,160 total). PSLF saves you ~$26,000 in real dollars in Killeen.

## FAQs

**Q: Can I work at CRDAMC as a contractor and still get PSLF?**
A: No. PSLF requires direct employment by a qualifying employer. Contractors are paid by the contracting company (Leidos, etc.), which is for-profit. Apply for the GS civilian role directly through USAJOBS.

**Q: Is the OTA program at Central Texas College PSLF-strategy compatible?**
A: Yes. CTC is a public community college, so the program itself is affordable (~$8K total), and graduates typically have under $15K in federal loans — making even partial PSLF a strong value.

**Q: What if my spouse PCSes mid-PSLF?**
A: PSLF travels with you. As long as your next employer is also 501(c)(3) or government, your 120-payment count continues. Use the [PSLF Help Tool](https://studentaid.gov/pslf/) to recertify after every move.

San Diego has one of the densest concentrations of PSLF-eligible employers in the western United States. Three major nonprofit health systems, two federal hospitals, the UC system, plus the largest naval installation on the West Coast — but the city also has dozens of for-profit traps that look nonprofit at first glance. This guide is the complete PSLF eligibility map.

## How PSLF Eligibility Works in San Diego

For PSLF eligibility, your **W-2 employer** must be one of:

- **Federal, state, or local government** (any branch)
- **501(c)(3) nonprofit** (verifiable on IRS exempt org search)
- **Public university or college**

For-profit employers do NOT qualify even if they contract with qualifying institutions. Job title and work location are irrelevant — only the legal employer on your W-2 matters.

## PSLF Verification Checklist for San Diego Job Seekers

1. **Check the W-2 employer name on your offer letter**, not the hospital brand on the building.
2. **Look up the EIN on the [IRS Exempt Organizations Search](https://apps.irs.gov/app/eos/)**. If your employer is a 501(c)(3), it will appear.
3. **Submit Form PSLF (Employment Certification Form) annually** to MOHELA. This tracks qualifying payments.
4. **Verify each time you change employers**, even within the same hospital system. Some hospital subsidiaries are for-profit (e.g., physician groups leasing space at nonprofit hospitals).
5. **For Naval Medical Center San Diego civilian roles**: SF-50 confirms federal employment. For active duty: military service automatically qualifies.

## Common Mistakes by San Diego PSLF Pursuers

1. **Assuming "UCSD" is the same as UC San Diego Health Physicians.** UC San Diego Health Physicians is a 501(c)(3) academic medical group. But contracted physicians at UCSD facilities through outside groups may not be UCSD employees.

2. **Assuming Sharp Coronado / Sharp Memorial / Sharp Chula Vista are all the same EIN.** Sharp HealthCare is one parent 501(c)(3), but verify the actual employer entity on your W-2. Usually all qualify, but check the specific entity.

3. **Confusing Kaiser Permanente of Southern California with Kaiser Foundation Hospitals.** Both are 501(c)(3) but they're separate legal entities. Verify W-2 EIN.

4. **Joining Alvarado or Paradise Valley because the hospital looks similar to Sharp or Scripps.** Both Alvarado and Paradise Valley are Prime Healthcare for-profit. The Prime Healthcare brand is sometimes hidden in HR communications.

5. **Not re-certifying after promotion or department change.** Even within UC San Diego Health, switching from a research grant role to a clinical role may change your funding/employment entity. Re-certify Form PSLF after every HR system change.

## Strategic Advice for Maximizing PSLF in San Diego

- **For maximum PSLF + benefits**: Naval Medical Center San Diego (civilian GS positions) or VA San Diego Healthcare System. Federal pay + TSP automatic 4% + immediate PSLF eligibility.
- **For private-sector PSLF**: UC San Diego Health, Sharp HealthCare, Scripps Health, Kaiser Permanente, Rady Children's. All qualifying 501(c)(3) or public systems.
- **For nonprofit social services/teaching**: City of San Diego, County of San Diego, San Diego Unified School District. Pay is lower but benefits and pension are strong.
- **Avoid Prime Healthcare hospitals (Alvarado, Paradise Valley) for PSLF**. They sometimes recruit nurses with signing bonuses but the bonus is dwarfed by lifetime PSLF discharge.

## FAQ

**Does Naval Medical Center San Diego qualify for PSLF as a contractor?**
**No.** Contractor positions at NMCSD (Navy contractors employed by third-party staffing firms) do NOT qualify. Only direct federal civilian (GS) or active duty positions qualify. Verify your W-2 employer is the Department of Defense / Navy.

**Is Encompass Health Rehab San Diego PSLF-eligible since they take Medicare patients?**
**No.** Encompass Health Corporation is publicly traded for-profit (NYSE: EHC). Medicare participation does not affect PSLF eligibility — only your employer's tax status matters.

**Can I transfer from Sharp to Scripps without losing PSLF time?**
**Yes** — both are 501(c)(3). Your accumulated qualifying months transfer. Submit a new Form PSLF after the move to confirm.

## 5 Tips for Greenville OTs

1. **Prisma Health is a major PSLF employer.** Prisma Health Upstate (formerly Greenville Health System) is a nonprofit health system. OTs employed there qualify for [PSLF](https://gettidyflow.com/blog/pslf-for-nurses-complete-guide). With typical OT debt of $80K–$130K, this can mean $40,000–$80,000 forgiven tax-free.

2. **Bon Secours is another nonprofit option.** Bon Secours St. Francis Health System in Greenville is also nonprofit, offering another PSLF-qualifying employer for OTs.

3. **School district OT contracts.** Greenville County Schools is one of the largest school districts in SC. School-based OTs are government employees and qualify for PSLF while typically receiving strong benefit packages.

4. **Per-diem rates are rising.** Greenville's OT shortage means per-diem hourly rates of $40–$55 are available through staffing agencies. Adding even one per-diem shift/week ($320–$440) meaningfully accelerates debt payoff.

5. **Budget for SC state taxes early.** Unlike Florida neighbors, SC's 5.5% state tax reduces take-home by $300+ monthly. Build this into your budget from day one rather than discovering it at tax season.

## FAQ

**Are there good OT jobs in Greenville without hospital systems?**
Yes — outpatient clinics, pediatric therapy centers, and home health agencies are abundant. However, these are typically for-profit, so PSLF doesn't apply.

**What's driving OT demand in Greenville?**
Population growth from retirees (who need more OT services), BMW and tech sector expansion, and general healthcare demand growth. The Greenville metro added 15,000+ residents in 2024 alone.

**Is Greenville affordable on an OT salary?**
Comfortably. A single OT at $76K can pay rent, loans, and save meaningfully. Couples with dual OT incomes can aggressively build wealth in Greenville's housing market.

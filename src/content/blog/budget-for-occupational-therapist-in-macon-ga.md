Macon is an underrated occupational therapist market. The cost of living is among the lowest in Georgia, while OT salaries are surprisingly competitive — and Atrium Health Navicent's recent merger with Advocate Health makes the local 501(c)(3) network even larger. This guide covers salary, cost of living, and a Macon-specific PSLF map.

## Average Occupational Therapist Salary in Macon

Macon and Bibb County OT pay runs **$70,000–$88,000 per year**, with a median near $78,000. Slightly below Atlanta but the cost-to-pay ratio is one of the strongest in Georgia.

## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Distinction in Macon

Macon's PSLF map is small but has changed substantially in recent years:

- **Atrium Health Navicent — The Medical Center** (Macon downtown) — 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible. **Note**: Atrium Health merged with Advocate Aurora in 2022 to form Advocate Health. Same parent 501(c)(3) — both qualify for PSLF.
- **Atrium Health Navicent Children's Hospital, Atrium Health Navicent Rehabilitation Hospital** — 501(c)(3) → PSLF-eligible.
- **Mercer University Medical Center / Mercer University School of Medicine** — Mercer is a 501(c)(3) private nonprofit university → PSLF-eligible.
- **Carl Vinson VA Medical Center** (Dublin GA, ~50 miles east) — Federal → PSLF-eligible.
- **Houston Healthcare** (Warner Robins) — Public hospital authority → PSLF-eligible.
- **Coliseum Medical Centers, Coliseum Northside Hospital** — Owned by **HCA Healthcare → for-profit, NOT PSLF-eligible.**
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Central Georgia (Macon)** — Publicly traded for-profit → NOT PSLF-eligible.

**Big trap for rehab OTs**: Encompass Health's Macon rehab hospital looks like the obvious rehab placement, but it's publicly traded for-profit. Atrium Health Navicent Rehabilitation Hospital (the nonprofit alternative) is just minutes away.

## 5 Money Tips for OTs in Macon

1. **Confirm Atrium Navicent EIN after Advocate merger.** The 2022 Advocate Health merger may have changed employer entity names. Even if you've been employed continuously at "Navicent" for years, recertify Form PSLF after any HR/payroll system update.

2. **Choose Atrium Navicent Rehab over Encompass Health Central Georgia.** Both serve similar rehab populations. Atrium Navicent is 501(c)(3) → PSLF-eligible. Encompass Health is for-profit → not eligible. The Encompass signing bonus is dwarfed by 10-year PSLF discharge on $100k+ debt.

3. **Take advantage of Macon's housing cost.** Rent at 24% of net is exceptional for a Southeastern OT. Push retirement contributions to 12%+ of gross. With state-tax saving + low rent, Macon OTs can save more in absolute dollars than Nashville OTs at higher salary.

4. **Avoid Coliseum.** Coliseum Medical Centers is HCA's Macon brand. The recruiting language is general "healthcare" rather than "HCA," which trips up applicants. Always check the W-2 EIN on offers.

5. **Carl Vinson VA Medical Center (Dublin) is a federal alternative.** 50 miles east of Macon, in Dublin GA. Federal salary + TSP 4% automatic match. Many Macon residents commute or move to Dublin for the federal benefits. Strong PSLF play if you can tolerate the commute or relocate.

## Student Loan Considerations

Most Macon-area OTs trained at Mercer University DPT/MOT or other Georgia programs. Typical debt: **$80,000–$140,000**.

On IDR at $78,000 income (GA):
- SAVE plan payment: ~$330–$470/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$900–$1,500/month

10-year PSLF math at Macon median:
- Average debt: $110,000
- 120 SAVE payments at ~$400/month = $48,000 paid in
- PSLF discharges remaining ~$60,000+ accrued interest
- **Net benefit: $70,000–$120,000 over a career**

PSLF is highly impactful for Macon OTs with typical debt loads. The combination of low cost of living + PSLF discharge can produce one of the best net financial outcomes in the Southeast.

## FAQ

**Is Macon a strong PSLF market for OTs?**
Yes — Atrium Health Navicent is the dominant employer and is 501(c)(3). Mercer University offers an alternative. Carl Vinson VA Medical Center (Dublin) is a federal option within commute. The low cost of living makes the PSLF + savings math exceptionally strong.

**Why doesn't Coliseum Medical Centers qualify for PSLF?**
Coliseum is owned by HCA Healthcare, publicly traded for-profit (NYSE: HCA). Hospital tax status determines PSLF eligibility, not the brand or quality of care.

**Atrium Health vs Atrium Health Navicent — same EIN?**
Atrium Health Navicent is the Macon-area division of Atrium Health. After the 2022 Advocate Health merger, the parent is Advocate Health (501(c)(3)). All Atrium Health Navicent W-2s should qualify for PSLF, but always verify EIN.

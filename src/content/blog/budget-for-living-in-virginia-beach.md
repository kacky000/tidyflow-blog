# How to Budget for Living in Virginia Beach

**Budgeting for life in Virginia Beach** means planning for a coastal city that's significantly more affordable than other beach destinations on the East Coast. Virginia Beach is the largest city in Virginia by population, home to one of the largest US Navy and military concentrations in the world, and offers a quality of life that competes with cities twice its cost.

Virginia Beach's cost of living sits roughly 5-10% above the national average — reasonable for a coastal city, and notably cheaper than comparable beach cities like Miami, San Diego, or Charleston.

## Money-Saving Tips Specific to Virginia Beach

**1. Take full advantage of military discounts.** NAS Oceana and Naval Station Norfolk are nearby. Even civilian residents benefit indirectly — businesses across Virginia Beach offer competitive prices to stay attractive to the large military population. If you're military, access to commissary and exchange shopping saves hundreds per month on groceries and goods.

**2. Plan around tourist season.** Summer brings tourist pricing to restaurants and activities near the boardwalk. Locals eat and entertain in Kempsville, Great Neck, or the Northwest section to avoid inflated prices. Reverse in winter: oceanfront restaurants dramatically cut prices off-season.

**3. Use Bayport Credit Union or Navy Federal.** Both are headquartered in the Hampton Roads area and offer excellent rates on auto loans, mortgages, and credit products. For military members, Navy Federal is hard to beat.

**4. No car, no problem in some areas.** Virginia Beach has Hampton Roads Transit (HRT) buses and a beachfront Bayfront Connector. If you live near Town Center or the oceanfront, you can reduce car dependence significantly.

**5. Fish and beach for free.** Virginia Beach's oceanfront is free to access year-round. Pier fishing and crabbing are popular free activities — locals catch their own seafood regularly, cutting grocery costs.

## FAQ

### Is Virginia Beach expensive to live in?
It's moderate — more expensive than the Midwest but significantly cheaper than comparable coastal cities. Think of it as a coastal lifestyle at inland prices.

### What salary do you need to live comfortably in Virginia Beach?
A gross salary of $55,000-$70,000/year is comfortable for a single person. Military BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) covers housing costs for most service members stationed here.

### Is Virginia Beach good for young professionals?
Yes — Town Center offers a modern urban core with good restaurants and walkability. The job market is anchored by defense and government contracting, tech, and healthcare, offering solid opportunities.

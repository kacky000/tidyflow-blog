# OTA Salary & Budget in Tacoma WA 2026: JBLM + PSLF-Eligible Employers

If you're an **occupational therapy assistant in Tacoma JBLM** corridor — or a military spouse stationed at Joint Base Lewis-McChord weighing OTA as a portable career — Tacoma is quietly one of the strongest PSLF markets on the West Coast. **OTA salary in Tacoma** runs in the low-to-mid $70s, Washington has **no state income tax**, and you can stack PSLF with the WA State Loan Repayment Program (WSLRP). This guide breaks down exactly which employers qualify, the for-profit traps to avoid, and what a real Tacoma OTA budget looks like in 2026.

## Top PSLF-Eligible Employers Near JBLM

PSLF wipes the remaining federal student loan balance after 120 qualifying payments at a 501(c)(3) or government employer. Tacoma has one of the densest concentrations of qualifying employers in the Pacific Northwest:

### 1. Madigan Army Medical Center — JBLM (Federal Government)
On-post Level II trauma center. As a **civilian Department of the Army (DA) employee** (GS-grade or Title 38), you're a federal employee → automatic PSLF qualifying. Madigan has both inpatient rehab and outpatient OT clinics. Caution: contractor placements (Leidos, Magellan) do **not** qualify — apply through USAJOBS for civilian roles directly.

### 2. MultiCare Health System (501(c)(3))
Tacoma's largest non-profit health system. Covers Tacoma General, Allenmore, Auburn Medical Center, and the standout **Mary Bridge Children's Hospital** — the only dedicated pediatric hospital south of Seattle. Mary Bridge is the most coveted new-grad OTA destination in the region for peds rehab.

### 3. CHI Franciscan / St. Joseph Medical Center Tacoma (501(c)(3) — CommonSpirit)
Catholic non-profit system, part of CommonSpirit Health. Strong inpatient rehab unit and home health OTA division. Confirmed 501(c)(3) — full PSLF qualifying.

### 4. Western State Hospital — Lakewood (Washington State Government)
State psychiatric hospital. OTA roles in adult forensic and civil commitment wards. Government employer → PSLF qualifying. Higher complexity but excellent pension and PSLF stack.

### 5. Pierce County Human Services / Tacoma Public Schools (Government)
County and school district OTA roles. Lower base pay (~$58-$65K) but cleanest PSLF documentation in the region plus state pension (PERS/SERS).

## Acquisition Trap Warning

The PNW rehab market has been heavily consolidated by for-profit chains. Avoid these for PSLF purposes:

- **HCA Capital Medical Center** (Olympia, ~30 min south) — **For-profit HCA Healthcare.** Never PSLF-eligible regardless of recruiter claims.
- **Select Medical Northwest Specialty Hospital** — **For-profit** long-term acute care. Common new-grad destination because they hire aggressively. Wrong move if PSLF is your plan.
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital** (formerly HealthSouth) — **For-profit** inpatient rehab chain. Disqualifying.
- **Staffing agencies** (Aureus, AMN, Jackson, Cross Country) — Even when placing you at MultiCare or CHI Franciscan, **your W-2 is from the staffing firm = for-profit = no PSLF credit.**
- **Recent acquisition risk:** Watch for private equity announcements involving any "non-profit" you join. PSLF qualification ends the day the entity converts.

**Rule of thumb:** Before you sign, ask HR to sign the PSLF Employment Certification Form (ECF). If they hesitate, the answer is no.

## Military Spouse OTA Career Path

JBLM is the largest military installation on the West Coast — over 40,000 service members plus families. That makes Tacoma uniquely strong for **military spouse OTA careers**:

- **MyCAA Scholarship** — Up to $4,000 for spouses of E-1 to E-6, W-1 to W-2, O-1 to O-3. Pierce College and Bates Technical College both run OTA programs structured around PCS-friendly enrollment.
- **BAH stack** — E-5 with dependents at JBLM receives ~$2,400/mo BAH, which **covers a Tacoma 1BR with $600 left over**. Your OTA income becomes nearly 100% discretionary or savings.
- **WSLRP (Washington State Loan Repayment Program)** — Up to $75,000 over 5 years for healthcare workers in shortage areas. **Stackable with PSLF**, meaning you can reduce principal aggressively while still building toward 120 qualifying payments.
- **TRICARE** eliminates healthcare premiums (~$400-$600/mo savings vs. civilian peers).
- **OT Licensure Compact** — Washington joined the OT Compact, meaning your next PCS doesn't require a brand-new license application in most CONUS states.
- **VA Chapter 35 / Chapter 31** — If your service member has a service-connected disability, additional dependent education benefits apply.

Few civilian career paths offer this stacking potential — it's why military spouse OTAs in Tacoma routinely hit a 50%+ savings rate.

## PSLF Forgiveness Math (Tacoma Example)

Assume $48,000 in loans at 6.8%, starting OTA salary $70,000, joining Madigan (civilian) or MultiCare immediately after graduation:

- **SAVE/IBR payment Year 1:** ~$310/mo
- **Total paid over 120 months:** ~$46,000 (payments rise with income)
- **Remaining balance forgiven at month 120:** ~$28,000 (interest accrual)
- **Effective interest rate:** ~3.5% after forgiveness
- **WSLRP stack bonus:** Up to $75K in additional principal reduction over 5 years if you qualify for a shortage-area placement

Compare to standard 10-year repayment ($552/mo × 120 = $66,240). PSLF + WSLRP stacking saves you **$40,000+** in Tacoma — among the best OTA loan outcomes in the country.

## FAQs

**Q: Can I work at Madigan as a contractor and still get PSLF?**
A: No. PSLF requires direct W-2 employment by a qualifying employer. Madigan contractor placements are paid by for-profit defense contractors. Apply through USAJOBS for the civilian DA position.

**Q: Is Mary Bridge Children's a good first OTA job?**
A: Yes — both clinically and financially. MultiCare is 501(c)(3) (PSLF-qualifying), Mary Bridge is the regional pediatric specialty hub, and new-grad OTA mentorship is among the strongest in the PNW.

**Q: Can I stack PSLF with WSLRP without losing forgiveness?**
A: Yes, but with nuance. WSLRP payments reduce your principal, which means less is forgiven at month 120. The math still favors stacking — you pay less interest along the way. Confirm WSLRP eligibility through the WA State Health Care Authority before applying.

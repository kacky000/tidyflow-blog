**PSLF for dietitians** is one of the most overlooked — and most misunderstood — forgiveness pathways in healthcare. Since the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics moved RD/RDN credentialing to a master's-level requirement in 2024, average debt loads have climbed to **$50,000–$90,000**, while the median Registered Dietitian salary still sits at just **$66,000–$72,000**. That math makes **RD PSLF** financially decisive — a successful 120-payment run can wipe out **$25,000–$60,000**. But there's a brutal trap most RDs walk into: working inside a nonprofit hospital while their W-2 is issued by **Aramark, Sodexo, Compass Group, or Morrison Healthcare** — all for-profit food service contractors that **do not qualify** for Public Service Loan Forgiveness.

## 30-Second Summary

- RDs qualify for PSLF if employed by a **government agency or 501(c)(3) nonprofit**.
- The biggest trap: working physically inside a nonprofit hospital but receiving a W-2 from **Aramark Healthcare, Sodexo, or Compass/Morrison** — ❌ not eligible.
- ✅ Eligible: **VA medical centers, county WIC clinics, K-12 school nutrition (direct district hire), county public health, FQHCs, Indian Health Service**.
- ❌ Not eligible: **Private practice LLCs, MLM nutrition coaches (Beachbody, Isagenix), telehealth contractors (Nourish, Berry Street), HCA/Tenet for-profit hospitals**.

## Specialty Track Breakdown

### Clinical RD at 501(c)(3) Hospital — ✅ Highest PSLF rate, BUT...

Working at a nonprofit hospital does not automatically mean PSLF eligibility. The decisive question is: **who issues your W-2?** Major academic systems like **Cleveland Clinic, Mayo Clinic, Johns Hopkins, and Kaiser Permanente** typically employ in-house clinical RDs directly under the nonprofit hospital entity — these positions qualify. Mid-sized and smaller nonprofit hospitals, however, frequently outsource the entire Nutrition & Food Services department to Aramark, Sodexo, or Compass. Even if your badge says "Mercy Hospital Dietitian," your W-2 may say "Aramark Healthcare Services LLC." That single line determines whether you get $40,000 forgiven — or nothing.

### VA Dietitian — ✅ Highest PSLF rate

VA Nutrition and Food Services hires clinical dietitians, diabetes educators, and TPN specialists as **federal employees**. PSLF is automatic when paired with an IDR plan, and you also gain access to the **federal HRSA loan repayment programs** stackable on top.

### WIC RD — ✅ Highest PSLF rate

County health department WIC programs employ RDs directly as government workers. Salaries are lower ($55,000–$65,000), but the **government employer status is rock-solid** and the work-life balance is exceptional. WIC remains one of the most reliable PSLF pipelines for early-career RDs.

### School Nutrition Director (K-12) — ✅ if direct district employee

A K-12 School Nutrition role qualifies **only if the school district issues your W-2 directly**. If the district has outsourced food service to **Chartwells K-12, Sodexo Magic, or Aramark Education**, the same role becomes ❌. Even a "Director of Child Nutrition Services" title can be misleading — always check the W-2 entity, not the job title.

### Correctional Facility RD — ✅ if government, ❌ if contractor

The **Federal Bureau of Prisons, state DOC, and county jail direct hires** qualify. But the majority of county jails outsource food service to **Aramark Correctional Services or Trinity Services Group** — both for-profit and ❌.

### Private Practice / Telehealth — ❌ Almost never qualifies

LLCs, sole proprietorships, and 1099 contracts cannot qualify for PSLF, period. Telehealth platforms like Nourish, Berry Street, and Foodsmart classify RDs as contractors or for-profit W-2 employees — none qualify.

## The Aramark / Sodexo / Compass Trap

Roughly **70% of U.S. hospital food service operations** are run by three for-profit giants: **Aramark, Sodexo, and Compass Group (which owns Morrison Healthcare and Crothall Healthcare)**. This means a majority of "hospital RD" jobs posted on nonprofit hospital websites are actually staffed by contractor W-2s.

Common warning signs:

- The job listing lives on a nonprofit hospital site but the application redirects to **careers.aramark.com**, **sodexousa.com/careers**, or **compass-usa.com**.
- The recruiter is an Aramark or Sodexo employee.
- The benefits packet (401k, PTO, health insurance) is branded Aramark/Sodexo, not the hospital.

**How to verify before accepting an offer:**

1. Ask explicitly: "Will my W-2 be issued by [Hospital Name] or by a food service contractor?"
2. Run the employer's EIN through the **PSLF Employer Search Tool** at StudentAid.gov.
3. Known disqualifying EINs include **Aramark Services (23-1988677)** and **Sodexo Operations LLC (13-3017381)**.

If you discover post-hire that you're a contractor employee, your only paths to PSLF are: **transfer to an in-house hospital position, move to a VA or government role, or join an FQHC**.

## IDR Math for RDs

- **Debt range** (post-2024 MS requirement): $50,000–$90,000
- **Median salary**: $66,000–$72,000
- **Standard 10-year repayment**: $550–$1,000/month
- **IDR (PAYE / IBR / SAVE replacement)**: $250–$450/month
- **10-year PSLF forgiveness**: **$30,000–$60,000 wiped out**

For an RD earning $68,000 with $75,000 in loans, PSLF + IDR saves roughly **$45,000 in total repayment** versus the standard plan.

## 5 Common RD PSLF Traps

1. **The Aramark/Sodexo "in-hospital" trap** — by far the most common. The hospital is nonprofit; your W-2 is for-profit.
2. **School district contractor trap** — Chartwells K-12, Aramark Education, and Sodexo Magic operate thousands of district cafeterias.
3. **Dialysis clinic trap** — DaVita and U.S. Renal Care employ many renal RDs; both are publicly traded and ❌.
4. **MLM nutrition side gig trap** — Beachbody, Isagenix, AdvoCare "coach" income is irrelevant for PSLF if your day-job W-2 is also for-profit.
5. **Telehealth platform trap** — Nourish, Berry Street, and Foodsmart RDs are typically 1099 contractors or for-profit W-2s. Convenient work, zero PSLF.

## Step-by-Step PSLF for RDs

1. **Verify your W-2 entity** in the PSLF Employer Search Tool before accepting or renewing any job.
2. If your current role is under Aramark/Sodexo/Compass, **plan a transfer** to an in-house nonprofit hospital position, VA, WIC, or FQHC within 6–12 months.
3. **Enroll in an IDR plan** (PAYE or IBR while SAVE litigation continues).
4. **Submit a PSLF Employer Certification Form annually** — never wait until year 10.
5. After 120 qualifying payments, file the PSLF application and request forgiveness.

## FAQ

**Q: I work as a clinical RD on the hospital floor, but my W-2 is Aramark. Am I completely disqualified from PSLF?**
Yes. Only your W-2 employer counts — not where you physically work. The fix is to request transfer to the hospital's in-house clinical nutrition team or to move to a qualifying employer.

**Q: I worked at WIC in one state and moved to another state's WIC program. Do my old payments still count?**
Yes. Both county/state WIC offices are government employers. As long as you certify employment with both via PSLF Form, all 120 payments stack across employers.

**Q: Do payments made during my dietetic internship count?**
Only if your internship sponsor was a qualifying employer (most VA, IHS, and 501(c)(3) hospital internships qualify) **and** you made qualifying IDR payments during that period. Most interns are in deferment, which does not count.

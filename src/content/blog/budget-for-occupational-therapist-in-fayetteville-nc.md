## 5 Budget Tips for Fayetteville OTs

1. **VA PSLF is the cleanest path.** The Fayetteville VAMC hires OTs for geriatric, TBI, and rehabilitation programs. Federal government = automatic PSLF eligibility. No nonprofit status verification needed.

2. **DoDEA school contracts are underutilized.** Schools on Fort Liberty (Department of Defense) need school-based OTs. These are federal government jobs with PSLF eligibility, strong benefits, and 10-month pay spread over 12 months.

3. **Rent in Hope Mills or Eastover.** These suburbs outside Fayetteville proper offer 1BR apartments for $850–$950 with easy commutes to major employers.

4. **Negotiate relocation bonuses.** Fayetteville has an active OT recruitment market. $3,000–$8,000 relocation assistance is negotiable at Cape Fear Valley and larger practices.

5. **Build your military-community OT skills.** PTSD, TBI, and military families with unique needs are the Fayetteville specialty. OTs with these competencies command higher salaries and have stronger job security.

## FAQ

**What's the PSLF timeline for an OT at Cape Fear Valley?**
Cape Fear Valley is county-owned, making it a government employer. OTs working there qualify for PSLF after 120 qualifying monthly payments (10 years) on an IDR plan.

**How does Fayetteville OT salary compare to Charlotte?**
Charlotte OTs earn $5,000–$12,000 more annually, but Charlotte's higher rent and cost of living often negates the difference. Fayetteville's affordability advantage is real.

**Are there pediatric OT opportunities in Fayetteville?**
Yes — multiple pediatric outpatient centers serve the large Fort Liberty family population. However, these are typically for-profit, so PSLF doesn't apply in those settings.

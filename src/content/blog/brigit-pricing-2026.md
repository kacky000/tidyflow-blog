# Brigit Pricing 2026: Plans, Fees, and Is It Worth It?

**Brigit pricing** revolves around two tiers: a free plan with basic budgeting tools and Brigit Plus at $9.99 per month for instant cash advances, credit building, and identity theft protection. Unlike most cash advance apps that bury their costs in confusing tip structures or express fee schedules, Brigit keeps its pricing relatively straightforward — you either pay nothing and get limited features, or you pay a flat monthly subscription and unlock everything.

The real question is whether $9.99 per month is justified when competing apps like Dave and Earnin offer similar cash advance amounts at lower (or no) subscription costs. This guide walks through every cost associated with Brigit in 2026, including the fees that are easy to miss.

## Brigit Free Plan: What You Get for $0

Brigit's free tier is not a cash advance tool. It is a lightweight budgeting assistant with a few useful features that cost nothing:

- **Balance alerts** — Brigit monitors your linked bank account and warns you when your balance drops below a threshold you set. This alone can prevent overdraft fees, which average $35 per incident at most US banks.
- **Spending insights** — The Finance Helper feature categorizes your transactions and shows where your money is going. It is not as detailed as a dedicated budgeting app like YNAB or Goodbudget, but it gives you a general picture without any setup effort.
- **Income tracking** — Brigit identifies your recurring income deposits and estimates when your next paycheck will arrive. This is helpful for timing bills and avoiding low-balance situations.
- **Credit score monitoring** — You can check your credit score through the app without affecting it. The score updates periodically, though it is not a real-time tracker.

**What the free plan does NOT include:**
- No cash advances of any kind
- No credit builder loan
- No identity theft protection
- No automatic advance scheduling

The free plan is genuinely useful as a passive financial monitoring tool. If all you need is a heads-up when your balance is getting low and a rough breakdown of your spending categories, it works fine. But the moment you need actual cash to cover a gap between paychecks, you need Plus.

## Brigit Plus: $9.99/Month Breakdown

Brigit Plus is where the app's real value proposition lives. At $9.99 per month (billed monthly, no annual discount available as of 2026), you unlock the following:

### Instant Cash Advances — Up to $250

The headline feature. Brigit will advance you up to $250, deposited directly into your linked bank account. The amount you qualify for depends on your income patterns, account history, and spending behavior — not everyone starts at $250. Many new users begin with limits of $50–$100 and see the cap increase as Brigit builds confidence in their repayment ability.

**Key details:**
- **Standard delivery (1–3 business days):** Free. No fee, no tip, no interest. This is one of Brigit's strongest selling points — many competitors charge for standard delivery or prompt you to leave a "voluntary" tip that feels mandatory.
- **Express delivery (within minutes):** Brigit charges a fee for instant delivery, typically ranging from $0.99 to $3.99 depending on the advance amount. The exact fee is displayed before you confirm the transaction.
- **Repayment:** Brigit automatically deducts repayment from your bank account on your next payday. You can also repay early at any time with no penalty.
- **No interest:** Cash advances through Brigit are not loans in the traditional sense. There is no APR, no compounding interest, and no late fees if your repayment is delayed (though Brigit may pause future advances if repayment fails).
- **No tipping:** Unlike Earnin and Dave, Brigit does not ask for optional tips on advances. The $9.99 subscription is the only cost (plus express delivery fees, if you choose them).

### Credit Builder

Brigit Plus includes a credit builder loan — a small installment loan reported to all three major credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion). You do not receive the loan funds directly; instead, Brigit holds the money in an account and makes payments on your behalf, building a positive payment history on your credit report.

**How it works:**
- Brigit opens a small credit builder loan (typically $50–$150)
- Monthly payments are automatically deducted from your bank account
- Each on-time payment is reported to the credit bureaus
- After the loan term ends, you receive the principal back minus any fees

This feature is comparable to standalone credit builder services like Self or MoneyLion, which typically charge $5–$15 per month as separate subscriptions. Having it bundled into the $9.99 Brigit Plus subscription adds genuine value if you are actively trying to improve your credit score.

### Identity Theft Protection

Brigit Plus includes basic identity theft protection and monitoring. This covers:

- Dark web monitoring for your personal information (SSN, email, phone number)
- Alerts if your information appears in known data breaches
- Up to $1 million in identity theft insurance

This is a relatively new addition to the Brigit Plus bundle. While it is not as comprehensive as dedicated services like LifeLock or Aura, having it included at no extra cost is a meaningful bonus — standalone identity monitoring services typically run $8–$25 per month.

## Hidden Costs and Fees to Watch For

Brigit is more transparent than many cash advance apps, but there are still a few costs worth understanding:

### Express Delivery Fees

If you need your cash advance immediately rather than waiting 1–3 business days, Brigit charges an express delivery fee. This fee ranges from roughly $0.99 to $3.99 depending on the advance amount. While not outrageous, it adds up if you consistently use express delivery. Over a year of biweekly express advances at $2.99 each, you are looking at an extra $77.74 on top of your $119.88 subscription — bringing your total annual cost closer to $200.

### No Annual Billing Discount

Unlike many subscription services, Brigit does not offer an annual payment option with a discount. You pay $9.99 every month, period. This is a minor annoyance compared to apps that reward commitment with lower per-month pricing.

### Overdraft Risk from Automatic Repayment

Brigit automatically deducts your advance repayment on your next payday. If your paycheck is late or your bank account balance is lower than expected, this automatic deduction could trigger an overdraft fee from your bank — which is ironic, since avoiding overdrafts is one of the reasons people use Brigit in the first place. You can contact Brigit to reschedule repayment if you anticipate a problem, but you need to do this proactively.

### Subscription Charges Continue Even When Not Used

Your $9.99 monthly subscription is billed regardless of whether you take any advances. If you sign up, use one advance, and then forget to cancel, you are paying $9.99 per month for a service you are not actively using. Brigit does not pause billing based on inactivity.

## How to Cancel Brigit Plus

Canceling Brigit Plus is done through the app:

1. Open the Brigit app
2. Go to your account settings
3. Select "Manage Subscription"
4. Follow the prompts to cancel

Your subscription remains active through the end of your current billing period. You must repay any outstanding advances before or shortly after cancellation. If you have an active Credit Builder loan, Brigit will provide instructions on completing the loan term.

**Important:** If you subscribed through the iOS App Store or Google Play, you may need to cancel through those platforms rather than through the Brigit app directly. Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription — a common mistake that leads to continued billing.

## Brigit vs. Competitor Pricing

To put Brigit's $9.99/month in perspective, here is how it compares to similar cash advance apps:

**Dave** — $1/month membership, advances up to $500, but express delivery fees of $1.99–$13.99 and optional tipping. Lower subscription, but total costs can exceed Brigit depending on usage. See our [Dave vs Brigit 2026](/blog/dave-vs-brigit-2026) comparison for a full breakdown.

**Earnin** — No subscription fee, advances up to $150/day ($750/pay period), but relies on "voluntary" tips that average $3–$5 per advance. No credit builder included. For a detailed look at how Earnin stacks up against Dave, see our [Earnin vs Dave 2026](/blog/earnin-vs-dave-2026) analysis.

**Albert** — $14.99/month for Genius tier (cash advances up to $250), includes budgeting and savings features. More expensive than Brigit with a similar advance cap.

**MoneyLion** — $0 for Instacash advances (up to $500 with direct deposit), but the credit builder loan ($19.99/month) and other premium features are separately priced. Complex pricing structure.

**Chime** — SpotMe feature covers overdrafts up to $200 at no cost for qualifying direct deposit customers. No subscription, no fees, but requires direct deposit of $200+/month. Not technically a cash advance app.

Brigit lands in the middle of the pack. It is more expensive than Dave's $1/month base subscription, but cheaper than Albert's $14.99/month. Its biggest differentiator is the no-tipping policy and bundled credit builder — if you value predictable costs over the lowest possible price, Brigit has an edge.

## Who Should Pay for Brigit Plus?

### Brigit Plus makes sense if you:
- Regularly need small cash advances ($50–$250) between paychecks
- Want predictable costs without tipping pressure or surprise fees
- Are actively building or repairing your credit score
- Value having identity theft monitoring bundled in
- Prefer standard (free) delivery and rarely need instant access

### Skip Brigit Plus if you:
- Rarely or never need cash advances
- Only need an advance once or twice a year (the subscription cost outweighs the benefit)
- Already have a credit builder service through another provider
- Qualify for Chime SpotMe or a similar free overdraft feature
- Want advances larger than $250 (Dave and MoneyLion offer higher limits)

If you are living paycheck to paycheck and frequently need $50–$150 to bridge the gap, Brigit Plus at $9.99/month is cheaper than a single $35 overdraft fee. That math works in Brigit's favor. But if you are only hitting cash shortfalls a few times a year, the cumulative subscription cost may not justify the convenience. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Brigit charge interest on cash advances?
No. Brigit advances are not loans and carry no interest rate, APR, or compounding charges. The $9.99 monthly subscription is the cost of access. The only additional fee is for express delivery if you choose it.

### Can I get a Brigit advance without a Plus subscription?
No. Cash advances are exclusively available to Brigit Plus subscribers. The free plan provides budgeting tools and balance alerts only.

### How much can I borrow from Brigit on my first advance?
Most new users start with a limit between $50 and $100. Your limit increases over time based on consistent income deposits, account stability, and repayment history. The maximum is $250.

### Is Brigit's Credit Builder worth it compared to Self or MoneyLion?
Brigit's Credit Builder is included in the $9.99/month Plus subscription. Self charges $5–$25/month as a standalone service, and MoneyLion's credit builder costs $19.99/month. If you are already paying for Brigit Plus for the cash advance feature, the bundled credit builder is essentially free — which makes it a strong value compared to paying for a separate service.

### What happens if I cannot repay my Brigit advance on time?
Brigit does not charge late fees. However, if automatic repayment fails, Brigit will attempt to debit your account again. Repeated failed repayments may result in your advance limit being reduced or your account being suspended. Brigit may also report delinquent Credit Builder loan payments to the credit bureaus, which could negatively affect your credit score.

## Bottom Line: Brigit Pricing in 2026

Brigit's pricing model is simple: free for basic budgeting tools, $9.99/month for everything else. The Plus subscription bundles cash advances (up to $250), credit building, and identity theft protection into a single predictable cost — no tipping, no hidden interest, no confusing fee schedules.

Whether that $9.99 is worth it depends entirely on how often you use it. If you take two or three advances per month with standard delivery, Brigit costs you $9.99 total — cheaper than a single bank overdraft fee and more predictable than tip-based competitors. If you rarely need advances but want the credit builder, compare the $9.99 against standalone credit builder services to see if the bundle makes financial sense.

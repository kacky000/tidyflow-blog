Killeen, Texas exists because of **Fort Hood (now officially Fort Cavazos)** — the largest active-duty US Army installation in the world by population. The base and its surrounding region drive an unusually **federal-heavy PSLF economy**: more than half of all PSLF-eligible jobs in Bell County are tied to the Army, the school districts that serve military families, or the federally chartered hospitals and clinics that ring the base.

If you have student debt and you live in Killeen, Harker Heights, Copperas Cove, Belton, or Temple, your PSLF map looks completely different from a typical Texas city.

## Why Killeen Is Different

Three factors shape Killeen's PSLF landscape:

1. **Fort Cavazos** employs ~36,000 active-duty soldiers plus ~9,000 civilian employees — most of them federal GS-scale jobs that automatically qualify for PSLF.
2. **Killeen ISD** is the largest school district in Central Texas (~46,000 students) and a major PSLF-eligible employer for teachers, counselors, school psychologists, SLPs, OTs, PTs, and nurses.
3. **The major nonprofit hospital systems are in nearby Temple and Belton** (Baylor Scott & White, AdventHealth Central Texas) — 25–35 minutes north. Many Killeen healthcare workers commute for PSLF.

## ✅ PSLF-Eligible Employers in Killeen and Bell County

### Federal (Fort Cavazos and military medicine)

- **Carl R. Darnall Army Medical Center (CRDAMC)** — Federal Army medical center on Fort Cavazos. Largest healthcare employer in the area. Hires civilian RNs, NPs, PAs, OTs, PTs, pharmacists, social workers, mental health providers, dental hygienists, and clerical staff under the federal GS pay scale. **PSLF-eligible from day one.**
- **Fort Cavazos garrison civilian workforce** — IT, engineering, HR, education services, child & youth services, MWR. All federal → PSLF-eligible.
- **Veterans Affairs — Olin E. Teague VA Medical Center (Temple)** — 30 minutes north of Killeen. Federal. PSLF-eligible. Hires across most healthcare and administrative roles.
- **Defense Health Agency clinics** on Fort Cavazos (primary care, dental, behavioral health) — Federal → PSLF-eligible.
- **US Postal Service, federal courts, IRS, SSA offices in Bell County** — Federal → PSLF-eligible.

### Public Schools

- **Killeen ISD** — The largest district in Central Texas. Roughly 6,500 employees. Teachers, paraprofessionals, school nurses, counselors, SLPs, school psychologists, librarians, custodial, transportation. All PSLF-eligible.
- **Copperas Cove ISD, Belton ISD, Lampasas ISD, Salado ISD, Florence ISD, Gatesville ISD, Temple ISD** — Public school districts within commuting distance. PSLF-eligible.

### Public Higher Education

- **Central Texas College (Killeen)** — Public community college. PSLF-eligible. Heavy military-affiliated enrollment.
- **Texas A&M University Central Texas (Killeen)** — Public university. PSLF-eligible.
- **Temple College (Temple)** — Public community college. PSLF-eligible.

### Local Government

- **City of Killeen, City of Harker Heights, City of Copperas Cove, City of Belton, City of Temple** — Municipal employees. PSLF-eligible.
- **Bell County Government** — Sheriff's office, county clerk, public health, indigent care, juvenile services. PSLF-eligible.
- **Coryell County and Lampasas County governments** — PSLF-eligible.

### Nonprofit Hospitals (commuting north to Temple/Belton)

- **Baylor Scott & White Medical Center — Temple** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Flagship hospital of the Baylor Scott & White Health system. Level I trauma center for Central Texas. ~30 minute commute from Killeen. PSLF-eligible. The single largest nonprofit hospital employer in the region.
- **Baylor Scott & White McLane Children's Medical Center (Temple)** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit. PSLF-eligible.
- **Baylor Scott & White Medical Center — Marble Falls / Round Rock / etc.** — All Baylor Scott & White entities are 501(c)(3) → PSLF-eligible.
- **AdventHealth Central Texas (Killeen)** — Formerly Metroplex Adventist Hospital and Metroplex Health System; rebranded as part of AdventHealth in 2018. **Seventh-day Adventist 501(c)(3) nonprofit. PSLF-eligible.** This is the closest nonprofit hospital to downtown Killeen — and the most important option for those who can't commute to Temple.
- **AdventHealth Rollins Brook (Lampasas)** — Same AdventHealth nonprofit network. PSLF-eligible.

### FQHCs and Community Health

- **Texas A&M Family Care Clinic, Family Care Clinic of Bell County** — verify 501(c)(3) status individually.
- **Central Counties Services (mental health authority)** — Public community mental health center. PSLF-eligible.

## ❌ NOT PSLF-Eligible Employers in the Killeen Area

- **Seton Medical Center Harker Heights** — Previously part of Ascension Seton (nonprofit), but **sold to AdventHealth in late 2024**. Verify the current employer EIN — AdventHealth ownership is PSLF-eligible. (However, during the brief Steward/transition periods, employer status may have changed; check certifications carefully.)
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Killeen** (if operating in the area) — NYSE: EHC, publicly traded → NOT PSLF.
- **DSO dental chains (Aspen Dental, Heartland Dental, Western Dental)** in Killeen — for-profit → NOT PSLF.
- **For-profit private practices, urgent cares, surgery centers, private therapy clinics** — for-profit → NOT PSLF.

## ⚠️ The "AdventHealth" Rebranding Note

Many longtime Killeen healthcare workers still call AdventHealth Central Texas by its old name "**Metroplex**" or "**Metroplex Adventist Hospital**." The rebranding to AdventHealth happened in 2018 as part of the system-wide AdventHealth rollout. The legal status — Seventh-day Adventist 501(c)(3) nonprofit — has remained throughout. **All AdventHealth hospitals are PSLF-eligible.** Don't be confused by the name change.

## FAQ

**Q: I'm an active-duty soldier at Fort Cavazos. Do my military years count for PSLF?**
Yes. Active military service is federal employment and counts for PSLF qualifying payments while you're making qualifying student loan payments (often $0 under SAVE/IBR/PAYE). Submit Employer Certification Forms every year — your finance office or VA representative can help.

**Q: I'm a teacher at Killeen ISD. Can I combine PSLF and Teacher Loan Forgiveness?**
Yes, but not on the same payments. The standard strategy is: take Teacher Loan Forgiveness ($5K or $17.5K depending on subject) first after 5 years, then continue toward PSLF for the remaining 5 years. Or skip TLF and go straight for the full PSLF discharge — usually larger if your balance is high.

**Q: AdventHealth Central Texas is closer than Baylor Scott & White Temple. Is the commute worth it for Baylor?**
Depends on the role and pay differential. Baylor Scott & White is a Level I trauma academic medical center with a wider mix of specialty roles; AdventHealth Central Texas is a community hospital. Both are 501(c)(3) PSLF-eligible. For most clinical roles, AdventHealth Killeen is the cleaner choice (no commute, equivalent PSLF status).

## 5 Action Items for Killeen-Area Workers Pursuing PSLF

1. **Federal first.** CRDAMC, the Temple VA, and Fort Cavazos civilian jobs are the strongest PSLF anchors in the region.
2. **AdventHealth Central Texas is your in-town nonprofit hospital.** Don't drive to Temple if you don't need to.
3. **Killeen ISD is huge.** ~6,500 PSLF-eligible jobs, not just teachers — nurses, OTs, PTs, SLPs, counselors, paraprofessionals.
4. **Avoid Encompass Health and DSO dental chains.** Both are for-profit and disqualify PSLF.
5. **Texas has no state income tax.** Combine PSLF with maxing a Roth IRA — your tax-advantaged retirement compounds while you wait for forgiveness.

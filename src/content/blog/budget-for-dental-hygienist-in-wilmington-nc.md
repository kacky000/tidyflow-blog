## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Warning: Novant Health Acquisition

**This is the most important paragraph in this guide.**

In 2021, New Hanover Regional Medical Center (NHRMC) — historically the area's largest PSLF-eligible nonprofit hospital — was sold to **Novant Health** in a $1.5 billion transaction.

- **Pre-2021 employment**: If you worked at NHRMC before the sale and were on PSLF, your qualifying payments during that period count.
- **Post-2021 employment**: Novant Health IS a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so it does qualify for PSLF. However, employment must be direct with Novant Health (not contracted, leased, or via a separate practice group).
- **Confirm with Form PSLF certification annually.** Don't assume.

This is exactly the kind of acquisition that can quietly disrupt loan forgiveness plans. Always verify your employer's tax status with HR.

## 5 Money Tips for Dental Hygienists in Wilmington

1. **Verify PSLF eligibility annually.** With Novant Health's acquisition of NHRMC, the local PSLF landscape changed. File Form PSLF every year. If your employer status changes mid-year, payments before the change still count — but you need documentation.

2. **Avoid Wrightsville Beach rent unless you commute is short.** Rentals on the beach can be $2,200+/month for 1BR. Living in Carolina Place or Forest Hills cuts $700–$900/month without sacrificing lifestyle.

3. **Budget for hurricane season.** Wilmington is on the coast and has been hit by major hurricanes (Florence 2018, Isaias 2020). Keep $1,000+ in a separate evacuation/repairs fund. Renter's insurance is essential.

4. **Tax-advantaged accounts maximize over a career.** Use your dental hygienist HSA (if offered) and a Roth IRA. NC's flat tax means tax-advantaged savings is simpler to plan than in tiered-tax states.

5. **Watch dental office consolidation.** Wilmington's dental market has corporate DSO (dental service organization) consolidation pressure. Independent practices may close or sell, changing your benefits package mid-career. Build a 4-month emergency fund.

## Student Loan Considerations

The average dental hygienist graduates with $20,000–$45,000 in student debt. On an IDR plan at $68,000 income:
- SAVE plan payment: ~$240–$300/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$260–$500/month

PSLF eligibility is employer-specific in Wilmington:
- **Novant Health (NHRMC)**: 501(c)(3) — PSLF eligible since the acquisition.
- **Wilmington Health (multi-specialty group)**: For-profit DSO — does NOT qualify for PSLF.
- **Coastal Horizons / Cape Fear Health Net**: FQHCs — PSLF eligible.
- **Private dental practices**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.

## FAQ

**Is Wilmington a good city for new dental hygienists?**
Mixed. The job market is strong, but housing costs eat a larger share of income than in inland NC metros. Plan for 30–35% of income on rent.

**Does the Novant Health acquisition of NHRMC affect PSLF?**
Yes, but Novant is also a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so PSLF eligibility continues post-acquisition. The risk is improper categorization. File Form PSLF certification every year.

**How does Wilmington compare to other NC dental hygienist markets?**
Wilmington pays slightly above NC average due to coastal premium, but rent eats most of that advantage. Greensboro and Raleigh offer better take-home savings rates.

# Dental Hygienist Budget Template for Tacoma, WA (2026 Guide)

A working **dental hygienist budget for Tacoma, WA** has to handle three things simultaneously: the higher cost of living around **Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM)** and its 40,000-soldier military community, the **no Washington state income tax** advantage, and the deep bench of PSLF-eligible employers like **Madigan Army Medical Center**, **MultiCare Health System**, and **Virginia Mason Franciscan Health**. This 2026 guide gives Pierce County DHs salary benchmarks, a realistic monthly budget, a JBLM military-spouse playbook, and a complete PSLF employer map.

Tacoma offers a West Coast premium for DHs, driven by demand from JBLM, a strong nonprofit hospital ecosystem, and proximity to Seattle.

Add **no state income tax** in Washington and a $100,000 DH salary in Tacoma nets meaningfully more than Portland or Sacramento at the same gross.

## 2. Cost of Living Overview

Tacoma is the affordable alternative to Seattle, but COL is still notably higher than the national median.

- **Housing:** 1BR $1,600-2,000/mo; 2BR $2,000-2,600/mo; 3BR house $2,800-3,500/mo
- **Utilities:** $190-260/mo (mild summers, wet winters; heat dominant)
- **Groceries:** $480-560/mo single, $850-1,000 couple
- **Transportation:** Car helpful but Sound Transit available; gas $160-220/mo, insurance $140-185/mo
- **Healthcare premiums (DH employer plan):** $130-230/mo

## 4. Military Spouse Network Strategy

JBLM's footprint is enormous, and Tacoma DHs who tap into it consistently get hired faster and earn more:

- **Washington DOH** recognizes military-spouse licenses transferred from other states — turnaround is often <30 days vs. months elsewhere.
- The **JBLM Military Spouse Employment Partnership (MSEP)** maintains a hiring list specifically including dental practices and nonprofit hospitals around Lakewood, DuPont, and Tacoma.
- **MyCAA** funds (E-1 to E-5 spouses) cover Washington DH license renewal CEs.
- Practices serving TRICARE often offer flexible PCS-aware scheduling — ask up front.
- Join **Tacoma-Pierce County Dental Hygienists' Society**; its military-spouse subgroup actively shares openings.

## 5. PSLF Eligible Employers in Tacoma

These are confirmed 501(c)(3) or government employers qualifying for **Public Service Loan Forgiveness**:

- **Madigan Army Medical Center** — federal/Army hospital at JBLM with major dental services
- **MultiCare Health System** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit; flagship is Tacoma General, dental clinics across Pierce County
- **Virginia Mason Franciscan Health (CHI Franciscan)** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit including St. Joseph Medical Center
- **Western State Hospital** — Washington state psychiatric hospital, employs dental staff
- **Tacoma Public Schools** — district-based school dental health programs
- **Sea Mar Community Health Centers** — FQHC (PSLF + NHSC loan repayment double-qualifies)
- **JBLM Dental Activity (DENTAC)** — federal civilian dental positions

For the full breakdown, see [PSLF Eligible Employers in Tacoma, WA](/blog/pslf-eligible-employers-in-tacoma-wa/).

## 6. Savings Strategy for Tacoma DHs

1. **Convert the no-state-tax win into Roth contributions** — what would be CA/OR state tax goes straight to your IRA.
2. **NHSC + PSLF stack:** If you work at Sea Mar or similar FQHC, you can layer NHSC loan repayment on top of PSLF.
3. **Build a 6-month emergency fund** — Tacoma's rent is steep enough that a 90-day job gap hurts.
4. **HSA before Roth** if you have an HDHP — WA has no state tax to recapture, making the HSA triple-tax-free.
5. Compare against southern markets in [Dental Hygienist Budget for Killeen, TX](/blog/budget-for-dental-hygienist-in-killeen-tx/) to see how WA's higher pay offsets higher COL.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: Is Tacoma really $30k+ better than Killeen for DHs?**
A: On gross, yes (~$100k vs ~$78k). On take-home after housing, the gap narrows to ~$8-12k/year — but Tacoma's career ceiling is much higher.

**Q: Can I work at Madigan as a civilian DH for PSLF?**
A: Yes — federal civilian positions at Madigan AMC qualify directly for PSLF as government employment.

**Q: Should I commute from Lakewood/DuPont to save on rent?**
A: Lakewood/Spanaway can save $300-500/mo on rent with a 15-25 minute commute. DuPont costs about the same as Tacoma but offers newer housing.

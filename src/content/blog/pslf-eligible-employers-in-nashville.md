Nashville is the most PSLF-confusing city in America. It's the **headquarters of HCA Healthcare** — the largest for-profit hospital chain in the U.S. — and at the same time home to Vanderbilt University Medical Center, one of the strongest academic nonprofits in the South. This guide maps every major employer.

## Why Nashville Is Unique

Roughly **40% of Nashville's hospital beds** are HCA Healthcare (for-profit) under the TriStar brand. This is the highest for-profit hospital share of any major US metro. Meanwhile, Vanderbilt and Saint Thomas (Ascension Catholic) dominate the nonprofit side.

For healthcare workers with student debt, a single job decision in Nashville determines whether you receive **$50,000–$200,000+** in eventual loan forgiveness — or zero.

## ✅ PSLF-Eligible Hospital Systems in Nashville

### Academic / Major Nonprofit
- **Vanderbilt University Medical Center (VUMC)** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit (independent since 2016) → fully PSLF-eligible. All VUMC entities: Vanderbilt University Hospital, Monroe Carell Jr. Children's Hospital, Vanderbilt Wilson County, Vanderbilt Bedford, Vanderbilt Tullahoma-Harton.
- **Vanderbilt University** (academic, separate from VUMC) — 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible.

### Catholic Nonprofit
- **Saint Thomas Health (Ascension Saint Thomas)** — Catholic 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible. All locations: Saint Thomas West, Saint Thomas Midtown, Saint Thomas Rutherford, Saint Thomas Hickman, Saint Thomas DeKalb, Saint Thomas Stones River, Saint Thomas Highlands, Saint Thomas River Park.

### Public / Governmental
- **Nashville General Hospital at Meharry** — Public (city-owned) → PSLF-eligible.
- **Meharry Medical College** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible.
- **Nashville VA Medical Center / TVHS** — Federal (Tennessee Valley Healthcare System, includes Murfreesboro VA) → PSLF-eligible.

### FQHCs
- **Neighborhood Health, United Neighborhood Health Services, Connectus Health, Matthew Walker Comprehensive Health Center, Faith Family Medical Center** → PSLF-eligible Federally Qualified Health Centers.

### Schools and Universities
- **Metro Nashville Public Schools (MNPS), Rutherford County Schools, Williamson County Schools, Wilson County Schools** — Public → PSLF-eligible.
- **Tennessee State University, Belmont University, Lipscomb University, Trevecca Nazarene** — Various public/nonprofit; verify 501(c)(3) status individually.
- **Fisk University** — 501(c)(3) HBCU → PSLF-eligible.

## ❌ The TriStar Trap (HCA Healthcare)

**HCA Healthcare is headquartered at One Park Plaza, Nashville**. Its TriStar regional brand owns **6 hospitals in the Nashville area** — all publicly traded for-profit (NYSE: HCA). **NONE are PSLF-eligible**:

1. **TriStar Centennial Medical Center** (main midtown campus, near Vanderbilt) — HCA → NOT PSLF.
2. **TriStar Skyline Medical Center** (Madison) — HCA → NOT PSLF.
3. **TriStar Southern Hills Medical Center** (Antioch) — HCA → NOT PSLF.
4. **TriStar Summit Medical Center** (Hermitage) — HCA → NOT PSLF.
5. **TriStar Hendersonville Medical Center** (Hendersonville) — HCA → NOT PSLF.
6. **TriStar StoneCrest Medical Center** (Smyrna) — HCA → NOT PSLF.

Plus surrounding HCA TriStar facilities:
- **TriStar Horizon Medical Center** (Dickson) — HCA → NOT PSLF.
- **TriStar Greenview Regional Hospital** (Bowling Green, KY) — HCA → NOT PSLF.
- **HCA Healthcare Corporate Headquarters** (One Park Plaza) — for-profit → NOT PSLF.

## ⚠️ Big Brand Name Confusions

**"TriStar" sounds generic** — many healthcare workers assume it's a regional nonprofit. It is **HCA Healthcare's Tennessee brand**. Every TriStar hospital is publicly traded for-profit.

**"Encompass Health" sounds nonprofit** — the word "Health" is a common nonprofit signal. But Encompass Health is **NYSE: EHC**, publicly traded for-profit. The largest IRF (inpatient rehab) operator in the U.S. — and almost universally NOT PSLF-eligible.

**"Saint Thomas" is PSLF-eligible** — unlike "Saint Mary's" in Reno or "Saint Joseph's" in some other cities (which may be Prime or Tenet), Saint Thomas in Nashville is genuinely Ascension Catholic nonprofit. PSLF-eligible.

## The HCA TriStar Concentration Risk

If you're a Nashville healthcare worker with student debt, your PSLF strategy must actively **avoid the TriStar brand**. Here's the math:

- Average dental hygienist at TriStar Centennial: $72k salary, $32k loan balance, no PSLF → pays full $32k + interest over 10 years.
- Same hygienist at Vanderbilt Health: $69k salary (slightly lower), $32k loan balance, PSLF after 120 payments → potentially discharges $20–25k after 10 years on IDR.

**Net PSLF benefit: $15,000–$25,000** over 10 years even with the $3k/year salary discount.

For OTs and PTs with $130k debt, the same math is **$80,000–$150,000+** in lifetime savings by avoiding TriStar.

## How to Verify Your Employer

1. **Check the W-2 EIN.** Go to the IRS Exempt Organization Search (apps.irs.gov/app/eos). VUMC, Saint Thomas, Meharry — all appear as 501(c)(3) public charities.
2. **For TriStar / HCA jobs**, the EIN search will not show 501(c)(3) status — confirming for-profit status.
3. **Submit the PSLF Employer Certification Form** annually via StudentAid.gov/pslf. Don't wait 10 years.

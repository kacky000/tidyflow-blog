Macon is one of the cheapest mid-size dental hygienist markets in the Southeast. The cost of living is significantly below Atlanta or Augusta, while a dental hygienist's salary is only modestly lower. The PSLF map is also unusually clean because there are only two major hospital systems — but both have changed ownership in the last decade, and that matters for verification.

## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Distinction: Atrium Navicent vs Coliseum vs Mercer

Macon has been through major hospital ownership changes:

- **Atrium Health Navicent** (formerly Navicent Health, now part of Advocate Health after 2022 merger) — 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible. **Verify EIN** because the Advocate Health merger may have updated employer entity names on W-2s.
- **Atrium Health Navicent — The Medical Center, Navicent Health (downtown Macon)** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible.
- **Coliseum Medical Centers (Coliseum Health System)** — Owned by **HCA Healthcare → for-profit, NOT PSLF-eligible.**
- **Mercer University Medical Center** — Mercer University is a 501(c)(3) private nonprofit university → PSLF-eligible for university-employed staff. Confirm whether your role is Mercer-employed or Atrium Navicent-employed.
- **Carl Vinson VA Medical Center (Dublin, ~50 miles east)** — Federal → PSLF-eligible.
- **Houston Healthcare (Warner Robins)** — Authority-based public hospital → PSLF-eligible.

**The trap**: a hygienist who joins Coliseum Medical Centers assuming "big hospital = PSLF" loses qualifying employment. Coliseum is HCA's Macon-area brand.

## 5 Money Tips for Dental Hygienists in Macon

1. **Confirm Atrium Navicent EIN after Advocate merger.** In 2022, Atrium Health (parent of Navicent) merged with Advocate Aurora to form Advocate Health. This is a major HR system change. Re-submit Form PSLF after any payroll change — confirm the W-2 EIN matches a 501(c)(3).

2. **Avoid the Coliseum trap.** Coliseum Medical Centers and Coliseum Northside Hospital are HCA Healthcare's Macon-area brand. Both look like nonprofit-style hospital names. Both are for-profit. **W-2s from Coliseum do NOT qualify for PSLF.**

3. **Use Macon's low rent to load retirement aggressively.** Median rent at 26% of net take-home is the lowest among Georgia hygienist cities we've covered. Max out Roth IRA ($7,000/year) and contribute 10%+ of gross to employer retirement.

4. **Watch DSO consolidation.** Heartland Dental and Aspen Dental have expanded into Middle Georgia. They pay competitive rates but are for-profit. If you have student debt and want PSLF, stay with Atrium Navicent, Mercer, or public health employers.

5. **Cherry Blossom Festival short-term rental income.** Macon hosts the International Cherry Blossom Festival every March. Short-term rentals during the festival can add $500–$2,500/week. Section 280A "Augusta Rule" allows 14 nights/year tax-free rental of your primary residence — festival week is one of the few opportunities.

## Student Loan Considerations

Macon-area hygienist programs (Central Georgia Technical College, Middle Georgia State University) graduate students with **$15,000–$35,000** debt. Below national average.

On IDR at $60,000 income:
- SAVE plan payment: ~$210–$280/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$200–$420/month

PSLF eligibility map for Macon:
- **Atrium Health Navicent, Mercer University, Houston Healthcare, Carl Vinson VA**: PSLF eligible.
- **Coliseum Medical Centers (HCA)**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.
- **Private dental, DSOs**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.

## FAQ

**Is Macon a good city for new dental hygienists?**
Yes if you want low cost of living and don't need Atlanta's career velocity. The trade-off: smaller market means fewer job options if you don't like your first employer. Better savings rate than most peer cities.

**Atrium Navicent vs Atrium Health — same employer?**
Atrium Health Navicent is the Macon division of Atrium Health (now Advocate Health after 2022 merger). Same parent 501(c)(3) — both qualify for PSLF. But always verify your specific employer EIN on Form PSLF.

**Should I work at Coliseum Medical Centers?**
Coliseum is HCA Healthcare. For-profit. If you have $20k+ student debt and want PSLF, Atrium Navicent or Mercer is the better choice even at slightly lower hourly rate.

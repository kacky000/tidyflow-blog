Occupational Therapy Assistants (OTAs) get an underrated deal in Charleston, SC: a real PSLF-eligible market via MUSC (Medical University of South Carolina) and Joint Base Charleston, plus a lower-debt entry path than full OT licensure. The catch is Charleston's well-known rent inflation. This guide shows how to make the math work.

## Average OTA Salary in Charleston

OTAs in the Charleston-North Charleston MSA earn between **$58,000–$72,000 per year**, with a median near $64,000. Acute care and pediatric OTAs at MUSC earn at the upper end. School district OTAs are at median.

## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Information: MUSC + Joint Base Charleston

**This is the most important paragraph in this guide.**

OTA-relevant employers in Charleston and their PSLF status:

- **MUSC Health (Medical University of South Carolina)**: Public state university → PSLF-eligible. MUSC is one of the largest employers in the Lowcountry and a major OTA employer (acute care, pediatric, neuro rehab).
- **Roper St. Francis Healthcare**: 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible (Catholic-affiliated).
- **Trident Medical Center**: Owned by HCA Healthcare → **for-profit, does NOT qualify for PSLF**.
- **Joint Base Charleston (Naval Health Clinic Charleston)**: Direct federal employment → PSLF-eligible.
- **Ralph H. Johnson VA Medical Center**: Federal VA employment → PSLF-eligible.
- **Charleston County Schools / Berkeley County Schools / Dorchester District 2**: Public school districts → PSLF-eligible.
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Charleston**: For-profit (Encompass NYSE: EHC) → does NOT qualify.

**MUSC + the federal facilities (VA + Joint Base Charleston) make Charleston unusually strong for PSLF-pursuing OTAs.** That's rare for the OTA salary band — most cities only have one or two qualifying systems.

## 5 Money Tips for OTAs in Charleston

1. **Live in West Ashley or North Charleston, not Mount Pleasant.** Mount Pleasant rent is 30–40% higher with only 10–15 min commute advantage. North Charleston cuts $400+/month off rent.

2. **MUSC + PRN VA stacking.** MUSC plus PRN shifts at Ralph H. Johnson VA Medical Center is the ideal OTA PSLF stack — both qualifying, both clinical experience-rich. Build the relationship early.

3. **Roommate math saves $700+/month.** A 2BR in West Ashley split with a roommate averages $900/person vs $1,400 for a 1BR alone. For an OTA on $64k, that's enough to fully fund a Roth IRA.

4. **Hurricane fund.** Charleston is on the coast and has been hit by major hurricanes (Hugo 1989, Matthew 2016, Florence 2018 grazing). Keep $1,500+ in a separate evacuation/repair fund. Renter's insurance is essential.

5. **Avoid Trident Medical Center if you need PSLF.** HCA's tax status doesn't change just because the hospital is local. The same shift at MUSC or Roper qualifies; at Trident it doesn't.

## Student Loan Considerations

OTAs typically graduate with $20,000–$40,000 in student debt (associate-degree credential, lower than full OT). On an IDR plan at $64,000 income:
- SAVE plan payment: ~$220–$280/month
- Standard 10-year payment: ~$220–$450/month

OTAs benefit less from PSLF than full OTs (because debt is lower), but $20,000+ in forgiven debt + 10 years of low IDR payments is still meaningful. Plus, at the OTA salary band in expensive Charleston, every dollar of student-loan cash flow matters.

PSLF eligibility in Charleston:
- **MUSC Health**: Public university — PSLF eligible.
- **Roper St. Francis Healthcare**: 501(c)(3) — PSLF eligible.
- **Trident Medical Center (HCA)**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.
- **Encompass Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Charleston**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.
- **Joint Base Charleston (Naval Health Clinic), Ralph H. Johnson VA**: Federal — PSLF eligible.
- **Charleston/Berkeley/Dorchester County Schools**: PSLF eligible.
- **Private OT clinics, DSO chains**: For-profit — does NOT qualify.

## FAQ

**Is Charleston a good city for OTAs?**
Mixed. PSLF options are unusually strong. Cost of living is unusually high. If you have student loans and can stomach the rent (or get a roommate), it can work. If you have no debt and want maximum savings, Columbia SC or Greenville SC offer better take-home rates.

**Why does MUSC count for PSLF?**
MUSC is a public state university — like UAB Alabama, like USA Health Mobile. Public university health systems are by definition government employers, which qualify for PSLF.

**How does Charleston compare to Greenville SC or Columbia SC for OTAs?**
Charleston pays slightly more but rent is significantly higher. Net savings rate is usually better in Columbia SC. Charleston wins for PSLF concentration (MUSC + federal facilities).

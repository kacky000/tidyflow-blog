## ⚠️ Critical PSLF Distinction: Erlanger vs CHI Memorial vs Parkridge

Chattanooga has a rare **three-tier** PSLF map:

- **Erlanger Health System** — Hamilton County **public hospital** (governmental) → fully PSLF-eligible. Includes Erlanger Baroness, Erlanger East, Erlanger North, T.C. Thompson Children's.
- **CHI Memorial Hospital** — Part of **CommonSpirit Health**, a Catholic 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible. CHI Memorial Hixson, CHI Memorial Georgia all qualify.
- **Parkridge Medical Center, Parkridge East, Parkridge West** — Owned by **HCA Healthcare** → **for-profit, NOT PSLF-eligible.**
- **Siskin Hospital for Physical Rehabilitation** — 501(c)(3) nonprofit → PSLF-eligible.
- **Chattanooga VA Clinic** — Federal → PSLF-eligible.

**The trap**: Parkridge Medical Center is large, modern, and centrally located — many hygienists assume it's nonprofit because of its scale. It's HCA. The Parkridge brand is HCA's Chattanooga regional brand, like TriStar is in Nashville.

## 5 Money Tips for Dental Hygienists in Chattanooga

1. **Use Tennessee's no-state-tax advantage.** $0 state tax on $59k saves you ~$2,400/year vs Georgia. That covers most of a Roth IRA contribution.

2. **Verify your hospital's tax status before signing.** Chattanooga's three-system map means a hygienist switching jobs might inadvertently switch from public (Erlanger) to for-profit (Parkridge) and lose PSLF qualification.

3. **Avoid the Parkridge trap.** Parkridge is HCA Healthcare. Like TriStar in Nashville, the brand looks generic but is publicly traded for-profit.

4. **Ringgold/Fort Oglethorpe arbitrage.** If you're licensed in both TN and GA, you can live in Ringgold GA (cheaper housing) and work in Chattanooga TN. But beware: GA has state income tax (5.39%), so the math may not work unless rent is 15%+ cheaper.

5. **DSO consolidation is moderate.** Heartland and Aspen are present but less dominant than Nashville. Confirm Erlanger or CHI Memorial employment status before assuming PSLF.

## FAQ

**Is Chattanooga a good city for new dental hygienists?**
Yes — it's the cheapest of the major Tennessee metros and has a clean public/nonprofit/for-profit hospital split. Best for hygienists with high student debt who want to maximize PSLF.

**Why doesn't Parkridge Medical Center qualify for PSLF?**
Parkridge is HCA Healthcare's Chattanooga brand. HCA is publicly traded for-profit (NYSE: HCA). The hospital being large or modern does not change tax status.

**Is Erlanger really a public hospital?**
Yes. Erlanger is a Hamilton County hospital authority (a special-purpose governmental entity). Governmental employers qualify for PSLF.

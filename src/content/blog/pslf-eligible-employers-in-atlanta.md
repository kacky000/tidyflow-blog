Atlanta has more PSLF-eligible employers than any other Southeast city — but it also has one of the densest concentrations of for-profit hospital traps. This guide is the definitive 2026 map of who qualifies and who doesn't.

## Why Atlanta Matters for PSLF

Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) discharges your remaining federal student loan balance after **120 qualifying monthly payments** while working full-time for a qualified employer. Atlanta has roughly **180,000 healthcare workers**, plus state/federal employees, public school teachers, and the largest VA in the Southeast — but the for-profit hospital footprint (HCA, Tenet, Encompass) is also one of the densest in the country.

A single wrong job decision in Atlanta can cost you **$50,000–$200,000** in forfeited forgiveness.

## ⚠️ Big Brand Name Confusions

**Northside Hospital (Atlanta, Cherokee, Forsyth, Duluth, Gwinnett)** — Northside is an **independent nonprofit** 501(c)(3) — PSLF-eligible. Often confused with for-profit because of its scale. **Northside is NOT HCA**. (Northside Hospital is one of the largest birthing hospitals in the U.S. and is genuinely nonprofit.)

**Saint Joseph's** — Multiple "Saint Joseph's" exist nationally. The Atlanta Saint Joseph's was historically Tenet-affiliated but transferred to Emory Healthcare in 2012 — now Emory Saint Joseph's, which is **PSLF-eligible**.

**Atlanta Medical Center (AMC)** — Tenet-owned, **closed November 2022**. No longer operating as a hospital. If you see an AMC listing, it's outdated.

**Wellstar Atlanta Medical Center South (East Point)** — **Closed May 2023** alongside the main AMC closure.

## How to Verify Your Employer

1. **Check the W-2 EIN.** Go to the IRS Exempt Organization Search (apps.irs.gov/app/eos) and enter your employer's EIN. If it appears as a 501(c)(3) public charity, it's PSLF-eligible.
2. **For public/governmental employers**, the IRS database won't list them — use the PSLF Help Tool (StudentAid.gov/pslf) and search by employer EIN.
3. **For uncertain cases**, submit the PSLF Employer Certification Form. The Department of Education will officially certify or deny each employer.

## The Wellstar–Augusta University Acquisition Pattern

In 2023, Wellstar (nonprofit) acquired AU Health (Augusta) — both PSLF-eligible. This is a **rare PSLF-friendly acquisition** because both pre and post-acquisition entities were nonprofit. Most acquisitions trap PSLF workers (e.g., Brookwood Baptist sold to Orlando Health in 2024 — but Orlando Health is nonprofit, so this also remains PSLF-eligible).

By contrast, common **PSLF-destroying acquisitions** are:
- Nonprofit hospital → HCA (for-profit) → PSLF eligibility lost
- Nonprofit hospital → CHS (Community Health Systems) → PSLF eligibility lost
- Nonprofit hospital → Prime Healthcare → PSLF eligibility lost

Always recertify employer status with the PSLF Help Tool after any ownership change.

## 5 Action Items for Atlanta Healthcare Workers

1. **If you have student loans and you're choosing between Emory and a private practice, choose Emory.** The 10-year PSLF math is worth $80,000–$200,000+.
2. **Confirm Northside is NOT HCA.** It's frequently misidentified. Northside is independent nonprofit → PSLF-eligible.
3. **Submit annual PSLF Employer Certification Forms.** Don't wait 10 years — verify every year that your payments count.
4. **Use IDR + PSLF together.** Apply for the SAVE plan (or its successor) and confirm your payments count toward PSLF.
5. **Avoid Encompass Health Rehab and Select Specialty.** These publicly traded rehab/LTACH operators have multiple Atlanta-area facilities. NOT PSLF-eligible.
